# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for January 2, 2009

```
[[Page 24808]]




                     SENATE--Friday, January 2, 2009

            (Legislative day of Wednesday, December 10, 2008)
  The Senate met at 10 and 2 seconds a.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Jeff Bingaman, a 
Senator from the State of New Mexico.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                  Washington, DC, January 2, 2009.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, section 3, of the Standing 
     Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Jeff 
     Bingaman, a Senator from the State of New Mexico, to perform 
     the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. BINGAMAN thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



                          ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned sine die under the provisions of H. Con. Res. 
440.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 33 seconds a.m., adjourned sine die.
```
