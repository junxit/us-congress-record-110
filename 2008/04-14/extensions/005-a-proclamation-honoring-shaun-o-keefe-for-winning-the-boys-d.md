# A PROCLAMATION HONORING SHAUN O'KEEFE FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION I STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING SHAUN O'KEEFE FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION I 
                     STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 14, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Shaun O'Keefe showed hard work and dedication to the sport 
of basketball; and
  Whereas, Shaun O'Keefe was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Shaun O'Keefe always displayed sportsmanship on and off of 
the court; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Shaun O'Keefe on 
winning the Boys' Division I State Basketball Championship. We 
recognize the tremendous hard work and sportsmanship he has 
demonstrated during the 2007-2008 basketball season.

                          ____________________
```
