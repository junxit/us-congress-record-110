# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5719, TAXPAYER ASSISTANCE AND SIMPLIFICATION ACT OF 2008

**Measures:** H.Res. 1102, H.R. 5719

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5719, TAXPAYER 
               ASSISTANCE AND SIMPLIFICATION ACT OF 2008

  Ms. CASTOR, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-585) on the resolution (H. Res. 1102) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5719) to amend the Internal Revenue 
Code of 1986 to conform return preparer penalty standards, delay 
implementation of withholding taxes on government contractors, enhance 
taxpayer protections, assist low-income taxpayers, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
