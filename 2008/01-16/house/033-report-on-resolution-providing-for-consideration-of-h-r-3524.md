# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3524, HOPE VI IMPROVEMENT AND REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2007

**Measures:** H.Res. 922, H.R. 3524

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3524, HOPE VI 
              IMPROVEMENT AND REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2007

report (Rept. No. 110-509) on the resolution (H. Res. 922) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3524) to reauthorize the HOPE VI
```
