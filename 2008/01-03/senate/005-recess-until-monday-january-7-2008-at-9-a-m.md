# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, JANUARY 7, 2008, AT 9 A.M.

```
            RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, JANUARY 7, 2008, AT 9 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Monday, January 7, 2008, at 9 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:05 and 12 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Monday, January 7, 2008, at 9 a.m.
```
