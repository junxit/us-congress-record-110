# Senate

```
[[Page S1]]

Senate

  The 3d day of January being the day prescribed by the Constitution of 
the United States for the annual meeting of the Congress, the 2d 
session of the 110th Congress commenced this day at 12:04 and 26 
seconds p.m.
  The Senate was called to order by the Presiding Officer [Ms. 
Landrieu], a Senator from the State of Louisiana.
```
