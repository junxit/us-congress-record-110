# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
WORKERS' RETIREMENT SECURITY--IMPACT OF FINANCIAL CRISIS
Committee on Education and Labor: Held a hearing on the Impact of the 
Financial Crisis on Workers' Retirement Security. Testimony was heard 
from Peter Orszag, Director, CBO; and public witnesses.
AIG BAILOUT--CAUSES AND EFFECTS
Committee on Oversight and Government Reform: Held a hearing on the 
Causes and Effects of the AIG Bailout. Testimony was heard from Lynn E. 
Turner, former Chief Accountant, SEC; Eric R. Dinallo, Superintendent, 
Insurance Department, New York State; the following former Chief 
Executive Officers of AIG: Robert B. Willumstad; and Martin J. 
Sullivan.
```
