# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, OCTOBER 10, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, OCTOBER 10, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 11 a.m., Friday, October 10, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 36 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Friday, October 10, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
