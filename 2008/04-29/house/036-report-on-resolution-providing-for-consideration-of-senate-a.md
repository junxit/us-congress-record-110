# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 493, GENETIC INFORMATION NONDISCRIMINATION ACT OF 2008

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENT TO 
      H.R. 493, GENETIC INFORMATION NONDISCRIMINATION ACT OF 2008

  Ms. SLAUGHTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-612) on the resolution (H. Res. 1156) providing 
for consideration of the Senate amendment to the bill (H.R. 493) to 
prohibit discrimination on the basis of genetic information with 
respect to health insurance and employment, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
