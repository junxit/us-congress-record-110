# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5522, COMBUSTIBLE DUST EXPLOSION AND FIRE PREVENTION ACT OF 2008

**Measures:** H.Res. 1157, H.R. 5522

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5522, 
       COMBUSTIBLE DUST EXPLOSION AND FIRE PREVENTION ACT OF 2008

  Ms. SLAUGHTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-613) on the resolution (H. Res. 1157) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5522) to require the Secretary of 
Labor to issue interim and final occupational safety and health 
standards regarding worker exposure to combustible dust, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
