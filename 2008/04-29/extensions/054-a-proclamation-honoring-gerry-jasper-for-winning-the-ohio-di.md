# A PROCLAMATION HONORING GERRY JASPER FOR WINNING THE OHIO DIVISION IV STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

```
 A PROCLAMATION HONORING GERRY JASPER FOR WINNING THE OHIO DIVISION IV 
                     STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 29, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Gerry Jasper showed hard work and dedication to the sport of 
basketball; and
  Whereas, Gerry Jasper was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Gerry Jasper always displayed sportsmanship on and off of 
the court; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Gerry Jasper on winning 
the Ohio Division IV State Basketball Championship. We recognize the 
tremendous hard work and sportsmanship he has demonstrated during the 
2007-2008 basketball season.

                          ____________________
```
