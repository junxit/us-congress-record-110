# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 30, 2008

**Speaking:** Menendez, Robert (M000639) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2881

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 30, 2008

  Mr. MENENDEZ. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m. tomorrow, 
Wednesday, April 30; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for use later in the day, and 
the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 2881, the FAA 
reauthorization bill, with Senator Durbin recognized to offer an 
amendment; that at 10:40 a.m., the Senate recess until 12 noon for the 
joint meeting of Congress.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
