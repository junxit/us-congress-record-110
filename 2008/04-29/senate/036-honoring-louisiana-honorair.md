# HONORING LOUISIANA HONORAIR

```
                      HONORING LOUISIANA HONORAIR

● Mr. VITTER. Mr. President, today I acknowledge and honor a 
very special group, the Louisiana HonorAir. Louisiana HonorAir is a 
not-for-profit group that flies as many as 200 World War II veterans a 
year up to Washington, DC, free of charge. On May 3, 2008, a group of 
95 veterans will reach Washington as part of this very special program.
  I want to take a moment to thank all the brave veterans visiting our
```
