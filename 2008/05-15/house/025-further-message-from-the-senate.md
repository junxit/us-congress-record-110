                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate agrees to the report of the committee of 
conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment 
of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 2419) ``An Act to provide for the 
continuation of agricultural programs through fiscal year 2012, and for 
other purposes.''.

Senate amendment with amendment No. 3, if ordered; and the motion to 
suspend the rules with regard to H.R. 2894, if ordered.
