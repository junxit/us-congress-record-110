# DEMOCRATIC WIZARD BEHIND THE CURTAIN WAS REVEALED TODAY

```
        DEMOCRATIC WIZARD BEHIND THE CURTAIN WAS REVEALED TODAY

  (Mr. DANIEL E. LUNGREN of California asked and was given permission 
to address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his 
remarks.)
  Mr. DANIEL E. LUNGREN of California. Mr. Speaker, the Democratic 
wizard behind the curtain was revealed today.
  Pulling open the curtain, they brought forward their proposal as to 
dealing with our troops. After being kept out of consideration of that 
which was being brought to the floor, Republicans decided to leave the 
Democrats to their own devices.
  Left to their own devices, what do we have? No money for our troops, 
a cut-and-run policy, and extraneous funding being put on the bill. 
That's the Democratic approach to our war and fighting those who would 
kill us in places overseas. No funds for the troops, a cut-and-run 
policy, and extraneous funding placed on it.
  The wizard has been seen.

                          ____________________
```
