# HONORING MEECO, INC.

```
                          HONORING MEECO, INC.

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. PATRICK J. MURPHY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 29, 2008

  Mr. PATRICK J. MURPHY of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, today I am 
proud to recognize MEECO on their 60th anniversary. MEECO, Incorporated 
is a privately-held, family-owned manufacturing business headquartered 
in Warrington, Bucks County, PA. They produce and sell quality gas 
analysis measurement tools to industries across the globe allowing them 
to efficiently and safely manufacture their products.
  MEECO has played an important role as a small business in our Bucks 
County community and the world. They employ more than 3 dozen 
hardworking people from the Bucks County area and have succeeded 
through an approach that focuses on product innovation, global 
expansion, and progressive management and manufacturing practices.
  MEECO shows no signs of slowing down after winning 2 R&D 100 Awards 
for its products from R&D Magazine, developing a highly successful, 
award-winning spin-off company called Tiger Optics and recently 
celebrated the strongest financial first half in the company's history.
  Madam Speaker, please join me in congratulating MEECO, Incorporated 
for their success and their high business standards. I thank them for 
the work they do on behalf of our community and across the world.

                          ____________________
```
