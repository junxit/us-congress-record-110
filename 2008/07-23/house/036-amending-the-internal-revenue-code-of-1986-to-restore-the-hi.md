  So (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the rules were suspended and

  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
  Stated for:
  Mr. LARSON of Connecticut. Mr. Speaker, I regret that my vote today 
on rollcall vote No. 518, on final passage of H.R. 6532, a bill to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to restore the Highway Trust 
Fund balance, was not recorded. I was present on the floor and voting 
during this voting series, but did not realize that the electronic 
voting system did not register my support for this specific bill. As a 
cosponsor of this important legislation my vote should have been 
recorded as ``yea'' on rollcall vote No. 518.
  Ms. GRANGER. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall No. 518, I would like to 
clarify that my intent was to vote ``yea.''
