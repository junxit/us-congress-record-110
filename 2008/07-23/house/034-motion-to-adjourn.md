  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion to adjourn.
  The question was taken; and the Speaker pro tempore announced that 
the noes appeared to have it.
  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, on that I demand the yeas and nays.
  The yeas and nays were ordered.

minute vote on the motion to adjourn will be followed by a 15-minute 
vote on ordering the previous question on H. Res. 1363 and 5-minute 
votes on:
  Adopting H. Res. 1363, if ordered, and
  Suspending the rules and passing H.R. 6532.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 20, 
nays 400, not voting 14, as follows:
