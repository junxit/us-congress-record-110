# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for March 21, 2008

```
[[Page 4452]]


                     SENATE--Friday, March 21, 2008

  (Legislative day of Thursday, March 13, 2008)
  The Senate met at 10:59 and 59 seconds a.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Jack Reed, a Senator 
from the State of Rhode Island.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The bill clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                   Washington, DC, March 21, 2008.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Jack Reed, a Senator from the State of Rhode Island, to 
     perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. REED thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro tempore.

                          ____________________



              RECESS UNTIL 10 A.M., MONDAY, MARCH 24, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Monday, March 24, 2008, at 10 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11:00 and 27 seconds a.m. recessed until 
Monday, March 24, 2008, at 10 a.m.
```
