# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 6331

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Ms. Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly 
enrolled a bill of the House of the following title, which was 
thereupon signed by the Speaker:

  H.R. 6331. An act to amend titles XVII and XIX of the Social Security 
Act to extend expiring provisions under the Medicare Program, to 
improve beneficiary access to preventive and mental health services to 
enhance low-income benefit programs, and to maintain access to care in 
rural areas, including pharmacy access, and for other purposes.
```
