# 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF WOLSEY, SOUTH DAKOTA

```
               125TH ANNIVERSARY OF WOLSEY, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Wolsey, SD. The 
town of Wolsey commemorated its 125th anniversary of its founding with 
celebrations June 27-29, 2008.
  Located in Beadle County, Wolsey was founded in 1883. Since its 
beginning 125 years ago, the community of Wolsey has continued to serve 
as a strong example of South Dakota values and traditions.
  I would like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Wolsey on 
this milestone anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the 
years to come.●
```
