# 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF ETHAN, SOUTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                125TH ANNIVERSARY OF ETHAN, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Ethan, SD. The 
town of Ethan commemorated its 125th anniversary of its founding with 
celebrations June 13-15, 2008.
  Located in Davison County, Ethan was founded in 1883 and was named 
after Revolutionary War patriot Ethan Allen. Since its beginning 125 
years ago, the community of Ethan has continued to serve as a strong 
example of South Dakota values and traditions.
  I would like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Ethan on 
this milestone anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the 
years to come.●
```
