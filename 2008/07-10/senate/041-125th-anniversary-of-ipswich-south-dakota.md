# 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF IPSWICH, SOUTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
               125TH ANNIVERSARY OF IPSWICH, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Ipswich, SD. The 
town of Ipswich commemorated its 125th anniversary of its founding with 
celebrations June 13-15, 2008.
  Located in Edmunds County, Ipswich was founded in 1883 and was named 
after a city of the same name in Britain. Since its beginning 125 years 
ago, the community of Ipswich has continued to serve as a strong 
example of South Dakota values and traditions.
  I would like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Ipswich 
on this milestone anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the 
years to come.●
```
