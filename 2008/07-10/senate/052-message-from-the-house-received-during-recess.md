# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

**Measures:** H.R. 6304

```
             MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

                                 ______
                                 

                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 2007, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on July 9, 2008, during the recess of the 
Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives announcing 
that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 6304. An act to amend the Foreign Intelligence 
     Surveillance Act of 1978 to establish a procedure for 
     authorizing certain acquisitions of foreign intelligence, and 
     for other purposes.

  Under authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 2007, the 
enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the Vice President during the 
recess of the Senate, on July 9, 2008.

                          ____________________
```
