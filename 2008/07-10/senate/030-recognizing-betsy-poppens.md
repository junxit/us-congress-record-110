# RECOGNIZING BETSY POPPENS

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       RECOGNIZING BETSY POPPENS

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Betsy Poppens, an 
intern in my Sioux Falls, SD, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Betsy is a graduate of Marion High School in Marion, SD. Currently 
she is attending Northwestern College, where she is majoring in public 
relations. She is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the 
most out of her internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Betsy 
for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
