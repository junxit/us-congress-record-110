# 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF MINA, SOUTH DAKOTA

```
                125TH ANNIVERSARY OF MINA, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Mina, SD. The town 
of Mina commemorated its 125th anniversary of its founding with 
celebrations on July 3, 2008.
  Located in Edmunds County, Mina was founded in 1883. Since its 
beginning 125 years ago, the community of Mina has continued to serve 
as a strong example of South Dakota values and traditions.
  I would like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Mina on 
this milestone anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the 
years to come.●
```
