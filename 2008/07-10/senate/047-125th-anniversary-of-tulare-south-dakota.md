# 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF TULARE, SOUTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
               125TH ANNIVERSARY OF TULARE, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Tulare, South 
Dakota. The town of Tulare commemorated its 125th anniversary of its 
founding with celebrations June 20-22, 2008.
  Located in Spink County, Tulare was founded in 1883. Since its 
beginning 125 years ago, the community of Tulare has continued to serve 
as a strong example of South Dakota values and traditions.
  I would like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Tulare on 
this milestone anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the 
years to come.●
```
