# 125TH ANNIVERSARY OF ARTESIAN, SOUTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
              125TH ANNIVERSARY OF ARTESIAN, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Artesian, SD. The 
town of Artesian commemorated its 125th anniversary of its founding 
with celebrations July 4-6, 2008.
  Located in Sanborn County, Artesian was founded in 1883 and was named 
after the abundance of flowing wells in the area, known as artesian 
wells.
  Since its beginning 125 years ago, the community of Artesian has 
continued to serve as a strong example of South Dakota values and 
traditions.
  I would like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Artesian 
on this milestone anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the 
years to come.●
```
