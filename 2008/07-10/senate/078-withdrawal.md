# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on July 
10, 2008 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       MARK MCKINNON, OF TEXAS, TO BE A MEMBER OF THE BROADCASTING 
     BOARD OF GOVERNORS FOR A TERM EXPIRING AUGUST 13, 2009, VICE 
     FAYZA VERONIQUE BOULAD RODMAN, TO WHICH POSITION HE WAS 
     APPOINTED DURING THE LAST RECESS OF THE SENATE, WHICH WAS 
     SENT TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 9, 2007.
```
