# GAS PRICES

```
                               GAS PRICES

  Mr. PRYOR. Mr. President, I wish to follow up on something the two 
leaders were talking about a few minutes ago, and that is gas prices 
are a very serious issue in this country, certainly in my State. 
Everywhere I go in Arkansas, people ask me about it. I encourage all of 
our colleagues, Democrats and Republicans, House and Senate, let's all 
work together and try to do something. Certainly it is going to take a 
Herculean effort to get President Bush to change his policy on gas 
prices, but I hope we could all work together, find some consensus, and 
move forward on some meaningful legislation to try to bring relief to 
people today on gas prices.

                          ____________________
```
