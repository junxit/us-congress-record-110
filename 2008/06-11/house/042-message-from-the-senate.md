# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 634, H.R. 3179

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Byrd, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment in which the concurrence 
of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the following title:

       H.R. 3179. An act to amend title 40, United States Code, to 
     authorize the use of Federal supply schedules for the 
     acquisition of law enforcement, security, and certain other 
     related items by State and local governments.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed with an 
amendment a bill of the House of the following title:

       H.R. 634. An act to require the Secretary of the Treasury 
     to mint coins in commemoration of veterans who became 
     disabled for life while serving in the Armed Forces of the 
     United States.

                          ____________________
```
