# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following my remarks and those of the 
Republican leader, there will be a period of morning business for an 
hour. Senators will be allowed to speak for 10 minutes each, with the 
time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees. The Republicans will control the first half, the majority 
controls the final half. Following morning business, the Senate will 
resume consideration of S. Con. Res. 70, the concurrent resolution on 
the budget, for debate only until the 12:30 recess for the caucus 
luncheons.
```
