# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 12, 2008

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 12, 2008

  Mr. BROWN. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m., Wednesday, March 
12; that on Wednesday, following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day 
and that the Senate then resume consideration of H. Con. Res. 70, the 
concurrent resolution on the budget.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
