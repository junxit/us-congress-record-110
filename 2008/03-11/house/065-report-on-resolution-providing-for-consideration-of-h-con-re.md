# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 312, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2009

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 312, H.Res. 1036

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 312, 
        CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2009

  Ms. SUTTON, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-548) on the resolution (H. Res. 1036) providing 
for consideration of the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 312) 
revising the congressional budget for the United States Government for 
fiscal year 2008, establishing the congressional budget for the United 
States Government for fiscal year 2009, and setting forth appropriate 
budgetary levels for fiscal years 2010 through 2013, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
