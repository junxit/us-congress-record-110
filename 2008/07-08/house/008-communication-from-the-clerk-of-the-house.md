# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 377, H.R. 528, S. 3015, S. 3082, H.R. 3564, H.R. 3721, H.R. 4185, H.R. 5168, H.R. 5395, H.R. 5479, H.R. 5517

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                                    June 27, 2008.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, The Capitol, House of Representatives, Washington, 
         DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, the Clerk received the following message 
     from the Secretary of the Senate on June 27, 2008, at 3:51 
     p.m.:
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 3721.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 4185.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 5168.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 5395.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 5479.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 5517.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 528.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 3564.
       That the Senate passed S. 3015.
       That the Senate passed S. 3082.
       That the Senate agreed to H. Con. Res. 377.
       With best wishes, I am,
           Sincerely,
                                               Lorraine C. Miller,
                                               Clerk of the House.
                                               Deborah M. Spriggs,
     Deputy Clerk.

                          ____________________
```
