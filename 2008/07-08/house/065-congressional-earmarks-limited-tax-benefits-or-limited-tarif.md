# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 1286

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

       The amendment to be offered by Representative Bishop of 
     Utah, or a designee, to H.R. 1286, the Washington Rochambeau 
     Revolutionary Route National Historic Trail Designation Act, 
     does not contain any congressional earmarks, limited tax 
     benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 
     9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of rule XXI.
```
