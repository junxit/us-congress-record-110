# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5811, ELECTRONIC MESSAGE PRESERVATION ACT

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5811, 
                  ELECTRONIC MESSAGE PRESERVATION ACT

  Mr. Hastings of Florida, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 110-745) on the resolution (H. Res. 1318) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5811) to amend title 44, 
United States Code, to require preservation of certain electronic 
records by Federal agencies, to require a certification and reports 
relating to Presidential records, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
