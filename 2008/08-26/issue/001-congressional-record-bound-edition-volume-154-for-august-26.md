# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for August 26, 2008

```
[[Page 17960]]

                    SENATE--Tuesday, August 26, 2008

               (Legislative day of Friday, August 1, 2008)
  The Senate met at 2 and 8 seconds p.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the President pro tempore (Mr. 
Byrd).

                          ____________________



              RECESS UNTIL 2 P.M., FRIDAY, AUGUST 29, 2008

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate 
stands in recess until 2 p.m., Friday, August, 29, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 2 and 33 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Friday, August 29, 2008, at 2 p.m.
```
