# RECESS UNTIL 2 P.M., FRIDAY, AUGUST 29, 2008

```
              RECESS UNTIL 2 P.M., FRIDAY, AUGUST 29, 2008

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate 
stands in recess until 2 p.m., Friday, August, 29, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 2 and 33 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Friday, August 29, 2008, at 2 p.m.
```
