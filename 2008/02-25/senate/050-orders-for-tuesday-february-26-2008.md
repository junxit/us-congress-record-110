# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 26, 2008

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 26, 2008

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
tomorrow, Tuesday, February 26; that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be agreed to, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 1200, the 
Indian Health Care Improvement Act, as under the previous order. 
Further, I ask that the Senate stand in recess from 12:30 p.m. until 
2:30 p.m. to allow for the weekly caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
