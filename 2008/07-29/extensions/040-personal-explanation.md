# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                              HON. TED POE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 29, 2008

  Mr. POE. Madam Speaker, on H. Res. 1311--National Gear Up Day, H. 
Res. 1202--National Guard Youth Challenge Day, H.R. 6493--Aviation 
Safety Enhancement Act of 2008, I was not able to return to the House 
because myself and 6 other Members of Congress from Texas had to make 
an emergency plane landing due to mechanical problems on CO flight 458. 
Thus this emergency prevented a timely return to Washington, DC.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on all three of these 
measures.
```
