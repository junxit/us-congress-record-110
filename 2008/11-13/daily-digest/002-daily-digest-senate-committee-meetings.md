Center for Responsible Lending, Durham, North Carolina; Barry L. 
Zubrow, JPMorgan Chase and Company, Far Hills, New Jersey; Gregory K. 
Palm, The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., New York, New York; Susan M. 
Wachter, University of Pennsylvania, The Wharton School, Philadelphia; 
Anne Finucane, Bank of America, Lincoln, Massachusetts; Jon Campbell, 
Wells Fargo and Company, Minneapolis, Minnesota; and Nancy Zirkin, 
Leadership Conference on Civil Rights, Washington, D.C.
