# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, MARCH 21, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

```
            RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, MARCH 21, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 11 a.m., on Friday, March 21, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12 and 33 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Friday, March 21, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
