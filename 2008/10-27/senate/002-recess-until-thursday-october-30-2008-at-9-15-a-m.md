# RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, OCTOBER 30, 2008, AT 9:15 A.M.

```
         RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, OCTOBER 30, 2008, AT 9:15 A.M.

Senate stands in recess until 9:15 a.m., on Thursday, October 30, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9 and 31 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Thursday, October 30, 2008, at 9:15 a.m.
```
