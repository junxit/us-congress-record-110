# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for October 27, 2008

```
[[Page 24064]]


                    SENATE--Monday, October 27, 2008

           (Legislative day of Wednesday, September 17, 2008)
  The Senate met at 9 and 3 seconds a.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Sheldon Whitehouse, a 
Senator from the State of Rhode Island.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The bill clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                 Washington, DC, October 27, 2008.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Sheldon Whitehouse, a Senator from the State of Rhode Island, 
     to perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



         RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, OCTOBER 30, 2008, AT 9:15 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 9:15 a.m., on Thursday, October 30, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9 and 31 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Thursday, October 30, 2008, at 9:15 a.m.
```
