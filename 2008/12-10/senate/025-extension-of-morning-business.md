# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Lautenberg, Frank R. (L000123) [D-NJ]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
period for the transaction of morning business be extended until 6 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I be 
permitted to present my remarks. I should not go over 10 minutes, but I 
ask unanimous consent that I be permitted to do so.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
