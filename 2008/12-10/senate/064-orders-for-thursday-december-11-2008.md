# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 11, 2008

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 7005

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 11, 2008

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess until 10 a.m. 
tomorrow, Thursday, December 11; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business for up to 1 hour, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; that following 
morning business, the Senate resume consideration of the motion to 
proceed to H.R. 7005.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
