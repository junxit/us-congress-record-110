# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Harkin, Tom (H000206) [D-IA]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I now ask the Senate proceed to a period 
of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. HARKIN. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
