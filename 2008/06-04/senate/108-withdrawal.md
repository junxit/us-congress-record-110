# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
4, 2008 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       JOHN R. STEER, OF VIRGINIA, TO BE A MEMBER OF THE UNITED 
     STATES SENTENCING COMMISSION FOR A TERM EXPIRING OCTOBER 31, 
     2011 (REAPPOINTMENT), TO WHICH POSITION HE WAS APPOINTED 
     DURING THE LAST RECESS OF THE SENATE, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE 
     SENATE ON JANUARY 9, 2007.
```
