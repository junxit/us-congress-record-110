# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, today, following my remarks and the 
remarks of Senator McConnell, there will be a period of morning 
business until 11:30 a.m., with the time equally divided and 
controlled. The majority will control the first 30 minutes, and the 
Republicans will control the next 30 minutes.
  Following morning business, the Senate will proceed to the 
consideration of the budget conference report. There will be 15 minutes 
for debate equally divided prior to a vote on adoption of the 
conference report. Therefore, Senators should expect the first vote to 
begin at 11:45 a.m.
  Upon disposition of the budget conference report, I expect the Senate 
to begin consideration of the climate change bill.
```
