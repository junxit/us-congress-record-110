# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. STEVEN R. ROTHMAN

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 4, 2008

  Mr. ROTHMAN. Madam Speaker, I would like to state for the record my 
position on the following votes I missed on June 3, 2008. On Tuesday, 
June 3, 2008, I was unable to be present in the Capitol and missed 
rollcall votes Nos. 367 through 369. Had I been present, I would have 
voted in the following manner:
  On rollcall vote No. 367, on H. Con. Res. 138, a resolution 
supporting National Men's Health Week, I would have voted ``aye.''
  On rollcall vote No. 368, on H. Res. 923, a resolution recognizing 
the state of Minnesota's 150rh anniversary, I would have voted ``aye.''
  On rollcall vote No. 369, on H. Res. 1114, a resolution supporting 
the goals and ideals of the Arbor Day Foundation and National Arbor 
Day, I would have voted ``aye.''
```
