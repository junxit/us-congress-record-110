# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       252. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Board of 
     County Commissioners of Douglas County, Nebraska, relative to 
     Resolution No. 143 opposing any cutback of the National 
     Institute of Correction's budget; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
       253. Also, a petition of American Bar Association, relative 
     to a resolution regarding prosecutor obligation regarding new 
     exculpatory evidence; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       254. Also, a petition of American Bar Association, relative 
     to a resolution regarding criminal standards on prosecutorial 
     investigations; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
