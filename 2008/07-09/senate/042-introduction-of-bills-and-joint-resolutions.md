# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 3234, S. 3235, S. 3236

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Ms. MURKOWSKI (for herself and Mr. Stevens):
       S. 3234. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to provide a temporary income tax credit for commercial 
     fisherman to offset high fuel costs; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
           By Mr. VITTER:
       S. 3235. A bill to reduce the amount of financial 
     assistance provided to the Government of Mexico in response 
     to the illegal border crossings from Mexico into the United 
     States, which serve to dissipate the political discontent 
     with the higher unemployment rate within Mexico; to the 
     Committee on Foreign Relations.
           By Mr. McCONNELL (for himself, Mr. Grassley, and Mr. 
             Kyl):
       S. 3236. A bill to amend titles XVIII and XIX of the Social 
     Security Act to extend provisions under Medicare and Medicaid 
     programs, and for other purposes; read the first time.

                          ____________________
```
