# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 24, 2008

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1315

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 24, 2008

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m., 
tomorrow morning, Thursday, April 24; following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; that there then be a period of morning business for 
up to 60 minutes with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees, with the majority controlling the first 
half and the Republicans controlling the final half; that following 
morning business, the Senate resume consideration of S. 1315, the 
Veterans' Benefits Enhancement Act, as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
