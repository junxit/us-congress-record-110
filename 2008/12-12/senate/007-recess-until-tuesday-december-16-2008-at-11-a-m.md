# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, DECEMBER 16, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

```
          RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, DECEMBER 16, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 11 a.m. on Tuesday, December 16, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 54 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, December 16, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
