# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
JUSTICE FOR ALL ACT
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded an oversight hearing to 
examine the Justice for All Act (Public Law 108-405), focusing on the 
administration of the Bloodsworth and Coverdell DNA Grant Programs by 
the Department of Justice, after receiving testimony from Glenn A. 
Fine, Inspector General, and John Morgan, Deputy Director, National 
Institute of Justice, Office of Justice Programs, both of the 
Department of Justice; Peter M. Marone, Virginia Department of Forensic 
Science, Richmond, on behalf of the Consortium of Forensic Science 
Organizations; Larry A. Hammond, Osborn Maledon, Phoenix, Arizona; and 
Peter Neufeld, Cardozo School of Law Innocence Project, New York, New 
York.
```
