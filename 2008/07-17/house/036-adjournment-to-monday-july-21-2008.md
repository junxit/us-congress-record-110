# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, JULY 21, 2008

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

```
                  ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, JULY 21, 2008

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 12:30 p.m. on Monday next; and 
further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 
12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, July 22, for morning-hour debate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Maryland?
  There was no objection.
```
