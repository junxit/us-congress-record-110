# A PROCLAMATION HONORING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF SALEM LUTHERAN CHURCH

```
 A PROCLAMATION HONORING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF SALEM LUTHERAN CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 17, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
   Whereas, Salem Lutheran Church is celebrating its 175th anniversary; 
and
   Whereas, the congregation of Salem Lutheran continue to be active, 
enthusiastic members of our community; and
   Whereas, the 175th anniversary of Salem Lutheran Church has drawn 
new and old congregation members to Evans Creek, Ohio to celebrate the 
life of their church: Now, therefore, be it
   Resolved, That along with the residents of the 18th Congressional 
District, I commend Salem Lutheran Church and its congregation for 
their unwavering commitment, dedication and contributions to their 
community and country in recognition of their 175 years.

                          ____________________
```
