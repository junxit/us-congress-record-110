# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 634, H.R. 814, S. 2967

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

   (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, pp. D907-908)
  H.R. 634, to require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint coins in 
commemoration of veterans who became disabled for life while serving in 
the Armed Forces of the United States. Signed on July 17, 2008. (Public 
Law 110-277)
  H.R. 814, to require the Consumer Product Safety Commission to issue 
regulations mandating child-resistant closures on all portable gasoline 
containers. Signed on July 17, 2008. (Public Law 110-278)
  S. 2967, to provide for certain Federal employee benefits to be 
continued for certain employees of the Senate Restaurants after 
operations of the Senate Restaurants are contracted to be performed by 
a private business concern. Signed on July 17, 2008. (Public Law 110-
279)
```
