# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 3268

```
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 3268

  Mr. NELSON of Florida. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
after the Senate convenes on Tuesday, July 22, the hour prior to the 
cloture vote on the motion to proceed to S. 3268 be equally divided and 
controlled between the leaders or their designees; that upon the use or 
yielding back of that time, the Senate proceed to vote on the motion to 
invoke cloture; further, that the mandatory quorum be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
