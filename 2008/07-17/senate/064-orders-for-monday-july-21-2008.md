# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 21, 2008

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 21, 2008

  Mr. NELSON of Florida. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess until 
3 p.m., Monday, July 21; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
resume consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 3268, the energy 
speculation bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
