# MARINE GUNNERY SERGEANT JOHN D. FRY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3721

```
        MARINE GUNNERY SERGEANT JOHN D. FRY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3721) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1190 Lorena Road in Lorena, Texas, as the 
``Marine Gunnery Sgt. John D. Fry Post Office Building'', was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
