# WILLIAM "BILL" CLAY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5395

```
               WILLIAM ``BILL'' CLAY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5395) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 11001 Dunklin Drive in St. Louis, Missouri, 
as the ``William `Bill' Clay Post Office Building'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
