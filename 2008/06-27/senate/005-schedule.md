# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks the Senate will 
proceed to a period of morning business, with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  There will be no rollcall votes today. The next vote will occur at 
5:30 p.m., Monday, July 7. That vote will be on the motion to invoke 
cloture on the motion to concur with respect to the housing reform 
legislation.
  Last night, we were able to reach agreement to complete action on the 
FISA legislation. That will be when we return from the recess. So there 
will be three amendments in order to the legislation, with limited 
debate time on each. Senators should be prepared to debate and vote on 
the FISA legislation, which we will complete on Tuesday, July 8.
```
