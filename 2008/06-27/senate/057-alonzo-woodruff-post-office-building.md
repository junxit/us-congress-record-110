# ALONZO WOODRUFF POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5479

```
                  ALONZO WOODRUFF POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5479) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 117 North Kidd Street in Ionia, Michigan, as 
the ``Alonzo Woodruff Post Office Building'', was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
