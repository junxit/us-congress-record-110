# ROCKY MARCIANO POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  ROCKY MARCIANO POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5528) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 120 Commercial Street in Brockton, 
Massachusetts, as the ``Rocky Marciano Post Office Building'', was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
