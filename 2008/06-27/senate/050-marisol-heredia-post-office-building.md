# MARISOL HEREDIA POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  MARISOL HEREDIA POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4185) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 11151 Valley Boulevard in El Monte, 
California, as the ``Marisol Heredia Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
