# CODY GRATER POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5168

```
                    CODY GRATER POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5168) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 19101 Cortez Boulevard in Brooksville, 
Florida, as the ``Cody Grater Post Office Building'', was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
