     Special Forces . . .
     And 107th Cav, those great men of honor who freedom so

     With great acts of daring and might, with all of their most

     Where do you go?
     When your face and body, have been blown and shattered here 
           so?
     How do you rebuild with no back left still? For it's all

     Day by Day, Hour by Hour . . . as all have so found out what

     To find the courage on battlefields of honor, in operation 
           rooms he towers! All in courage's finest hour!

     Eggleston, a doctor's son . . .
     A sure fine one, as is this America's Son . . . Terminating 
           evil this one!
     A Virginian who so shines, for all of us so in time . . . as

           Sun!

     For all of those who go off to war . . .
     Are but the greatest of all Americans so sure, who for us all

     For all that we have, all in this land of the free . . . is

     And leave their loved ones behind in tears, with sleepless

     In life, all is possible in what you feel . . .
     All in what you so stand for, your ideals . . . deep down 
           inside ones heart so real . . .
     That let's you go off to war, to face heartache and death . . 
           . and evil to endure and rebuild, and not yield!

     Like T4 . . .
     Bodies can grow and rebuild, with but hard work, courage and

     But, without a heart . . . one can not so live and endure . . 
           . only with, these special forces within like T4!
