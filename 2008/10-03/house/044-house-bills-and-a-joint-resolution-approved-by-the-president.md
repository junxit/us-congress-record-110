# HOUSE BILLS AND A JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

```
      HOUSE BILLS AND A JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that on the following 
dates, he had approved and signed bills and a joint resolution of the 
following titles:
```
