# SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 231, S. 2607, S. 3145, S. 3218

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  The SPEAKER announced her signature to enrolled bills of the Senate 
of the following titles:

       S. 231. To authorize the Edward Byrne Memorial Justice 
     Assistance Grant Program at fiscal year 2006 levels through 
     2012.
       S. 2607. An act to make a technical correction to section 
     3009 of the Deficit Reduction Act of 2005.
       S. 3145. To designate a portion of United States Route 20A, 
     located in Orchard Park, New York, as the ``Timothy J. 
     Russert Highway''.
       S. 3218. To extend the pilot program for volunteer groups 
     to obtain criminal history background checks.
```
