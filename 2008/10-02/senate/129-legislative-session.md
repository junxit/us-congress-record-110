# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 3001

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

                                 ______
                                 

                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 3001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that if the Senate 
receives from the House a correcting resolution to correct the 
enrollment of S. 3001 that is identical to the matter which is 
currently at the desk, then it be considered to have been agreed to and 
the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table; that if the House 
concurrent resolution is not identical, then this order be vitiated.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
