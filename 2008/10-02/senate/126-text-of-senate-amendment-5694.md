# Text of Senate Amendment 5694

**Speaking:** Nelson, Ben (N000180) [D-NE]

```
  SA 5694. Mr. NELSON of Nebraska (for Mrs. Lincoln) proposed an 
amendment to the resolution S. Res. 616, reducing maternal mortality 
both at home and abroad; as follows:

       On page 3, line 4, strike ``greater'' and insert ``more 
     effective''.
       On page 3, lines 6 and 7, strike ``maternal health as a 
     human right'' and insert ``that the right to access quality 
     and affordable health care is essential to improving maternal 
     health''.

                                 ______
```
