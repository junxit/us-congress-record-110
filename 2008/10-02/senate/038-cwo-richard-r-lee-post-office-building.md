# CWO RICHARD R. LEE POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 6834

```
                CWO RICHARD R. LEE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6834) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 4 South Main Street in Wallingford, 
Connecticut, as the ``CWO Richard R. Lee Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
