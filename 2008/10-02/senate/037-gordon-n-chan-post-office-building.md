# GORDON N. CHAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 6558

```
                  GORDON N. CHAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6558) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1750 Lundy Avenue in San Jose, California, as 
the ``Gordon N. Chan Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
