# MINNIE COX POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                    MINNIE COX POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4010) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 100 West Percy Street in Indianola, 
Mississippi, as the ``Minnie Cox Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
