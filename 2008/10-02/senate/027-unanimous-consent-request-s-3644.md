# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--S. 3644

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Allard, Wayne (A000109) [R-CO]

**Measures:** S. 3644

```
                   UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--S. 3644

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent, on behalf of 
Senator Landrieu and others, that the Agriculture Committee be 
discharged from further consideration of S. 3644, a bill to provide 
crop disaster assistance, and the Senate proceed to its consideration, 
that the bill be read three times and passed, the motion to reconsider 
be laid on the table, there be no intervening action or debate, and 
that any statements relating to this matter be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, I object. I understand Senator Coburn has 
a hold.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
