# Text of Senate Amendment 5692

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

```
  SA 5692. Mr. LEVIN (for Mr. Reid) proposed an amendment to the 
concurrent resolution H. Con. Res. 440, providing for an adjournment or 
recess of the two Houses; as follows:

       On page 1, line 3, strike ``from Monday, September 29, 
     2008, through Friday, October 3, 2008,''
       On page 2, line 2, strike ``that'' and all that follows 
     through line 9 and insert:
       ``the Senate may adjourn or recess at any time from 
     Thursday, October 2, 2008, through January 3, 2009, on a 
     motion offered pursuant to this concurrent resolution by its 
     Majority Leader or his designee until such time as specified 
     in that motion, but not beyond noon on January 3, 2009, and 
     it may reassemble pursuant to section 2 of this concurrent 
     resolution.''
       On page 2, line 15, strike ``time'' and insert ``respective 
     time''.
                                 ______
```
