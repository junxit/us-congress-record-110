# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--S. 3150

```
                   UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--S. 3150

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Commerce 
Committee be discharged from further consideration of S. 3150, the 
Access to Air Travel Act, that the bill be read a third time, passed, 
the motion to reconsider be laid on the table with no intervening 
action or debate, and that any statements relating to this matter 
appear at the appropriate place in the Record as if given.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Nelson of Nebraska). Is there objection?
  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, I object. There is objection on the 
Republican side.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
