# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 1424

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, the Senate will be in pro forma sessions 
until November 17, unless the House of Representatives fails to pass or 
amends H.R. 1424, the economic stabilization legislation. If the Senate 
is required to act further, the Senate could resume legislative session 
on Monday, October 6.
```
