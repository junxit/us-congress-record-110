# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA], Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mrs. BOXER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the period 
for morning business be extended until 2 p.m., with Senators permitted 
to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. McCaskill). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mrs. BOXER. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
