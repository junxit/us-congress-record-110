# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Mikulski, Barbara A. (M000702) [D-MD]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Ms. MIKULSKI. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
period for the transaction of morning business be extended until 5 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Ms. MIKULSKI. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
```
