# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


        Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs be authorized to meet 
during the session of the Senate on Thursday, December 11, 2008, at 10 
a.m. to conduct a hearing entitled ``World at Risk: A Report from the 
Commission on the Prevention of Weapons of Mass Destruction 
Proliferation and Terrorism.''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
