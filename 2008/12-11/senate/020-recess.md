# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate stands in 
recess until 3 o'clock.
  Thereupon, at 2:17 p.m., the Senate recessed until 3:03 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Ms. 
Klobuchar).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
Minnesota, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Ms. MIKULSKI. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
