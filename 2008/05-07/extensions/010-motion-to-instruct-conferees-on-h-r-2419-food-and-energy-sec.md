# MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 2419, FOOD AND ENERGY SECURITY ACT OF 2007

**Speaking:** Langevin, James R. (L000559) [D-RI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2419

```
MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 2419, FOOD AND ENERGY SECURITY ACT 
                                OF 2007

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                         HON. JAMES R. LANGEVIN

                            of rhode island

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 6, 2008

  Mr. LANGEVIN. Madam Speaker, on May 6, 2008, my vote on the Kind 
Motion to Instruct Conferees on H.R. 2419, the Food and Energy Security 
Act of 2007, rollcall vote 258, was recorded as a ``no'' vote when I 
intended to cast a ``yes'' vote. I wish to clarify on the record my 
support for the House-passed funding levels for the Grassland Reserve 
Program, the Environmental Quality Incentives Program, and Wetlands 
Reserve Program, as well as the Senate's sod saver provision.
```
