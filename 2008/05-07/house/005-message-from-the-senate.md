# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 5493

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed with amendment in which the concurrence of 
the House is requested, a bill of the House of the following title:

       H.R. 5493. An act to provide that the usual day for paying 
     salaries in or under the House of Representatives may be 
     established by regulations of the Committee on House 
     Administration.
```
