# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION ON THE PREVENTION OF WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION PROLIFERATION AND TERRORISM

```
 APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION ON THE PREVENTION OF WEAPONS OF 
              MASS DESTRUCTION PROLIFERATION AND TERRORISM

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Serrano). Pursuant to section 1853(a) of 
the Implementing Recommendations of the 9/11 Commission Act of 2007 
(P.L. 110-53), and the order of the House of January 4, 2007, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following members on the 
part of the House to the Commission on the Prevention of Weapons of 
Mass Destruction Proliferation and Terrorism:
  Mr. Timothy J. Roemer, Great Falls, Virginia
```
