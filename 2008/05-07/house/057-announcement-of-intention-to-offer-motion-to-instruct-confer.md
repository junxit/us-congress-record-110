# ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 2419, FOOD AND ENERGY SECURITY ACT OF 2007

**Speaking:** Upton, Fred (U000031) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2419

```
ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 
               2419, FOOD AND ENERGY SECURITY ACT OF 2007

  Mr. UPTON. Mr. Speaker, under rule XXII, clause 7(c), I hereby 
announce my intention to offer a motion to instruct on H.R. 2419.
  The form of the motion is as follows:


[[Page H3154]]


       Mr. Upton moves that the managers on the part of the House 
     at the conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses 
     on the Senate amendment to the bill H.R. 2419 (an Act to 
     provide for the continuation of agricultural programs through 
     fiscal year 2012) be instructed to recede to the provisions 
     proposed to be added to Section 9001 of the Farm Security and 
     Rural Investment Act of 2002 in the form of a definition of 
     ``Renewable Biomass.''

                          ____________________
```
