# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 8, 2008

**Speaking:** Dodd, Christopher J. (D000388) [D-CT]

**Measures:** S. 2284

```
                    ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 8, 2008

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Thursday, May 8; that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day; that there be a period of morning business 
for up to 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10

[[Page S3932]]

minutes each and the time equally divided and controlled between the 
two leaders or their designees, with the Republicans controlling the 
first half and the majority controlling the final half; that following 
morning business, the Senate resume consideration of S. 2284, flood 
insurance, as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
