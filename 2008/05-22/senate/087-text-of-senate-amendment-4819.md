     section 202.

       On page 4, line 12, strike ``SEC. 105.'' and insert ``SEC. 
     104.''.
       On page 6, line 10, strike ``SEC. 106.'' and insert ``SEC. 
     105.''.
       On page 6, line 24, strike ``SEC. 107.'' and insert ``SEC. 
     106.''.
       On page 8, beginning with line 6, strike through the end of 
     the bill.
