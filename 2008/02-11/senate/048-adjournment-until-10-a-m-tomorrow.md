# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Measures:** S.Res. 446

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate stands in adjournment until 10 a.m. 
tomorrow, February 12, pursuant to S. Res. 446, and does so as a mark 
of further respect to the memory of Tom Lantos, late a Representative 
from the State of California.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10:09 p.m., adjourned until Tuesday, 
February 12, 2008, at 10 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
