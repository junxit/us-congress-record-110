# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 12, 2008

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 12, 2008

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
Tuesday, February 12; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and that the Senate then resume consideration of S. 2248, the 
Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act, as under the previous order; and 
that the Senate recess from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly 
caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
