# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 21, 2008

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 21, 2008

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m., Monday, 
April 21; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and there then be a period of morning business with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each. I further ask that the 
Record remain open until 12 noon today, for the purpose of introducing 
bills, resolutions, cosponsors, and statements.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
