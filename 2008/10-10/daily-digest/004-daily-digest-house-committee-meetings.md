# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
BRIEFING--RECONCILIATION IN IRAQ AND THE HELSINKI II PROCESS
Committee on Foreign Affairs: On October 8, the Subcommittee on 
International Organizations, Human Rights, and Oversight held a 
briefing on Political Reconciliation in Iraq and the Helsinki Process: 
Lessons from South Africa and Northern Ireland. The Committee was 
briefed by the following members of the British Parliament: Lord John 
Alerdice, member House of Lords; and Jeffrey M. Donaldson, member House 
of Commons; and public witnesses.
```
