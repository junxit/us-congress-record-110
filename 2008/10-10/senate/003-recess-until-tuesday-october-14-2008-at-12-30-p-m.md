# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 14, 2008, AT 12:30 P.M.

```
         RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 14, 2008, AT 12:30 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Tuesday, October 14, 2008, at 12:30 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 14 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, October 14, 2008, at 12:30 p.m.
```
