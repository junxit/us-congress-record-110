# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for October 10, 2008

```
[[Page 24059]]


                    SENATE--Friday, October 10, 2008

           (Legislative day of Wednesday, September 17, 2008)
  The Senate met at 11 and 4 seconds a.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable John D. Rockefeller, 
IV, a Senator from the State of West Virginia.

                          ____________________



         RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 14, 2008, AT 12:30 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Tuesday, October 14, 2008, at 12:30 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 14 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, October 14, 2008, at 12:30 p.m.
```
