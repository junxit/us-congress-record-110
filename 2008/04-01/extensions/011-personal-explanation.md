                           HON. BOB ETHERIDGE

also voted ``yes'' on H.R. 2675, Help to Access Land for the Education 
of Scouts Act, and ``yes'' on H. Con. Res. 302, Supporting the 
Observance of Colorectal Cancer Awareness Month.
