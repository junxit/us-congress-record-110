  COMMUNICATION FROM LEGISLATIVE DIRECTOR, HON. WILLIAM J. JEFFERSON,

Honorable William J. Jefferson, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, January 29, 2008.

     Washington, DC.

     Legislative Director.
