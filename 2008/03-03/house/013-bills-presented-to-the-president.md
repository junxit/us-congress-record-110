# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, reports that on February 28, 
2008 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills.

       H.R. 5264. To extend the Andean Trade Preference Act, and 
     for other purposes.
       H.R. 5478. To provide for the continued minting and 
     issuance of certain $1 coins in 2008.

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, reports that on February 29, 
2008 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill.

       H.R. 2082. To authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2008 
     for intelligence and intelligence-related activities of the 
     United States Government, the Community Management Account, 
     and the Central Intelligence Agency Retirement and Disability 
     System, and for other purposes.
```
