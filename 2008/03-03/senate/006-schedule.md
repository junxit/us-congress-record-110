# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following my remarks and those of the 
Republican leader, the Senate will be in a period of morning business 
until 3:30, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, 
with the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees. Following morning business, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 2663, a bill to reform the 
Consumer Product Safety Commission.
  At 5:30 today, the Senate will proceed to a rollcall vote on the 
motion to invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to the product safety 
bill.
```
