# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2472

**Speaking:** Towns, Edolphus (T000326) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2472

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2472

  Mr. TOWNS. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 2472, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Wynn of Maryland, for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.
```
