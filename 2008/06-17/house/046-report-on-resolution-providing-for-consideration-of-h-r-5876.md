# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5876, STOP CHILD ABUSE IN RESIDENTIAL PROGRAMS FOR TEENS ACT OF 2008

**Measures:** H.Res. 31, H.Res. 1276, H.R. 5876

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5876, STOP 
       CHILD ABUSE IN RESIDENTIAL PROGRAMS FOR TEENS ACT OF 2008

  Ms. SLAUGHTER (during consideration of H. Res. 31), from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 110-717) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 1276) providing for consideration of the 
bill (H.R. 5876) to require certain standards and enforcement 
provisions to prevent child abuse and neglect in residential programs, 
and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
