# DESIGNATING CHAIRMAN AND VICE CHAIRMAN OF UNITED STATES INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
 DESIGNATING CHAIRMAN AND VICE CHAIRMAN OF UNITED STATES INTERNATIONAL 
   TRADE COMMISSION--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, without 
objection, referred to the Committee on Ways and Means:

To The Congress of the United States:
  Consistent with the provisions of 19 U.S.C. 1330(c)(1), this is to 
notify the Congress that I have designated Shara L. Aranoff as Chairman 
and Daniel Pearson as Vice Chairman of the United States International 
Trade Commission, effective June 17, 2008.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, June 17, 2008.

                          ____________________
```
