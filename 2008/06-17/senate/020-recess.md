# RECESS

**Speaking:** Baucus, Max (B000243) [D-MT], Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                 RECESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12:31 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:15 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Carper).
  Mr. BAUCUS. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum call 
be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
