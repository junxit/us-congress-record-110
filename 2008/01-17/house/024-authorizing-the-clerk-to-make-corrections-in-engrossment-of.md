# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 3524, HOPE VI IMPROVEMENT AND REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2007

**Speaking:** Waters, Maxine (W000187) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3524

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 3524, 
          HOPE VI IMPROVEMENT AND REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2007

  Ms. WATERS. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Clerk be 
authorized to make technical corrections in the engrossment of H.R. 
3524, to include corrections in spelling, punctuation, section 
numbering and cross-referencing, and the insertion of appropriate 
headings.
```
