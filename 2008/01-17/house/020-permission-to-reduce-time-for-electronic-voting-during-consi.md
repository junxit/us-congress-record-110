# PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3524

**Speaking:** Waters, Maxine (W000187) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 922, H.R. 3524

```
PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING CONSIDERATION OF 
                               H.R. 3524

  Ms. WATERS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, during 
consideration of H.R. 3524 pursuant to House Resolution 922, the Chair 
may reduce to 2 minutes the minimum time

[[Page H302]]

for electronic voting under clause 6 of rule XVIII and clauses 8 and 9 
of rule XX.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
