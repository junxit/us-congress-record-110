# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

```
                      HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

  Mr. HOYER. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 10:30 a.m. tomorrow, and further, 
when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 12:30 p.m. 
on Tuesday, January 22, for morning-hour debate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Maryland?
  There was no objection.
```
