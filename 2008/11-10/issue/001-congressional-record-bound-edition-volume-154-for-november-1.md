# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for November 10, 2008

```
[[Page 24068]]

                    SENATE--Monday, November 10, 2008

           (Legislative day of Wednesday, September 17, 2008)
  The Senate met at 1 p.m., on the expiration of the recess, and was 
called to order by the Honorable Robert P. Casey, Jr., a Senator from 
the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore [Mr. Byrd].
  The bill clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                Washington, DC, November 10, 2008.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Robert P. Casey, Jr., a Senator from the Commonwealth of 
     Pennsylvania, to perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. CASEY thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



            RECESS UNTIL 3 P.M., THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 3 p.m., Thursday, November 13, 2008,
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 1 and 33 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Thursday, November 13, 2008, at 3 p.m.
```
