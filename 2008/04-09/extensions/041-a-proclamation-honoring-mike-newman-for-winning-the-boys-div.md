# A PROCLAMATION HONORING MIKE NEWMAN FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION II STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
 A PROCLAMATION HONORING MIKE NEWMAN FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION II 
                     STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 9, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Mike Newman showed hard work and dedication to the sport of 
basketball; and
  Whereas, Mike Newman was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Mike Newman always displayed sportsmanship on and off of the 
court; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Mike Newman on winning 
the Boys' Division II State Basketball Championship. We recognize the 
tremendous hard work and sportsmanship he has demonstrated during the 
2007-2008 basketball season.

                          ____________________
```
