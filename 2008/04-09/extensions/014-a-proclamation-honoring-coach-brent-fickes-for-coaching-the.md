# A PROCLAMATION HONORING COACH BRENT FICKES FOR COACHING THE NEWARK HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' BASKETBALL TEAM TO WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION I STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING COACH BRENT FICKES FOR COACHING THE NEWARK HIGH 
  SCHOOL BOYS' BASKETBALL TEAM TO WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION I STATE 
                        BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 9, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Coach Brent Fickes showed hard work and dedication to the 
sport of basketball; and
  Whereas, Brent Fickes was a leader and mentor for the Newark High 
School Boys' Basketball Team; and
  Whereas, Coach Fickes has been a role model for sportsmanship on and 
off of the court; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Coach Brent Fickes for 
leading the Newark High School Boys' Basketball Team to winning the 
Boys' Division I State Basketball Championship. We recognize the 
tremendous hard work and leadership he has demonstrated during the 
2007-2008 basketball season.

                          ____________________
```
