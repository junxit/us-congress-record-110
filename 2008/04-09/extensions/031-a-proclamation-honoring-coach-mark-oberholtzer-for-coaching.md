# A PROCLAMATION HONORING COACH MARK OBERHOLTZER FOR COACHING THE NEWARK HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' BASKETBALL TEAM TO WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION I STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING COACH MARK OBERHOLTZER FOR COACHING THE NEWARK 
HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' BASKETBALL TEAM TO WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION I STATE 
                        BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 9, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Coach Mark Oberholtzer showed hard work and dedication to 
the sport of basketball; and
  Whereas, Mark Oberholtzer was a leader and mentor for the Newark High 
School Boys' Basketball Team; and
  Whereas, Coach Oberholtzer has been a role model for sportsmanship on 
and off of the court; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Coach Mark Oberholtzer 
for leading the Newark High School Boys' Basketball Team to winning the 
Boys' Division I State Basketball Championship. We recognize the 
tremendous hard work and leadership he has demonstrated during the 
2007-2008 Basketball season.




                          ____________________
```
