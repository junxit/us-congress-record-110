# A PROCLAMATION HONORING RAY CHAMBERS FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION II STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP.

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING RAY CHAMBERS FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION II 
                     STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP.

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 9, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Ray Chambers showed hard work and dedication to the sport of 
basketball; and
  Whereas, Ray Chambers was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Ray Chambers always displayed sportsmanship on and off of 
the court; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional

[[Page E560]]

District, I congratulate Ray Chambers on winning the Boys' Division II 
State Basketball Championship. We recognize the tremendous hard work 
and sportsmanship he has demonstrated during the 2007-2008 basketball 
season.

                          ____________________
```
