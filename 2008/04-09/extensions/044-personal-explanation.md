# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Johnson, Timothy V. (J000285) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 70

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY V. JOHNSON

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, April 9, 2008

  Mr. JOHNSON of Illinois. Madam Speaker, unfortunately last night, 
April 8, 2008, my plane was delayed due to a delay in my plane arriving 
from its previous destination and I was unable to cast my vote on 
suspending the rules and passing H.J. Res. 70 and wish the record to 
reflect my intentions had I been able to vote.
  Had I been present for rollcall No. 161 on suspending the rules and 
passing H.J. Res. 70, Congratulating the Army Reserve on its 
centennial, I would have voted ``aye.''
```
