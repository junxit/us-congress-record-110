# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, DECEMBER 26, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, DECEMBER 26, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Friday, December 26, 2008, at 11 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 40 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Friday, December 26, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
