# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 3541

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House reports that on February 12, 
2008 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill.

       H.R. 3541. To amend the `Do-not-call' Implementation Act to 
     eliminate the automatic removal of telephone numbers 
     registered on the Federal `do-not-call' registry.
```
