# APPOINTMENT

```
                              APPOINTMENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to section 5 of title I of Division H of Public Law 110-161, 
appoints the following Senator as chairman of the U.S.-Japan 
Interparliamentary Group conference for the 110th Congress: the 
Honorable Ted Stevens of Alaska.
  Mrs. MURRAY. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mrs. MURRAY. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
