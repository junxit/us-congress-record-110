# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following my remarks and those of the 
Republican leader, there will be a period for the transaction of 
morning business, with 1 hour equally divided, prior to a cloture vote 
on the conference report to accompany H.R. 2082, the Intelligence 
Authorization Act for 2008.


                           Order of Procedure

  On the majority side, I ask that the time of 30 minutes be divided, 
with 15 minutes for Senator Feinstein, 10 minutes for Senator 
Rockefeller, and 5 minutes for Senator Wyden.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
