# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Johnson, Timothy V. (J000285) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.Res. 954

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY V. JOHNSON

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 13, 2008

  Mr. JOHNSON of Illinois. Madam Speaker, unfortunately last night, 
February 12, 2008, I was unable to cast my vote on Suspending the Rules 
and passing H. Res. 954 and wish the record to reflect my intentions 
had I been able to vote.
  Had I been present for rollcall No. 43 on passing H. Res. 954, 
Honoring the life of senior Border Patrol agent Luis A. Aguilar, who 
lost his life in the line of duty near Yuma, Arizona, on January 19, 
2008, I would have voted ``aye.''
```
