# MEASURE READ FIRST TIME--H.J. RES. 92

```
                 MEASURE READ FIRST TIME--H.J. RES. 92

  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I understand that H.J. Res. 92 has 
been received from the House and is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. LAUTENBERG. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the joint resolution by 
title for the first time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A joint resolution (H. J. Res. 92) increasing the statutory 
     limit on the public debt.

  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I ask for its second reading and 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
