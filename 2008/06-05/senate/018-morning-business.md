# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will now proceed to a period of morning business for up to 2 
hours, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees, with the Republicans in control of the 
first 30 minutes and the majority in control of the second 30 minutes.
  The Senator from Wyoming is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
