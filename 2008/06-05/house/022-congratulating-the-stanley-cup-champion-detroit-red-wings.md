# CONGRATULATING THE STANLEY CUP CHAMPION DETROIT RED WINGS

**Speaking:** Kilpatrick, Carolyn C. (K000180) [D-MI]

```
       CONGRATULATING THE STANLEY CUP CHAMPION DETROIT RED WINGS

  (Ms. KILPATRICK asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Ms. KILPATRICK. I rise today to congratulate the Detroit Hockey Red 
Wings. The Detroit Red Wings, the 2008 National Hockey League champions 
for the Stanley Cup. We are most proud of you. To the coaches, the 
players, the Ilitch family, thank you for a wonderful, exciting season.
  The Detroit Red Wings, the Stanley Cup champions for 2008. You have 
brought joy and cheer and adventure to all of us. Good luck to you. 
Enjoy your time off. And have another wonderful season as we march to 
the 2009 Stanley Cup.
  Congratulations, Red Wings.

                          ____________________
```
