# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 70

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate agrees to the report of the committee of conference on 
the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment of the House 
to the resolution (S. Con. Res. 70) entitled ``Concurrent resolution 
setting forth the congressional budget for the United States Government 
for fiscal year 2009 and including the appropriate budgetary levels for 
fiscal years 2008 and 2010 through 2013.''
```
