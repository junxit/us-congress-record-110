# RECESS UNTIL 10 A.M., FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 22, 2008

```
            RECESS UNTIL 10 A.M., FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 22, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Friday, February 22, 2008, at 10 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11:02 and 10 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Friday, February 22, 2008, at 10 a.m.
```
