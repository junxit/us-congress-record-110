# TRIBUTE TO COMMANDER R. TREVOR KING, U.S. NAVY

```
             TRIBUTE TO COMMANDER R. TREVOR KING, U.S. NAVY

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. IKE SKELTON

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 18, 2008

  Mr. SKELTON. Madam Speaker, let me take this opportunity to recognize 
Commander R. Trevor King, United States Navy, for his service to the 
House Armed Services Committee as a member of the Navy's congressional 
liaison team.
  A native of Thousand Oaks, California, in 1991, Commander King earned 
an economics degree from the U.S. Naval Academy. Since that time, he 
has served his country well in various posts and in numerous 
operations. He has received the Commander Naval Surface Forces Battle 
Efficiency ``E'' for excellence award two years in a row.
  In August, Commander King will begin the curriculum at the National 
War College during which time he will complete his Joint Professional 
Military Education. Following that assignment, he will likely be 
assigned as commanding officer of one of the Navy's surface combatants.
  I am certain that the Members of the House will join me in honoring 
Commander R. Trevor King, his wife, Robynn, and his daughters, Erika 
and Caitlynn, for their outstanding and continued service to our 
country.

                          ____________________
```
