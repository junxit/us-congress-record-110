# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Mikulski, Barbara A. (M000702) [D-MD]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to a period of morning business for up to 2 hours, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the majority controlling the first hour and the 
Republicans controlling the next hour.
  The Senator from Maryland.
  Ms. MIKULSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
following Senators be permitted to speak for up to 5 minutes: Myself, 
McCaskill, Feinstein, Landrieu, Murray, Boxer, Stabenow, Klobuchar, and 
Lincoln.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
