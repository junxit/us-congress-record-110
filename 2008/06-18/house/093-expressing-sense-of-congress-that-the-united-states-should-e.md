# EXPRESSING SENSE OF CONGRESS THAT THE UNITED STATES SHOULD END COMMERCIAL WHALING

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 350

```
    EXPRESSING SENSE OF CONGRESS THAT THE UNITED STATES SHOULD END 
                           COMMERCIAL WHALING

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and agreeing to the concurrent resolution, H. Con. 
Res. 350.
  The Clerk read the title of the concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Massachusetts (Mr. Delahunt) that the House suspend the 
rules and agree to the concurrent resolution, H. Con. Res. 350.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the concurrent resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
