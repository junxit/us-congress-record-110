# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 
minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees, with the first half of the time under the 
control of the Republican leader or his designee and the second half of 
the time under control of the majority leader or his designee.
  The Senator from California.

                          ____________________
```
