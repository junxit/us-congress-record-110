# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for October 23, 2008

```
[[Page 24063]]

                   SENATE--Thursday, October 23, 2008

           (Legislative day of Wednesday, September 17, 2008)
  The Senate met at 2 and 18 seconds p.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Christopher J. Dodd, a 
Senator from the State of Connecticut.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                 Washington, DC, October 23, 2008.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Christopher J. Dodd, a Senator from the State of Connecticut, 
     to perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. DODD thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro tempore.

                          ____________________



            RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, OCTOBER 27, 2008, AT 9 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Monday, October 27, 2008, at 9 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 2 and 47 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Monday, October 27, 2008, at 9 a.m.
```
