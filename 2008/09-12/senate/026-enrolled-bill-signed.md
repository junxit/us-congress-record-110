# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 6475, H.R. 6532

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  The President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd) announced that on September 11, 
2008, he had signed the following enrolled bill, previously signed by 
the Speaker of the House:

       H.R. 6532. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to restore the Highway Trust Fund balance.
                                  ____

  At 11:55 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 6475. An act to establish the Daniel Webster 
     Congressional Clerkship Program.

                          ____________________
```
