# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
FEDERAL GOVERNMENT ROLE--SMALL BUSINESS DISASTER RECOVERY
Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure: Subcommittee on 
Economic Development, Public Buildings, and Emergency Management held a 
hearing on the Role of the Federal Government in Small Business 
Disaster Recovery. Testimony was heard from Representatives King and 
Braley, both of Iowa; Herbert Mitchell, Assistant Administrator, Office 
of Disaster Assistance, SBA; and Marko Bourne, Director, Policy and 
Program Analysis, FEMA, Department of Homeland Security.
```
