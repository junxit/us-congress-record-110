# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 
minutes, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, 
with the time equally divided and controlled by the two leaders or 
their designees, with the first half of the time under control of the 
Republicans and the second half of the time under control of the 
majority.
  The Senator from Missouri.

                          ____________________
```
