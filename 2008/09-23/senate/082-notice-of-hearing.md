# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Feinstein, Dianne (F000062) [D-CA]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                 COMMITTEE ON RULES AND ADMINISTRATION

  Mrs. FEINSTEIN. Mr. President, I wish to announce that the Committee 
on Rules and Administration will meet on Thursday, September 25, 2008,

members of the Election Assistance Commission.
  Individuals and organizations that wish to submit a statement for the 
hearing record are requested to contact the Chief Clerk, Lynden 
Armstrong, at 202-224-7078.
  For further information regarding this hearing, please contact Howard 
Gantman at the Rules and Administration Committee, 202-224-6352.
```
