# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

**Measures:** S. 1382, S. 1810, S. 2932, S. 3328, H.R. 4120, H.R. 6049

```
                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 5633. Mr. BAUCUS (for himself, Mr. Grassley, and Mr. 
     Reid) proposed an amendment to the bill H.R. 6049, to amend

     prevention, sustain the funding of poison centers, and
```
