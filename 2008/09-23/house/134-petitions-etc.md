# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.R. 3970

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       308. The SPEAKER presented a petition of San Francisco 
     Labor Council, relative to a resolution asking to Stop 
     Escalating the Confrontation with Iran & Reduce the Danger of 
     Another Military Conflict in the Persian Gulf; to the 
     Committee on Foreign Affairs.
       309. Also, a petition of Menke & Associates, Inc., relative 
     to a request to reject proposed Section 3701 of the Tax 
     Rduction and Reform Act of 2007 (H.R. 3970); to the Committee 
     on Ways and Means.
```
