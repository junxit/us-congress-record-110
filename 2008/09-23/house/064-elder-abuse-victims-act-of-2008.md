                              {time}  1858

  So (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the rules were suspended and 
the bill, as amended, was passed.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title was amended so as to read: ``A bill to protect seniors in 
the United States from elder abuse by establishing specialized elder 
abuse prosecution and research programs and activities to aid victims 
of elder abuse, to provide training to prosecutors and other law 
enforcement related to elder abuse prevention and protection, to 
establish programs that provide for emergency crisis response teams to 
combat elder abuse, and for other purposes.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
