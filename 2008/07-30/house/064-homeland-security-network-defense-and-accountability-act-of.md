# HOMELAND SECURITY NETWORK DEFENSE AND ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2008

```
    HOMELAND SECURITY NETWORK DEFENSE AND ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2008

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill, H.R. 5983, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Mississippi (Mr. Thompson) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, H.R. 5983, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
