# Text of House Amendment 31

**Speaking:** Jefferson, William J. (J000070) [D-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 6599

```
H.R. 6599

                       Offered By: Mr. Jefferson

       Amendment No. 31: At the end of title II (page 51, after 
     line 11), insert the following:
       Sec. 226.  In making amounts available under ``General 
     operating expenses'' and ``Medical support and compliance'' 
     to carry out the guaranteed transitional housing loan program 
     authorized by subchapter VI of chapter 20 of title 38, United 
     States Code, priority shall be given for funding to any area 
     in the Gulf Opportunity Zone.
```
