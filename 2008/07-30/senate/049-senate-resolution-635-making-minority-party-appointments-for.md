# SENATE RESOLUTION 635--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS FOR THE 110TH CONGRESS

**Measures:** S.Res. 635

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 635--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS FOR THE 110TH 
                                CONGRESS

  Mr. McCONNELL submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 635

       Resolved, That the following be the minority membership on 
     the following committee for the remainder of the 110th 
     Congress, or until their successors are appointed:
       Committee on Commerce, Science and Transportation: Mrs. 
     Hutchison, Mr. Stevens, Mr. McCain, Ms. Snowe, Mr. Smith, Mr. 
     Ensign, Mr. Sununu, Mr. DeMint, Mr. Vitter, Mr. Thune, Mr. 
     Wicker.

                          ____________________
```
