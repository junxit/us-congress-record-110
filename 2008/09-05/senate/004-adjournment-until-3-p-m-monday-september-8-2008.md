# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 3 P.M., MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 2008

          ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 3 P.M., MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the

Monday, September 8, 2008, at 3 p.m.
