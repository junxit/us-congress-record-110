# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for November 3, 2008

```
[[Page 24066]]

                    SENATE--Monday, November 3, 2008

           (Legislative day of Wednesday, September 17, 2008)
  The Senate met at 10 and 14 seconds a.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Benjamin L. Cardin, a 
Senator from the State of Maryland.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The bill clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                 Washington, DC, November 3, 2008.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Benjamin L. Cardin, a Senator from the State of Maryland, to 
     perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. CARDIN thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



                RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 11 a.m., Thursday, November 6, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 41 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Thursday, November 6, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
