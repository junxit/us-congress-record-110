# RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2008

```
                RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 11 a.m., Thursday, November 6, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 41 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Thursday, November 6, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
