# UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--S. 2664

```
                   UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--S. 2664

  Mr. REID. Before my friend leaves the floor, I have a unanimous 
consent request to make.
  Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to the 
immediate consideration of Calendar No. 583, S. 2664, which is the 30-
day extension of the Protect America Act; further, the bill be read a 
third time and passed, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, reserving the right to object, I don't 
believe this extension includes the immunity provision for the 
telecoms; thus, I will object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
