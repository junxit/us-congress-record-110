# COSPONSORS OF S. 2716

```
                         COSPONSORS OF S. 2716

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senator from 
Alabama, Mr. Sessions, the Senator from Texas, Mr. Cornyn, the Senator 
from Louisiana, Mr. Vitter, and the Senator from South Carolina, Mr. 
DeMint, be added as cosponsors to my bill to authorize the National 
Guard to provide support for the border control activities of the 
United States Customs and Border Protection of the Department of 
Homeland Security, and for other purposes, S. 2716.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
