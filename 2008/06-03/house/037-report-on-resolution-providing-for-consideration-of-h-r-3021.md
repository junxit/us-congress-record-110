# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3021, 21ST CENTURY GREEN HIGH-PERFORMING PUBLIC SCHOOL FACILITIES ACT

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3021, 21ST 
       CENTURY GREEN HIGH-PERFORMING PUBLIC SCHOOL FACILITIES ACT

  Mr. HASTINGS of Florida, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 110-678) on the resolution (H. Res. 1234) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3021) to direct the 
Secretary of Education to make grants and low-interest loans to local 
educational agencies for the construction, modernization, or repair of 
public kindergarten, elementary, and secondary educational facilities, 
and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
