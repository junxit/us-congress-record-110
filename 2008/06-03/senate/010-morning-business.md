# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period of morning business until 11 a.m., with 
the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half and 
the majority controlling the final half of the time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Texas is 
recognized.

                          ____________________
```
