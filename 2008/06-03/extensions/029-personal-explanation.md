Defense Authorization Act, 2009, had I been present, I would have voted 
``aye''; on rollcall No. 359, on agreeing to the Lee amendment to H.R. 
5658, the Department of Defense Authorization Act, 2009, had I been 
present, I would have voted ``no''; on rollcall No. 360, on agreeing to 
the Braley amendment to H.R. 5658, the Department of Defense 
Authorization Act, 2009, had I been present, I would have voted ``no''; 
on rollcall No. 361, on agreeing to the Price, NC, amendment to H.R. 
5658, the Department of Defense Authorization Act, 2009, had I been 
present, I would have voted ``no''; on rollcall No. 362, on agreeing to

Authorization Act, 2009, had I been present, I would have voted ``no'';

prisoners of war during the Vietnam conflict and calling for a full 
accounting of the 1,729 members of the Armed Forces who remain 
unaccounted for from the Vietnam conflict, had I been present, I would 
have voted ``aye.''
