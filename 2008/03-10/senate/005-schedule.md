# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following my remarks and those of the 
Republican leader, the Senate will be in a period of morning business 
until 3 p.m. Senators will be allowed to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees. Following morning business, the Senate will 
proceed to consideration of the concurrent resolution on the budget. 
There will be no rollcall votes today.
```
