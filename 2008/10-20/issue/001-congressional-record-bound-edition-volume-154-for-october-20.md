# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for October 20, 2008

```
[[Page 24062]]

                    SENATE--Monday, October 20, 2008

           (Legislative day of Wednesday, September 17, 2008)
  The Senate met at 3 and 5 seconds p.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Bernard Sanders, a 
Senator from the State of Vermont.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The bill clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                 Washington, DC, October 20, 2008.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Bernard Sanders, a Senator from the State of Vermont, to 
     perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. SANDERS thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



           RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, OCTOBER 23, 2008, AT 2 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Thursday, October 23, 2008, at 2 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 3 and 35 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Thursday, October 23, 2008, at 2 p.m.
```
