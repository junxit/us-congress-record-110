# Text of Senate Amendment 4524

**Speaking:** Nelson, Ben (N000180) [D-NE]

```
  SA 4524. Mr. NELSON of Nebraska submitted an amendment intended to be

activities in the Department of the Interior, the Forest Service, and 
the Department of Energy, to implement further the Act approving the 
Covenant to Establish a Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands in
```
