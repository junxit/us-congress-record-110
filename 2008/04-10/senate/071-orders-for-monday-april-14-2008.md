# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 14, 2008

**Speaking:** Salazar, Ken (S001163) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 1195

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 14, 2008

  Mr. SALAZAR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 2 p.m., 
Monday, April 14; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for use later in the day, the 
Senate proceed to a period of morning business until 3 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each; and that 
following morning business, the Senate resume the motion to proceed to 
Calendar No. 608, H.R. 1195, the highway technical corrections bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
