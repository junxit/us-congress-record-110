# Vote On Nomination Of James Randal Hall (Executive Session)

```
                Vote On Nomination Of James Randal Hall

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the nomination of James Randal 
Hall, of Georgia, to be United States District Judge for the Southern 
District of Georgia?
  The nomination was confirmed.
```
