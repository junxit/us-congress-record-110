# Vote On Nomination Of John A. Mendez (Executive Session)

```
                  Vote On Nomination Of John A. Mendez

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the nomination of John A. Mendez, 
of California, to be United States District Judge for the Eastern 
District of California?
  The nomination was confirmed.
```
