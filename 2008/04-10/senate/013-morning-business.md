# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period of morning business for 60 minutes, 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, 
with the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders, 
or their designees, with the majority controlling the first half and 
the Republicans controlling the final half.
  The Senator from Montana is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
