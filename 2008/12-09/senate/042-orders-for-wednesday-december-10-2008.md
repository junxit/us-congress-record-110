# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 10, 2008

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 10, 2008

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10:30 a.m., on 
Wednesday, December 10; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and that there then be a period for the transaction of 
morning business for up to 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak for 
up to 10 minutes each, and that the Senate then resume consideration of 
the motion to proceed to S. 3297.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
