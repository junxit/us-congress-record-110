# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord, You are powerful and strong, yet You bend to Your people and 
show them mercy and sustaining grace. Help the leaders of our Nation 
make wise decisions in our day. Set us on a path that will lead to

until we stand in Your light forever and ever.
```
