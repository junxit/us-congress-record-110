# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on April 
28, 2008, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       GEORGE A. KROL, OF NEW JERSEY, A CAREER MEMBER OF THE 
     SENIOR FOREIGN SERVICE, CLASS OF MINISTER-COUNSELOR, TO BE 
     AMBASSADOR EXTRAORDINARY AND PLENIPOTENTIARY OF THE UNITED 
     STATES OF AMERICA TO TURKMENISTAN, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE 
     SENATE ON JUNE 27, 2007.
```
