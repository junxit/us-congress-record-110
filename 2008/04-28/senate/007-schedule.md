# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2881

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we are going to be in a period of morning 
business until 4:30 today, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 
10 minutes each, the time equally divided and controlled between the 
two leaders or their designees. Senator Dorgan is controlling 30 
minutes of the majority's time. Following morning business, the Senate 
will resume consideration of the motion to proceed to H.R. 2881, the 
Federal Aviation Administration Reauthorization. At 5:30 today there 
will be a cloture vote on the motion to proceed to that bill.
  As a reminder to all Senators, Wednesday, at 11 a.m., there will be a 
joint meeting of Congress in the Hall of the House of Representatives, 
with the Prime Minister of Ireland, Bertie Ahern, presenting his 
statement to the country.

                          ____________________
```
