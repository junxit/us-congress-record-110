# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings

NOMINATIONS
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine the nominations of William J. Burns, of the District of 
Columbia, to be an Under Secretary of State (Political Affairs), Janice 
L. Jacobs, of Virginia, to be an Assistant Secretary of State (Bureau 
of Consular Affairs), and T. Vance McMahan, of Texas, to be 
Representative of the United States of America on the Economic and 
Social Council of the United Nations, with the rank of Ambassador, 
after the nominees testified and answered questions in their own 
behalf.
```
