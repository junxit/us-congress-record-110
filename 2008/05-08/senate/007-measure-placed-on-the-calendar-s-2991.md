# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2991

                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2991

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I understand that S. 2991 is at the desk 
and due for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator is correct.
  The clerk will read the bill by title for the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

     with regard to high energy prices, and for other purposes.

  Mr. DURBIN. I object to any further proceedings with respect to the 
bill.

bill will be placed on the calendar.
