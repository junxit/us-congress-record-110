# Text of Senate Amendment 4749

**Speaking:** Baucus, Max (B000243) [D-MT]

```
  SA 4749. Mr. BAUCUS submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 2284, to amend the National Flood Insurance Act of 
1968, to restore the financial solvency of the flood insurance fund, 
and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. AIRLINE MERGERS.

       The Comptroller General of the United States shall conduct 
     a study of, and submit a report regarding, whether the 
     proposed merger of Northwest Airlines and Delta Air Lines 
     announced April 14, 2008, will harm air transport services in 
     rural areas.
```
