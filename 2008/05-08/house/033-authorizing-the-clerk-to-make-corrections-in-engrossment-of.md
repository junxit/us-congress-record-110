**Measures:** H.R. 3221, H.R. 5818

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 5818, 
                 NEIGHBORHOOD STABILIZATION ACT OF 2008
```
