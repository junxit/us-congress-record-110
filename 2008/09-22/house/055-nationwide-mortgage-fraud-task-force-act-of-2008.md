                Announcement by the Speaker Pro Tempore

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (during the vote). There is 1 minute

                              {time}  1912

  Messrs. BURTON of Indiana and PENCE changed their votes from ``nay'' 
to ``yea.''
  So (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the rules were suspended and 
the bill, as amended, was passed.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title was amended so as to read: ``A bill to establish in the 
Federal Bureau of Investigation the Nationwide Mortgage Fraud 
Coordinator to address mortgage fraud in the United States, and for 
other purposes.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
