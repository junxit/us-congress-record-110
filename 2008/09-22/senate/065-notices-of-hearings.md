                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS

Committee at 202-224-2251.

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the

Virginia Federal Recognition Act of 2007; (2) S. 514, Muscogee Nation 
of Florida Federal Recognition Act; (3) S. 724, Little Shell Tribe of 
Chippewa Indians Restoration Act of 2007; and (4) S. 1058, Grand River 
Bands of Ottawa Indians of Michigan Referral Act.
  Those wishing additional information may contact the Indian Affairs 
Committee at 202-224-2251.
