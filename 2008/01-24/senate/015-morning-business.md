# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Harkin, Tom (H000206) [D-IA]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to a period of morning business, with Senators permitted to 
speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Nelson of Florida). The clerk will call 
the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. HARKIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Whitehouse). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
