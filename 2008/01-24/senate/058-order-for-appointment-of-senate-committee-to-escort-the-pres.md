# ORDER FOR APPOINTMENT OF SENATE COMMITTEE TO ESCORT THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
 ORDER FOR APPOINTMENT OF SENATE COMMITTEE TO ESCORT THE PRESIDENT OF 
                           THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Presiding 
Officer of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part 
of the Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the House of 
Representatives to escort the President of the United States into the 
House Chamber for the joint session to be held at 9 p.m. on Monday, 
January 28, 2008.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
