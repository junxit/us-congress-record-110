# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 25, 2008

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2248

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 25, 2008

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m. Friday, January 25; 
that on Friday following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; and the Senate then resume consideration of S. 2248, the FISA 
legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
