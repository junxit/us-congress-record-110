# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for August 8, 2008

```
[[Page 17955]]


                     SENATE--Friday, August 8, 2008

               (Legislative day of Friday, August 1, 2008)
  The Senate met at 11 o'clock and 11 seconds a.m., on the expiration 
of the recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Nancy Erickson, 
Secretary of the Senate.

                          ____________________



            RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, AUGUST 12, 2008, AT 2 P.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate stands in 
recess until Tuesday, August 12, 2008, at 2 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 o'clock and 35 seconds a.m., recessed 
until Tuesday, August 12, 2008, at 2 p.m.
```
