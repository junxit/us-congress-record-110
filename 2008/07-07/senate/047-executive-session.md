provided further that the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action, and the Senate return to legislative session.

  The nominations considered and confirmed en bloc are as follows:

     States Tax Court for a term of fifteen years.
       Elizabeth Crewson Paris, of the District of Columbia, to be 
     a Judge of the United States Tax Court for a term of fifteen 
     years.


                       department of the treasury

     Revenue Service Oversight Board for a term expiring September
