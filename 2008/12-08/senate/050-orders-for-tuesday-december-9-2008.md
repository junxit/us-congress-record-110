# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 9, 2008

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD]

**Measures:** S. 3297

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 9, 2008

  Mr. CARDIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10:30 
a.m. tomorrow, Tuesday, December 9; that following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, that the 
morning hour be deemed expired, that the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and that the Senate proceed to 
a period of morning business until 12 noon, with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each, and that following morning business 
the Senate resume the motion to proceed to S. 3297.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
