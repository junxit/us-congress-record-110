# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

**Measures:** H.R. 2040

```
             MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

                                 ______
                                 

                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 2008, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on November 21, 2008, during the recess of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker had signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 2040. An act to require the Secretary of the Treasury 
     to mint coins in commemoration of the semicentennial of the 
     enactment of the Civil Rights Act of 1964.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 2008, 
the enrolled bill was signed on November 24, 2008, by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Byrd).




                          ____________________
```
