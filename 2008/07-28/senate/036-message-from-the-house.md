# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 93

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

                                 ______
                                 

                    ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

  At 5:53 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker 
pro tempore (Mr. Hoyer) has signed the following enrolled joint 
resolution:

       H.J. Res. 93. Joint resolution approving the renewal of 
     import restrictions contained in the Burmese Freedom and 
     Democracy Act of 2003.

  The joint resolution was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Byrd).
```
