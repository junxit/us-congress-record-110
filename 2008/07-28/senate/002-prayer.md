# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, You have plans for us and power to make them happen. 
Give the Members of this body a knowledge of Your will for our Nation 
and world. Remind them that they serve a public trust, beyond personal 
gain or glory. May they see that no nation lives for itself alone but 
is responsible to You for peace, and for the well-being of all of Your 
children. We pray in Your sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
