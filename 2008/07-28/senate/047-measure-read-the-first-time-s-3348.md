# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3348

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3348

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I understand that S. 3348, introduced 
earlier today by Senator Coburn, is at the desk. I ask for its first 
reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the title of the bill 
for the first time.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3348) to provide for the investigation of 
     certain unsolved civil rights crimes, and for other purposes.

  Mr. DURBIN. I now ask for its second reading and object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will 
remain at the desk and have its second reading on the next legislative 
day.

                          ____________________
```
