# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period for the transaction of morning business 
until 12 noon, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the first hour equally divided and controlled between the 
two leaders or their designee, with the Republicans controlling the 
first 30 minutes and the majority controlling the next 30 minutes.
  The Senator from South Dakota is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
