# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5658, DUNCAN HUNTER NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2009

**Measures:** H.Res. 1213, H.R. 5658

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5658, DUNCAN 
     HUNTER NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2009

  Mr. ARCURI, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-661) on the resolution (H. Res. 1213) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5658) to authorize appropriations 
for fiscal year 2009 for military activities of the Department of 
Defense, to prescribe military personnel strengths for fiscal year 
2009, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
