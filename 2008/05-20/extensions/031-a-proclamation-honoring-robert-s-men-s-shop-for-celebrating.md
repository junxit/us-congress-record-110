# A PROCLAMATION HONORING ROBERT'S MEN'S SHOP FOR CELEBRATING THEIR 35TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING ROBERT'S MEN'S SHOP FOR CELEBRATING THEIR 35TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 20, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Robert's Men's Shop has been providing excellent service to 
the people of New Philadelphia, Ohio for 35 years; and
  Whereas, Robert's Men's Shop has grown and prospered since 1973 
through hard work and determination; and
  Whereas, Robert's Men's Shop was named 2005 Small Business of the 
Year by the Tuscarawas County Chamber of Commerce; now, therefore, be 
it

[[Page E977]]

  Resolved that along with the residents of the 18th Congressional 
District, I commend and thank Robert's Men's Shop for its contributions 
to its community and country.

                          ____________________
```
