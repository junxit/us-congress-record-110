  Mrs. WILSON of New Mexico. Madam Speaker, the following graduating

achievements, community service, and participation in school and civic 
activities. It is my pleasure to be able to recognize these outstanding 
students for their accomplishments. Their parents, their teachers, 
their classmates, the people of New Mexico and I are proud of them.
