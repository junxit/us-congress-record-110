**Speaking:** Serrano, Jose E. (S000248) [D-NY]

```
                 ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MARCH 10, 2008

  Mr. SERRANO (during the Special Order of Mr. Price of Georgia). Mr. 
Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House adjourns today, it

to meet at 10:30 a.m. on Thursday, March 13.
```
