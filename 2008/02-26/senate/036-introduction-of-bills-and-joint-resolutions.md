# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. MENENDEZ:
       S. 2667. A bill to direct the Attorney General to make an 
     annual grant to the A Child Is Missing Alert and Recovery 
     Center to assist law enforcement agencies in the rapid 
     recovery of missing children, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. KERRY (for himself and Mr. Ensign):
       S. 2668. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to remove cell phones from listed property under section 
     280F; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Ms. SNOWE (for herself, Mr. Rockefeller, Mr. Pryor, 
             Ms. Collins, and Mr. Kerry):
       S. 2669. A bill to provide for the implementation of a 
     Green Chemistry Research and Development Program, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
```
