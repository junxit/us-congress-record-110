# HONORING THOSE KILLED IN THE KHOJALY MASSACRE

```
             HONORING THOSE KILLED IN THE KHOJALY MASSACRE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ZOE LOFGREN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 26, 2008

  Ms. ZOE LOFGREN of California. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor 
those killed during the Khojaly Massacre on the 16th anniversary of 
that terrible event. On February 26, 1992, 613 Azerbaijanis lost their 
lives. I ask that this Congress remember those who were killed that 
tragic day.

                          ____________________
```
