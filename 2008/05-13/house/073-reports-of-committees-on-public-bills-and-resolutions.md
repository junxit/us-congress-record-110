     years 2008 and 2010 through 2013 (Rept. 110-630). Referred to 
     the House Calendar.
       Mr. RAHALL: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 3323. A

     Counties, New Mexico, and to establish the Lesser Prairie

     purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 110-632). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the State of the Union.
       Mr. RAHALL: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 4074. A 
     bill to authorize the implementation of the San Joaquin River 
     Restoration Settlement, and for other purposes (Rept. 110-
     633). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     State of the Union.
       Mr. RAHALL: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 2649. A 
     bill to make amendments to the Reclamation Projects 
     Authorization and Adjustment Act of 1992; with an amendment 
     (Rept. 110-634). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House 
     on the State of the Union.
       Mr. RAHALL: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 1771. A 
     bill to assist in the conservation of cranes by supporting

     the conservation programs of countries and activities of 
     which directly or indirectly affect cranes and the ecosystems 
     of cranes; with an amendment (Rept. 110-635). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the State of the Union.
