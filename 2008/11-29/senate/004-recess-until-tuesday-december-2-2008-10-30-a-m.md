# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, DECEMBER 2, 2008, 10:30 A.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, DECEMBER 2, 2008, 10:30 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Tuesday, December 2, 2008, at 10:30 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 2 and 30 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, December 2, 2008, at 10:30 a.m.
```
