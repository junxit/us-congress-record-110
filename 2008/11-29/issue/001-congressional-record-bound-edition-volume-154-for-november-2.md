# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for November 29, 2008

```
[[Page 24440]]


                   SENATE--Saturday, November 29, 2008

            (Legislative day of Thursday, November 20, 2008)
  The Senate met at 2 and 3 seconds p.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Patrick J. Leahy, a 
Senator from the State of Vermont.

                          ____________________



               APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                Washington, DC, November 29, 2008.
      To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, section 3, of the Standing 
     Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Patrick 
     J. Leahy, a Senator from the State of Vermont, to perform the 
     duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. LEAHY thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



           RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, DECEMBER 2, 2008, 10:30 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Tuesday, December 2, 2008, at 10:30 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 2 and 30 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, December 2, 2008, at 10:30 a.m.
```
