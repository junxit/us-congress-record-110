# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 5, 2008

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 5, 2008

  Mr. PRYOR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 
a.m., Wednesday, March 5; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and that the Senate proceed to a period of morning business 
for up to 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each with the time equally divided and controlled between the 
two leaders or their designees, with the Republicans controlling the 
first half and the majority controlling the final half; further, I ask 
that following morning business the Senate resume consideration of S. 
2663, a bill to reform the Consumer Product Safety Commission.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
