# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Pryor, Mark L. (P000590) [D-AR], Nelson, Bill (N000032) [D-FL]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. PRYOR. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to a 
period of morning business with Senators permitted to speak for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. PRYOR. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. NELSON of Florida. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
the order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
