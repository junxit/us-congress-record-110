# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. There will now be a period for the 
transaction of morning business for 60 minutes, with Senators permitted 
to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally 
divided and controlled by the two leaders or their designees, with the 
majority controlling the first half of the time and the Republicans 
controlling the final half.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Washington.

                          ____________________
```
