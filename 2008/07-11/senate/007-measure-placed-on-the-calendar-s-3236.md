# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3236

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3236

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, S. 3236 is at the desk, and it is my 
understanding it is now due for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the title of 
the bill for the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3236) to amend titles XVIII and XIX of the 
     Social Security Act to extend provisions under the Medicaid 
     programs, and for other purposes.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The majority leader is recognized.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, at this time, I object to any further 
proceedings with regard to this bill.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard. The bill will 
be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
