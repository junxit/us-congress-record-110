# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2731, H.R. 3221

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following any remarks Senator McConnell and 
I may make, we will resume the motion to disagree to the House 
amendments with respect to H.R. 3221, the housing reform legislation. 
At approximately 5:20 p.m., the 30-hour postcloture debate time will 
run out and the Senate will proceed to a rollcall vote on the motion to

cloture vote on the motion to proceed to S. 2731, the global HIV/AIDS
```
