# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, DECEMBER 5, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, DECEMBER 5, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Friday, December 5, 2008, at 11 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10:30 and 27 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Friday, December 5, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
