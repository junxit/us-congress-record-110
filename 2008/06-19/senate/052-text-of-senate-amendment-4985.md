# Text of Senate Amendment 4985

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

```
  SA 4985. Mr. BOND (for himself and Mr. Barrasso) proposed an 
amendment to amendment SA 4983 proposed by Mr. Reid (for Mr. Dodd (for 
himself and Mr. Shelby)) to the bill H.R. 3221, moving the United 
States toward greater energy independence and security, developing 
innovative new technologies, reducing carbon emissions, creating green

production of renewable energy and energy conservation; as follows:
```
