# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 20, 2008

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 20, 2008

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in recess until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Friday, June 20; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date and the Senate resume 
consideration of the House message to accompany H.R. 3221, the housing 
legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
