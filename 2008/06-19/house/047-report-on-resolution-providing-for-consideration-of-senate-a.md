# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENTS TO HOUSE AMENDMENTS TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 2642, SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2008

**Measures:** H.Res. 1284, H.R. 2642

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENTS 
  TO HOUSE AMENDMENTS TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 2642, SUPPLEMENTAL 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2008

  Ms. SLAUGHTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-720) on the resolution (H. Res. 1284) providing 
for consideration of the Senate amendments to the House amendments to 
the Senate amendment to the bill (H.R. 2642) making appropriations for 
military construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related 
agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2008, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
