# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6304, FISA AMENDMENTS ACT OF 2008

**Measures:** H.Res. 1285, H.R. 6304

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6304, FISA 
                         AMENDMENTS ACT OF 2008

  Ms. SLAUGHTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-721) on the resolution (H. Res. 1285) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 6304) to amend the Foreign 
Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978 to establish a procedure for 
authorizing certain acquisitions of foreign intelligence, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
