# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 6304

```
[[Page H5731]]
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                         Offered by Mr. Conyers

       The provisions in H.R. 6304, the FISA Amendments Act of 
     2008, that warranted a referral to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary do not contain any congressional earmarks, limited 
     tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 
     9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of rule XXI.

                          Offered by Mr. Reyes

       In accordance with clause 9 of rule XXI of the Rules of the 
     House of Representatives, H.R. 6304, the FISA Amendments Act 
     of 2008, does not contain any congressional earmarks, limited 
     tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 
     9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of rule XXI.

                          ____________________
```
