                   MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 12

  Mr. REID. Madam President, there is a bill at the desk due for its 
first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 12) to promote home ownership, manufacturing, 
     and economic growth.

order to place the bill on the Calendar under rule XIV, I object to my

the second time on the next legislative day.
