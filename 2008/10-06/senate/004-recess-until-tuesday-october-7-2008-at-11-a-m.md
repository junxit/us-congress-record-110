# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Tuesday, October 7, 2008, at 11 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 3 and 35 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, October 7, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
