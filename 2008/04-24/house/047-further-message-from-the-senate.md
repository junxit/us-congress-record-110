# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with an amendment in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 493. An act to prohibit discrimination on the basis of 
     genetic information with respect to health insurance and 
     employment.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1315. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     enhance veterans' insurance and housing benefits, to improve 
     benefits and services for transitioning servicemembers, and 
     for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
