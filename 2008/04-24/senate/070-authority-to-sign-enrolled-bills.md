# AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS

```
                    AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent the majority leader be authorized 
to sign duly enrolled bills and joint resolutions through the recess or 
adjournment of the Senate until Monday, April 28, of this year.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
