# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on April 
24, 2008 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:
       C. BOYDEN GRAY, OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, TO BE 
     REPRESENTATIVE OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA TO THE 
     EUROPEAN UNION, WITH THE RANK AND STATUS OF AMBASSADOR 
     EXTRAORDINARY AND PLENIPOTENTIARY, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE 
     SENATE ON JANUARY 9, 2007.
```
