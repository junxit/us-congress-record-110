# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, MARCH 31, 2008, AT 2 P.M.

```
             RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, MARCH 31, 2008, AT 2 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Monday, March 31, 2008, at 2 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9 and 39 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Monday, March 31, 2008, at 2 p.m.
```
