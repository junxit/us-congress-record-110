# JOBS TRAINING PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

```
                         JOBS TRAINING PROGRAM

  (Mr. DAVIS of Illinois asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I know we are going to be looking 
at economic stimulus packages, and we are going to be trying to jump-
start and help our economy. It is my belief that one way that we can do 
that is to make sure that we provide a jobs training program for the 1 
million young people across the country who don't have any money, no 
job, no employment, many of them are out of school.
  Let's consider a job training program for our young people as a part 
of the economic stimulus package.

                          ____________________
```
