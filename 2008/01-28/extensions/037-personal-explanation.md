**Measures:** H.R. 3524

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. STEVE COHEN

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, January 28, 2008

  Mr. COHEN. Madam Speaker, I was in the Chamber when votes were cast 
on H.R. 3524, the HOPE VI Improvement and Reauthorization Act of 2007.

to cast a vote of ``aye'' on the final passage of H.R. 3524.
```
