# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 31, 2008

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 31, 2008

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business, it adjourn until 11 a.m. tomorrow, 
Thursday, January 31, and that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the leaders be reserved for their use later in 
the day, and that the majority leader be recognized.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
