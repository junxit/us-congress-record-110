# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
               MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under authority of the order of the Senate of June 20, 2008, the 
Secretary of the Senate, on June 20, 2008, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives, 
delivered by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announcing that the 
House has passed the following bill, in which it requests the 
concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 6304. An act to amend the Foreign Intelligence 
     Surveillance Act of 1978 to establish a procedure for 
     authorizing certain acquisitions of foreign intelligence, and 
     for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
