                   AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 5020. Mr. ENSIGN (for himself, Mr. Sununu, Mr. Domenici, 
     Mr. Roberts, Ms. Collins, Mr. Hatch, Mr. Enzi, and Mrs. Dole) 
     submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment 
     SA 4983 proposed by Mr. Reid (for Mr. Dodd (for himself and 
     Mr. Shelby)) to the bill H.R. 3221, moving the United States

     innovative new technologies, reducing carbon emissions,

     infrastructure, and to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to provide tax incentives for the production of 
     renewable energy and energy conservation; which was ordered 
     to lie on the table.

     3221, supra; which was ordered to lie on the table.
