# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, DECEMBER 23, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

```
          RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, DECEMBER 23, 2008, AT 11 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Tuesday, December 23, 2008, at 11 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10:01 and 4 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, December 23, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
