# RECESS UNTIL 9 A.M., TUESDAY, AUGUST 19, 2008

```
             RECESS UNTIL 9 A.M., TUESDAY, AUGUST 19, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Tuesday, August 19, 2008, at 9 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10:00 and 32 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, August 19, 2008, at 9 a.m.
```
