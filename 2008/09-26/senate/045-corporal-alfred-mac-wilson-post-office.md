# CORPORAL ALFRED MAC WILSON POST OFFICE

```
                 CORPORAL ALFRED MAC WILSON POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 6437) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 200 North Texas Avenue in Odessa, Texas, as 
the ``Corporal Alfred Mac Wilson Post Office,'' was ordered to a third
```
