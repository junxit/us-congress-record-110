# CPL. JOHN P. SIGSBEE POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 5975

```
                    CPL. JOHN P. SIGSBEE POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 5975) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 101 West Main Street in Waterville, New York, 
as the ``Cpl. John P. Sigsbee Post Office,'' was ordered to a third
```
