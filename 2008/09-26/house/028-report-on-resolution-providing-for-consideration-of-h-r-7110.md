# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 7110, JOB CREATION AND UNEMPLOYMENT RELIEF ACT OF 2008

**Measures:** H.Res. 1507, H.R. 7110

```
[[Page H10050]]
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 7110, JOB 
              CREATION AND UNEMPLOYMENT RELIEF ACT OF 2008

  Mr. WELCH of Vermont, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 110-891) on the resolution (H. Res. 1507)

appropriations for job creation and preservation, infrastructure 
investment, and economic and energy assistance for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 2009, and for other purposes, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
