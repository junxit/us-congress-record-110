# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Tauscher, Ellen O. (T000057) [D-CA]

```
                      HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

  Mrs. TAUSCHER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns on this legislative day, it adjourn to meet at 10 a.m. 
tomorrow; and further, that when the House adjourns on that legislative 
day, it adjourn to meet at 1 p.m. on Sunday, September 28.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Altmire). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentlewoman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
