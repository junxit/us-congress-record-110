# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, President, St. Luke Institute, Silver 
Spring, Maryland, offered the following prayer:
  Gracious God, in this season, from what was lifeless in death, we now 
see the bursting forth of new life. Thus we enter a great time of hope. 
As we begin this day and the days ahead, may we open ourselves to You, 
drink freely of Your new life, and step forward in confidence and 
trust.
  We thank You for these great gifts that You share with us. We trust 
You to lead us into tomorrow. And we pray this, as we pray all things, 
in Your holy name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
