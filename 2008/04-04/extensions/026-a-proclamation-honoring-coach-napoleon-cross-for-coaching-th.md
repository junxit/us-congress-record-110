# A PROCLAMATION HONORING COACH NAPOLEON CROSS FOR COACHING THE CHILLICOTHE HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' BASKETBALL TEAM TO WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION II STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

```
     A PROCLAMATION HONORING COACH NAPOLEON CROSS FOR COACHING THE 
  CHILLICOTHE HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' BASKETBALL TEAM TO WINNING THE BOYS' 
               DIVISION II STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 4, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Coach Napoleon Cross showed hard work and dedication to the 
sport of basketball; and
  Whereas, Napoleon Cross was a leader and mentor for the Chillicothe 
High School Boys' Basketball Team; and
  Whereas, Coach Cross has been a role model for sportsmanship on and 
off of the court; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, That along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Coach Napoleon Cross 
for leading the Chillicothe High School Boys' Basketball Team to 
winning the Boys' Division II State Basketball Championship. We 
recognize the tremendous hard work and leadership he has demonstrated 
during the 2007-2008 Basketball season.

                          ____________________
```
