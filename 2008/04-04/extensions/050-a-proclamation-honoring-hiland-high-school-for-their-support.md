# A PROCLAMATION HONORING HILAND HIGH SCHOOL FOR THEIR SUPPORT OF THE HILAND HIGH SCHOOL'S GIRLS' BASKETBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
  A PROCLAMATION HONORING HILAND HIGH SCHOOL FOR THEIR SUPPORT OF THE 
              HILAND HIGH SCHOOL'S GIRLS' BASKETBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 4, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Hiland High School has displayed incredible dedication to 
creating well-rounded students; and
  Whereas, the Hiland High School has been supportive of their 
athletes; and
  Whereas, the Hiland High School has broadened the abilities and 
skills of their athletes in the sport of basketball; and
  Whereas, the Hiland High School has always promoted sportsmanship on 
and off of the court; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, That along with their friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate the Hiland High School 
on supporting their girls' basketball team in winning the Girls' 
Division IV State Basketball Championship. We recognize the tremendous 
amount of support they have given to their athletes.

                          ____________________
```
