# A PROCLAMATION HONORING STUART BEVERLY FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION II STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
 A PROCLAMATION HONORING STUART BEVERLY FOR WINNING THE BOYS' DIVISION 
                    II STATE BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 4, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Stuart Beverly showed hard work and dedication to the sport 
of basketball; and
  Whereas, Stuart Beverly was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Stuart Beverly always displayed sportsmanship on and off of 
the court; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, That along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Stuart Beverly on 
winning the Boys' Division II State Basketball Championship. We 
recognize the tremendous hard work and sportsmanship he has 
demonstrated during the 2007-2008 basketball season.

                          ____________________
```
