# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for August 29, 2008

```
[[Page 17961]]

                     SENATE--Friday, August 29, 2008

               (Legislative day of Friday, August 1, 2008)
  The Senate met at 2 and 17 seconds p.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the President pro tempore (Mr. 
Byrd).

                          ____________________



                RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 2, 2008

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate 
stands in recess until Tuesday, September 2, in the year of our Lord 
2008, at 12 noon meridian.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 2 and 50 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, September 2, 2008, at 12 noon.
```
