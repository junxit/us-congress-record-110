# RECOGNIZING KELSEY TEMPLE

```
                       RECOGNIZING KELSEY TEMPLE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. KENNY MARCHANT

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 22, 2008

  Mr. MARCHANT. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kelsey Temple 
on achieving the Girl Scout Gold Award. Receiving the Gold Award is a 
testament to Miss Temple's leadership, citizenship, and service to her 
community.
  For her Gold Award project, Kelsey Temple educated elementary aged 
children about bicycle safety. Miss Temple stressed the importance of 
wearing a helmet, learning and obeying traffic rules regarding 
cyclists, and bicycle maintenance.
  The Girl Scouts of America promotes a positive influence for young 
women of today. I am honored to represent Kelsey Temple in earning the 
highest award bestowed in Girl Scouts. I commend her commitment and 
dedication for the betterment of her life, her community, and her 
country.

                          ____________________
```
