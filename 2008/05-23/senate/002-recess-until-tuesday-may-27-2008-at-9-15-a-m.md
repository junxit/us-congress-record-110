# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, MAY 27, 2008, AT 9:15 A.M.

```
            RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, MAY 27, 2008, AT 9:15 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 9:15 a.m., on Tuesday, May 27, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 29 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, May 27, 2008, at 9:15 a.m.
```
