# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, DECEMBER 19, 2008, AT 10 A.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, DECEMBER 19, 2008, AT 10 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 10 a.m. on Friday, December 19, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 50 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Friday, December 19, 2008, at 10 a.m.
```
