# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
WAIVING A REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII WITH RESPECT TO 
CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS REPORTED FROM THE COMMITTEE ON RULES
Committee on Rules: Granted, by a non-record vote, a rule waiving clause 
6(a) of rule XIII (requiring a two-thirds vote to consider a rule on the 
same day it is reported from the Rules Committee) against certain 
resolutions reported from the Rules Committee. The rule applies the waiver 
to any resolution reported by the Rules Committee on the legislative day of 
September 28, 2008 or September 29, 2008.
F
```
