# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for October 30, 2008

```
[[Page 24065]]

                   SENATE--Thursday, October 30, 2008

           (Legislative day of Wednesday, September 17, 2008)
  The Senate met at 9:15 a.m., on the expiration of the recess, and was 
called to order by the Acting President pro tempore (Mr. Whitehouse).

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                 Washington, DC, October 30, 2008.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, Section 3, of the Standing 
     Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Sheldon 
     Whitehouse, a Senator from the State of Rhode Island, to 
     perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



             RECESS UNTIL 10 A.M., MONDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 10 a.m., Monday, November 3, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:15 and 8 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Monday, November 3, 2008, at 10 a.m.
```
