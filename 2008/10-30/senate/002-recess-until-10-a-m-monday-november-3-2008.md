# RECESS UNTIL 10 A.M., MONDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2008

```
             RECESS UNTIL 10 A.M., MONDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 10 a.m., Monday, November 3, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:15 and 8 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Monday, November 3, 2008, at 10 a.m.
```
