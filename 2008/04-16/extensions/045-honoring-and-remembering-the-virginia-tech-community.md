# HONORING AND REMEMBERING THE VIRGINIA TECH COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
          HONORING AND REMEMBERING THE VIRGINIA TECH COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 16, 2008

  Mr. WITTMAN. Madam Speaker, today we remember and honor the students 
and faculty who lost their lives a year ago at Virginia Tech. The 
entire community has made a valiant effort to move forward while still 
honoring those who were lost and injured on that fateful day. As a 
proud alumnus of Virginia Tech this day has very personal meaning to my 
family and me. My thoughts and prayers are with the families of the 
victims and the entire Hokie family today.

                          ____________________
```
