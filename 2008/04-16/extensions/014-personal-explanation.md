# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Rothman, Steven R. (R000462) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2634, H.R. 5715

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. STEVEN R. ROTHMAN

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 16, 2008

  Mr. ROTHMAN. Madam Speaker, on Thursday, April 16, I was unable to be 
present in the Capitol and missed rollcall votes Nos. 192 through 195. 
Had I been present, I would have voted in the following manner:
  On rollcall votes Nos. 192 and 193, procedural votes on H.R. 2634, 
the Jubilee Act for Responsible Lending and Expanded Debt Cancellation 
of 2007, I would have voted ``aye.'' On rollcall votes Nos. 194 and 
195, procedural votes on H.R. 5715, Ensuring Continued Access to 
Student Loans Act, I would have voted ``aye.''
```
