# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                         Offered By Ms. Waters

       The amendment to be offered by Representative Waters, or a 
     designee, to H.R. 5818, the Neighborhood Stabilization Act of 
     2008, does not contain any congressional earmarks, limited 
     tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits, as defined in 
     clause 9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of rule XXI.
```
