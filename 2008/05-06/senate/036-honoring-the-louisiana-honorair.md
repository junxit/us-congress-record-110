# HONORING THE LOUISIANA HONORAIR

```
                    HONORING THE LOUISIANA HONORAIR

● Mr. VITTER. Mr. President, I wish to acknowledge and honor a 
very special group, the Louisiana HonorAir. Louisiana HonorAir is a 
not-for-profit group that flies as many as 200 World War II veterans a 
year up to Washington, DC, free of charge. On May 10, 2008, a group of 
103 veterans will reach Washington as part of this very special 
program.
```
