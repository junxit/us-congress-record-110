# PERMISSION TO CONSIDER AS ADOPTED MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1207, H.R. 6169, H.R. 6513

```
     PERMISSION TO CONSIDER AS ADOPTED MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES

  Mr. HOYER. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the motions to 
suspend the rules relating to the following measures be considered as 
adopted in the form considered by the House on Tuesday, September 9, 
2008: House Resolution 1207, H.R. 6169, and H.R. 6513.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request from the 
gentleman from Maryland?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, sundry motions to 
reconsider are laid on the table.
  There was no objection.
```
