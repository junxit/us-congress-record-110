# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House reports that on February 25, 
2008 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills:

       H.R. 1216. To direct the Secretary of Transportation to 
     issue regulations to reduce the incidence of child injury and 
     death occurring inside or outside of light motor vehicles, 
     and for other purposes.
       H.R. 5270. To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to 
     extend the funding and expenditure authority of the Airport 
     and Airway Trust Fund, and for other purposes.
```
