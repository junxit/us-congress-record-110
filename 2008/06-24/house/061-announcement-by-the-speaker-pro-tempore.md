# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings

Concurrent Resolution 379; approval of the Journal; and motions to 
suspend the rules with regard to H.R. 6327 and H.R. 6346.
```
