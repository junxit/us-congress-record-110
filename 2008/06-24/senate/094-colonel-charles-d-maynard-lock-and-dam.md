# COLONEL CHARLES D. MAYNARD LOCK AND DAM

**Measures:** H.R. 781

```
                COLONEL CHARLES D. MAYNARD LOCK AND DAM

  The bill (H.R. 781) to redesignate Lock and Dam No. 5 of the 
McClellan-Kerr Arkansas River Navigation System near Redfield, 
Arkansas, authorized by the Rivers and Harbors Act approved July 24, 
1946, as the ``Colonel Charles D. Maynard Lock and Dam,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On Page S6081, June 24, 2008, the Record reads: . . . Rivers and 
Harbors Act approved July 24, 2019 . . .
  
  The online Record has been corrected to read: . . . Rivers and 
Harbors Act approved July 24, 1946 . . .
```
