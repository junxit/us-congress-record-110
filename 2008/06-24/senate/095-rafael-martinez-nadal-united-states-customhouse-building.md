# RAFAEL MARTINEZ NADAL UNITED STATES CUSTOMHOUSE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1019

```
        RAFAEL MARTINEZ NADAL UNITED STATES CUSTOMHOUSE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1019) to designate the United States customhouse 
building located at 31 Gonzalez Clemente Avenue in Mayaguez, Puerto 
Rico, as the ``Rafael Martinez Nadal United States Customhouse 
Building,'' was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third 
time, and passed.
```
