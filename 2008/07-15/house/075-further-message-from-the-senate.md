# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate having proceeded to reconsider the bill (H.R. 
6331) ``An Act to amend titles XVIII and XIX of the Social Security Act 
to extend expiring provisions under the Medicare Program, to improve 
beneficiary access to preventive and mental health services, to enhance 
low-income benefit programs, and to maintain access to care in rural 
areas, including pharmacy access, and for other purposes'', returned by 
the President of the United States with his objections, to the House, 
in which it originated, and passed by the House on reconsideration of 
the same, it was
  Resolved, That the said bill pass, two-thirds of the Senators present 
having voted in the affirmative.

```
