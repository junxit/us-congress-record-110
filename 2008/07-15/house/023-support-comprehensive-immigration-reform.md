# SUPPORT COMPREHENSIVE IMMIGRATION REFORM

**Speaking:** Baca, Joe (B001234) [D-CA]

```
                              {time}  1015
                SUPPORT COMPREHENSIVE IMMIGRATION REFORM

  (Mr. BACA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. BACA. Mr. Speaker, I speak on behalf of immigrants. In the midst 
of our energy crisis, gas prices, and housing crisis in the United 
States, there are those who continue to positively contribute to our 
economy. Immigrants throughout history have come to this Nation with a 
hunger for success in the American dream, to provide for a family, and 
have their kids obtain an education. And many of them are working two 
to three different jobs contributing to our country at stores, 
restaurants, and gas stations.
  Immigrants often live near their establishments, are avid sponsors 
for local Little League, soccer, schools, and churches. Our country has 
always welcomed immigrants. Let's remember that we need comprehensive 
immigration reform for those who positively contribute to our local 
communities.
  I urge my colleagues to support comprehensive immigration reform.




                          ____________________
```
