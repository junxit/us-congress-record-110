# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period of morning business for up to 1 hour, 
with the time equally divided between the two leaders or their 
designees and with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each and with the Republicans controlling the first 30 minutes.
  The Senator from Georgia is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
