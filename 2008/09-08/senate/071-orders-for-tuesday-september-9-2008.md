# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 9, 2008

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

**Measures:** S. 3001

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 9, 2008

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
tomorrow, Tuesday, September 9; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and that there be a period of morning business for up 
to 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, 
with the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half and 
the majority controlling the second half; that following morning 
business, the Senate resume consideration of the motion to proceed to 
S. 3001, the Department of Defense authorization bill; that the Senate 
recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly caucus 
luncheons to meet; and that time during any recess, adjournment, or 
morning business count postcloture.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
