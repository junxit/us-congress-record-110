# RESOLUTIONS DISCHARGED AND THE CALENDAR

```
                RESOLUTIONS DISCHARGED AND THE CALENDAR

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Judiciary 
Committee be discharged and the Senate now proceed to the en bloc 
consideration of the following Senate resolutions: S. Res. 647, S. Res. 
649. I further ask that the Senate then proceed to Calendar No. 922, S. 
Res. 620, and Calendar No. 923, S. Res. 622, all en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senate proceeded to consider the resolutions en bloc.
  Mrs. BOXER. I ask unanimous consent that the resolutions be agreed 
to, the preambles be agreed to, and the motions to reconsider be laid 
upon the table en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
