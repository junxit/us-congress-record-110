# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

```
                      HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

  Ms. JACKSON-LEE of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 10:30 a.m. 
tomorrow for morning-hour debate; and further, that when the House 
adjourns on Wednesday, September 10, it adjourn to meet at 11 a.m. on 
Thursday, September 11.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
