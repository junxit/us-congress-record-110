# MAYOR WILLIAM ``BILL'' SANDBERG POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
          MAYOR WILLIAM ``BILL'' SANDBERG POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6229) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2523 7th Avenue East in North Saint Paul, 
Minnesota, as the ``Mayor William `Bill' Sandberg Post Office 
Building,'' was ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
