# KENNETH PETER ZEBROWSKI POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
              KENNETH PETER ZEBROWSKI POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6199) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 245 North Main Street in New City, New York, 
as the ``Kenneth Peter Zebrowski Post Office Building,'' was ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
