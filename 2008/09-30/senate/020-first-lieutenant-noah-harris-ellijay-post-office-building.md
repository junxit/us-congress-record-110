# FIRST LIEUTENANT NOAH HARRIS ELLIJAY POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
       FIRST LIEUTENANT NOAH HARRIS ELLIJAY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6847) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 801 Industrial Boulevard in Ellijay, Georgia,
```
