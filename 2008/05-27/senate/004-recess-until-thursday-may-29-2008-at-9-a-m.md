# RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, MAY 29, 2008, AT 9 A.M.

```
             RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, MAY 29, 2008, AT 9 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Thursday, May 29, 2008, at 9 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:15 and 31 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Thursday, May 29, 2008, at 9 a.m.
```
