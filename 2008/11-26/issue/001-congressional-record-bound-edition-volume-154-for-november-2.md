# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for November 26, 2008

```
[[Page 24439]]


                  SENATE--Wednesday, November 26, 2008

            (Legislative day of Thursday, November 20, 2008)
  The Senate met at 10 and 14 seconds a.m. on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Richard J. Durbin, a 
Senator from the State of Illinois.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:
                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                Washington, DC, November 26, 2008.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Richard L. Durbin, a Senator from the State of Illinois, to 
     perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. DURBIN thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



            RECESS UNTIL 2 P.M., SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 29, 2008

  Mr. DURBIN. Under the previous order, the Senate stands in recess 
until Saturday, November 29, 2008, at 2 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 40 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Saturday, November 29, 2008, at 2 p.m.
```
