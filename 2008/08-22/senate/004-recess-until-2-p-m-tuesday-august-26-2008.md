# RECESS UNTIL 2 P.M., TUESDAY, AUGUST 26, 2008

```
             RECESS UNTIL 2 P.M., TUESDAY, AUGUST 26, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Tuesday, August 26, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate at 10:00 and 30 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, August 26, 2008, at 2 p.m.
```
