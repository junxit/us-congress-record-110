# PRIVATE FIRST CLASS DAVID H. SHARRETT II POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5483

```
     PRIVATE FIRST CLASS DAVID H. SHARRETT II POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5483) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal service located at 10449 White Granite Drive in Oakton, 
Virginia, as the ``Private First class David H. Sharrett II Post Office 
Building,'' was ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and
```
