# RECOGNIZING SHARAYAH SLAUGHTER

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                     RECOGNIZING SHARAYAH SLAUGHTER

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Sharayah 
Slaughter, an intern in my Rapid City, SD, office, for all of the hard 
work she has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over 
the past several weeks.
  Sharayah is currently a student at Douglas High School in Box Elder, 
SD. She is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out 
of her internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Sharayah 
for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.●
```
