# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3430

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

**Measures:** S. 3430

```
[[Page S8017]]
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3430

  Ms. CANTWELL. Mr. President, I understand that S. 3430, introduced 
earlier today by Senator Coburn, is at the desk, and I ask for its 
first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the title of the bill for 
the first time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3430) to provide for the investigation of 
     certain unsolved civil rights crimes, and for other purposes.

  Ms. CANTWELL. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading and 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read the second time on the next legislative day.
```
