# RECOGNIZING MICHAEL MERRY

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       RECOGNIZING MICHAEL MERRY

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Michael Merry, an 
intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Michael is a graduate of Washington High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently he is attending the University of South Dakota, where he is 
majoring in finance and economics. He is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Michael 
for all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
