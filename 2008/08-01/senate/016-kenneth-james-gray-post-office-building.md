# KENNETH JAMES GRAY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 6061

```
                KENNETH JAMES GRAY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6061) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal service located at 219 East Main Street in West Frankfort, 
Illinois, as the ``Kenneth James Gray Post Office Building,'' was 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
