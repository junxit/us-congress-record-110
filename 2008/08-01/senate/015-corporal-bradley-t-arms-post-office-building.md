# CORPORAL BRADLEY T. ARMS POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
             CORPORAL BRADLEY T. ARMS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5631) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal service located at 1155 Seminole Trail in Charlottesville, 
Virginia, as the ``Corporal Bradley T. Arms Post Office Building,'' was
```
