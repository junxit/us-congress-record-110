# RECOGNIZING JAMIE LOFTUS

```
                        RECOGNIZING JAMIE LOFTUS

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Jamie Loftus, an 
intern in my Rapid City, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.

majoring in accounting. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to 
getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jamie 
for all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in 
the years to come.●
```
