# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3221

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we will be in a period of morning business, 
following my statement and that of the Republican leader, with Senators 
allowed to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the times equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders. The majority will 
control the first half, the Republicans the final half.
  Then we will begin again the consideration of H.R. 3221, the housing 
legislation. The first vote today will be at 2:15 this afternoon on a 
motion to invoke cloture on the substitute amendment to H.R. 3221.
```
