# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 9, 2008

**Speaking:** Dodd, Christopher J. (D000388) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.R. 3221

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 9, 2008

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Wednesday, April 9; that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for use later 
in the day, the Senate then proceed to a period of morning business for 
up to 60 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each and the time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees, with the Republicans controlling the first 
half and the majority controlling the final half; that following 
morning business, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 3221, and 
that all time during any morning business, recess, or adjournment of 
the Senate count postcloture.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
