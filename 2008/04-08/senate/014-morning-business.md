# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will not be a period of morning business for 60 minutes, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, the time equally 
divided between the two leaders or their designees, with the majority 
controlling the first half of the time and Republicans controlling the 
second half.
  The Senator from Oklahoma is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
