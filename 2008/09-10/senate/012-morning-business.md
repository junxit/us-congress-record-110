# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 1 
hour, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with 
the time equally divided between the two leaders or their designees, 
with the majority controlling the first half of the time and the 
Republicans the final half.
  The assistant majority leader is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
