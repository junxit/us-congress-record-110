# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate agrees to the amendments of the House to the bill (S. 
2403) ``An Act to designate the new Federal Courthouse, located in the 
700 block of East Broad Street, Richmond, Virginia, as the ``Spottswood 
W. Robinson III and Robert R. Merhige, Jr. Federal Courthouse.''.
```
