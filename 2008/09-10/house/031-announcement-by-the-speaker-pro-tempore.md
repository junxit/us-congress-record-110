# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 1419, H.R. 1527, S. 2617

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on questions previously postponed. Votes will be taken in 
the following order: ordering the previous question on H. Res. 1419, by 
the yeas and nays; adoption of H. Res. 1419, if ordered; motions to 
suspend the rules on H.R. 1527 and Senate bill 2617, by the yeas and 
nays.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Remaining electronic votes will be conducted as 5-minute votes.

                          ____________________
```
