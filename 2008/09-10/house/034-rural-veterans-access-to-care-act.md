  The title was amended so as to read: ``A bill to amend title 38, 
United States Code, to direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to 
conduct a pilot program to permit certain highly rural veterans 
enrolled in the health system of the Department of Veterans Affairs to 
receive covered health services through providers other than those of 
the Department.''.
