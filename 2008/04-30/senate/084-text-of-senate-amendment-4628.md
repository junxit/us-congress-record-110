# Text of Senate Amendment 4628

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
  SA 4628. Mr. REID proposed an amendment to amendment SA 4627 proposed

States Code, to authorize appropriations for the Federal Aviation

safety and capacity, to provide stable funding for the national
```
