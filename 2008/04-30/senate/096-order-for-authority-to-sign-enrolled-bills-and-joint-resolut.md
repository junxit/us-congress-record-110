# ORDER FOR AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
    ORDER FOR AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader be authorized to sign 
duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
```
