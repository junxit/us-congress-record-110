# ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, JULY 26, 2008

```
                   ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, JULY 26, 2008

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9 a.m. 
tomorrow, Saturday, July 26; that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the 
motion to concur with respect to H.R. 3221, the Foreclosure Prevention 
Act, under the provisions of a previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
