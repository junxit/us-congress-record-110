# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:


                                     House of Representatives,

                                     Washington, DC, May 16, 2008.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, The Capitol,
     House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, I have the honor to transmit a sealed 
     envelope received from the White House on May 16, 2008, at 
     3:03 p.m. and said to contain a message from the President 
     whereby he submits a copy of a notice filed earlier with the 
     Federal Register continuing the emergency with respect to 
     Burma first declared in Executive Order 13407 of May 20, 
     1997.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
                                               Lorraine C. Miller,
                                               Clerk of the House.




                          ____________________
```
