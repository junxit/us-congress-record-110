# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 170

  At the request of Mr. Ensign, the name of the Senator from New Mexico 
(Mr. Domenici) was added as a cosponsor of S. 170, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the excise tax on telephone and 
other communications services.

(Mr. Obama) was added as a cosponsor of S. 573, a bill to amend the

stroke, and other cardiovascular diseases in women.
