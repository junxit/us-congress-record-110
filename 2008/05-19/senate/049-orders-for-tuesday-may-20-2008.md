# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 20, 2008

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI]

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 20, 2008

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 
a.m., Tuesday, May 20; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then proceed to a period for the transaction 
of morning business until 12 noon, with Senators permitted to speak for 
up to 10 minutes each, with the first hour equally divided and 
controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with the 
Republicans controlling the first 30 minutes and the majority 
controlling the next 30 minutes; that following morning business, the 
Senate proceed to executive session under the previous order. I further 
ask unanimous consent that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 
p.m. to allow for the weekly caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
