# PERMISSION TO ADJOURN UPON DISSOLUTION OF SECRET SESSION

**Speaking:** Reyes, Silvestre (R000170) [D-TX]

```
                              {time}  1930
        PERMISSION TO ADJOURN UPON DISSOLUTION OF SECRET SESSION

  Mr. REYES. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
secret session of the House is dissolved pursuant to the previous order 
of the House, the House stand adjourned.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
