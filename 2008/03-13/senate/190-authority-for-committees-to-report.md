# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO REPORT

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                   AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO REPORT

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, everyone has worked long and hard tonight. I 
appreciate everyone's patience and cooperation.
  I ask unanimous consent that notwithstanding the Senate's recess, 
committees be authorized to report legislative and executive matters on 
March 27 from 10 a.m. to 12 noon.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
