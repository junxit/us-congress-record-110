# ORDER FOR READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

```
           ORDER FOR READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

  Mr. SANDERS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding the resolution of the Senate of January 24, 1901, the 
traditional reading of Washington's Farewell Address take place on 
Monday, February 25, 2008, at a time to be determined by the majority 
leader, in consultation with the Republican leader.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
