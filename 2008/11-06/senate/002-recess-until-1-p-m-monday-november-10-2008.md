# RECESS UNTIL 1 P.M., MONDAY, NOVEMBER 10, 2008

```

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate stands in 
recess until Monday, November 10, 2008, at 1 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 8 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Monday, November 10, 2008, at 1 p.m.
```
