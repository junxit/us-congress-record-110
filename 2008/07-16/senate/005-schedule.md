# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, there will be a 
period of morning business for up to 1 hour, with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each. The majority will control the first 30 
minutes, Republicans will control the second 30 minutes. Following 
morning business, the Senate will resume consideration of S. 2731, the 
Global AIDS bill.
  There should be rollcall votes during the day. Senator Biden told me 
last night they expect to finish the bill today. So I hope that, in 
fact, is the case.
```
