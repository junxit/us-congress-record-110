# RECOGNIZING JON GARREY

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         RECOGNIZING JON GARREY

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Jon Garrey, an 
intern in my Sioux Falls, SD office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Jon is a graduate of West Central High School in Hartford, SD. 
Currently he is attending Grinnell College, where he plans to major in 
political science. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to 
getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jon for 
all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
