# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to a period for the transaction of morning business for 1 hour, 
with Senators allowed to speak for up to 10 minutes each, and the time 
be equally divided between the two sides.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
