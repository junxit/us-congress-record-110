# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3268

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 3268

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3268

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that S. 3268 is at the desk and 
due for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The leader is correct. The clerk 
will read the title of the bill for the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3268) to amend the Commodity Exchange Act to 
     prevent excessive price speculation with respect to energy 
     commodities, and for other purposes.

  Mr. REID. I object to any further proceedings with respect to the 
bill.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard. The bill will 
be placed on the calendar.
```
