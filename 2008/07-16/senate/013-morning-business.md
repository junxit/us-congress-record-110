# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period of morning business for up to 1 hour, 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, and 
the time equally divided between the two leaders or their designees, 
with the majority controlling the first half of the time.
```
