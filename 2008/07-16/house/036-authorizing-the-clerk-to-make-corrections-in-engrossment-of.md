**Measures:** H.R. 5959

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 5959, 
          INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2009
```
