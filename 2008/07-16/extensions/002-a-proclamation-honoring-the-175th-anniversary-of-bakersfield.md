# A PROCLAMATION HONORING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF BAKERSFIELD PRESBYTERIAN CHURCH

```
     A PROCLAMATION HONORING THE 175TH ANNIVERSARY OF BAKERSFIELD 
                          PRESBYTERIAN CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 16, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
   Whereas, Bakersfield Presbyterian Church is celebrating its 175th 
anniversary; and
   Whereas, the congregation of Bakersfield Presbyterian continue to be 
active, enthusiastic members of our community; and
   Whereas, Bakersfield Presbyterian received a congratulatory plaque 
from the Presbyterian Church General Assembly for their 175 years; now, 
therefore, be it
   Resolved that along with the residents of the 18th Congressional 
District, I commend Bakersfield Presbyterian Church and its 
congregation for their unwavering commitment, dedication and 
contributions to their community and country in recognition of their 
175 years.

                          ____________________
```
