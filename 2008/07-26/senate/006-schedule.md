# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the time until 11 
will be equally divided and controlled by the two leaders or their 
designees. At 11 a.m., the Senate will proceed to two stacked rollcall 
votes. The first vote will be on the motion to concur with respect to 
H.R. 3221, the housing reform legislation. The second vote will be on 
the motion to invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to S. 3186, the 
Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program, LIHEAP. The last 20 minutes 
prior to the 11 o'clock vote, it is my understanding, and I direct this 
to the Chair, has been reserved for Senator McConnell and me. I have 
the last 10 minutes; is that right?
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The majority leader is correct.
```
