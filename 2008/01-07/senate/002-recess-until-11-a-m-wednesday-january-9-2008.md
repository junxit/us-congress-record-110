# RECESS UNTIL 11 A.M. WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 9, 2008

```
            RECESS UNTIL 11 A.M. WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 9, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Wednesday, January 9, 2008, at 11 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:00 and 53 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Wednesday, January 9, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
