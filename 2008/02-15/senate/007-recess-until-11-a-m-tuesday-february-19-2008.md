# RECESS UNTIL 11 A.M., TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 19, 2008

```
            RECESS UNTIL 11 A.M., TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 19, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 11 a.m. on Tuesday, February 19, 2008.
   Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 o'clock a.m. and 35 seconds, recessed 
until Tuesday, February 19, 2008, at 11 a.m.
```
