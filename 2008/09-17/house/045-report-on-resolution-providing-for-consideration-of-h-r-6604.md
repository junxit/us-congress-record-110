```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6604, 
     COMMODITY MARKETS TRANSPARENCY AND ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2008

report (Rept. No. 110-859) on the resolution (H. Res. 1449) providing
```
