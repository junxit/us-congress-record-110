# FORMER VICE PRESIDENT PROTECTION ACT OF 2008

**Measures:** H.R. 5938

```
              FORMER VICE PRESIDENT PROTECTION ACT OF 2008

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the question on suspending the rules and 
concurring in the Senate amendments to the bill, H.R. 5938.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Virginia (Mr. Scott) that the House suspend the rules 
and concur in the Senate amendments to the bill, H.R. 5938.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the Senate amendments were concurred in.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
