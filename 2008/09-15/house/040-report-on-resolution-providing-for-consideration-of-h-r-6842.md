# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6842, NATIONAL CAPITAL SECURITY AND SAFETY ACT

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6842, NATIONAL 
                    CAPITAL SECURITY AND SAFETY ACT

  Mr. McGOVERN, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-852) on the resolution (H. Res. 1434) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 6842) to require the District of 
Columbia to revise its laws regarding the use and possession of 
firearms as necessary to comply with the requirements of the decision 
of the Supreme Court in the case of District of Columbia v. Heller, in 
a manner that protects the security interests of the Federal government 
and the people who work in, reside in, or visit the District of 
Columbia and does not undermine the efforts of law enforcement, 
homeland security, and military officials to protect the Nation's 
capital from crime and terrorism, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
