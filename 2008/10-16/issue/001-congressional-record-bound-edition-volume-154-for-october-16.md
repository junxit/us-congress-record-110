# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for October 16, 2008

```
[[Page 24061]]


                   SENATE--Thursday, October 16, 2008

           (Legislative day of Wednesday, September 17, 2008)
  The Senate met at 10 and 2 seconds a.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Jack Reed, a Senator 
from the State of Rhode Island.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                 Washington, DC, October 16, 2008.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, section 3, of the Standing 
     Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Jack 
     Reed, a Senator from the State of Rhode Island, to perform 
     the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. REED thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro tempore.

                          ____________________



               RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, OCTOBER 20, AT 3 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Monday, October 20, 2008, at 3 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 30 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Monday, October 20, 2008, at 3 p.m.
```
