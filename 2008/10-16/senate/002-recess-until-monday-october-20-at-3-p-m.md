# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, OCTOBER 20, AT 3 P.M.

```
               RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, OCTOBER 20, AT 3 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Monday, October 20, 2008, at 3 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 30 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Monday, October 20, 2008, at 3 p.m.
```
