# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 13, 2008

**Speaking:** Klobuchar, Amy (K000367) [D-MN]

**Measures:** S. 2284

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 13, 2008

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
tomorrow, Tuesday, May 13; that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 2284, flood 
insurance, as under the previous order.
  I further ask that the Senate recess from 12:30 until 2:15 p.m. to 
allow for the weekly caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
