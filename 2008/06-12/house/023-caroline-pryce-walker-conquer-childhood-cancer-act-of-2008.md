                              {time}  1224

  So (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the rules were suspended and 
the bill, as amended, was passed.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title was amended so as to read: ``A bill to amend the Public 
Health Service Act to advance medical research and treatments into 
pediatric cancers, ensure patients and families have access to 
information regarding pediatric cancers and current treatments for such 
cancers, establish a national childhood cancer registry, and promote 
public awareness of pediatric cancer.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
