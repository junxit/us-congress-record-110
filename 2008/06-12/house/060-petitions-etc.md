# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the

       269. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Ms. Josette Lee, a 
     citizen of Rhinebeck, New York, relative to petitioning the 
     Congress of the United States for action regarding proposed 
     cuts in Medicare payments for physicians and other health 
     care professionals; jointly to the Committees on Ways and 
     Means and Energy and Commerce.
```
