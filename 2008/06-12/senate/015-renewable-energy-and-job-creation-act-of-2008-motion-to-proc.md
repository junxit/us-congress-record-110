# RENEWABLE ENERGY AND JOB CREATION ACT OF 2008--MOTION TO PROCEED

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

**Measures:** H.R. 6049

```
    RENEWABLE ENERGY AND JOB CREATION ACT OF 2008--MOTION TO PROCEED

                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I move to proceed to Calendar No. 767, 
H.R. 6049, and I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

  We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the provisions of 
rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, hereby move to bring to 
a close debate on the motion to proceed to Calendar No. 767, H.R. 6049, 
the Renewable Energy and Job Creation Act of 2008.
```
