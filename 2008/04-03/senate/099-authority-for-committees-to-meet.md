  I. Bills: S.2136, Helping Families Save Their Homes in Bankruptcy Act 
of 2007, (Durbin, Schumer, Whitehouse, Biden, Feinstein); S.2133, Home 
Owners ``Mortgage and Equity Savings Act'', (Specter, Coleman); S.2041,

Grant Program Act, (Kohl, Kennedy, Durbin, Biden, Cardin, Leahy,
