# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, APRIL 4, 2008

**Speaking:** Dodd, Christopher J. (D000388) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.R. 3221

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, APRIL 4, 2008

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9 a.m. tomorrow, 
April 4; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 3221, as under 
the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
