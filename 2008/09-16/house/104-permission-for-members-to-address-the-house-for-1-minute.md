# PERMISSION FOR MEMBERS TO ADDRESS THE HOUSE FOR 1 MINUTE

**Speaking:** Pence, Mike (P000587) [R-IN]

```
        PERMISSION FOR MEMBERS TO ADDRESS THE HOUSE FOR 1 MINUTE

  Mr. PENCE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that every Member who 
has not spoken be allowed to address the House for 1 minute.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Recognition for requests to address the 
House for 1 minute rests in the discretion of the Chair. The 
gentleman's request on behalf of others will not be entertained.

                          ____________________
```
