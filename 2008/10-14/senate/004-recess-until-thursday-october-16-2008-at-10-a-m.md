# RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, OCTOBER 16, 2008, AT 10 A.M.

```
          RECESS UNTIL THURSDAY, OCTOBER 16, 2008, AT 10 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Thursday, October 16, 2008, at 10 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:30 and 18 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Thursday, October 16, 2008, at 10 a.m.
```
