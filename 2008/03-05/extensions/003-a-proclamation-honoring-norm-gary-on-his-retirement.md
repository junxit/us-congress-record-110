# A PROCLAMATION HONORING NORM GARY ON HIS RETIREMENT

```
          A PROCLAMATION HONORING NORM GARY ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 5, 2008

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Mr. Gary has demonstrated values of hard work and service 
throughout his life, always maintaining a positive outlook; and
  Whereas, Mr. Gary is recognized for 30 years of dedication to the 
Hocking County community; and
  Whereas, Mr. Gary has impacted the lives of many while teaching 
residents skills that have helped them obtain employment; Now, 
therefore, be it
  Resolved, That along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I thank Norm Gary for his 30 years of 
service. We recognize the tremendous impact he has had in his community 
and in the lives of all those people he has touched.

                          ____________________
```
