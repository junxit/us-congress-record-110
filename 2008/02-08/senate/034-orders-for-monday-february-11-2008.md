# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 11, 2008

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2248

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 11, 2008

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 2 p.m., 
Monday, February 11; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and that the Senate then resume consideration of S. 2248, the 
Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act, as under the previous order. 
Finally, I ask unanimous consent that the Record remain open until 2 
p.m. today to allow Senators to introduce bills and submit statements.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
