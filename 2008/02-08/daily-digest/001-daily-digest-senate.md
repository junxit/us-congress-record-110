helicopters, and the resolution was then agreed to.

Inaugural Ceremonies.

68, authorizing the use of the rotunda of the Capitol by the Joint 
Congressional Committee on Inaugural Ceremonies.
  Pages S820-21
Recognizing 50th Anniversary of NARAS: Committee on the Judiciary was 
discharged from further consideration of H. Con. Res. 273, recognizing 
the 50th Anniversary of the National Academy of Recording Arts & 
Sciences, and the resolution was then agreed to.
