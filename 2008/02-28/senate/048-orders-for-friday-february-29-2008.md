# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 29, 2008

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 29, 2008

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
tomorrow, Friday, February 29; that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved 
for their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume 
consideration of the motion to proceed to H.R. 3221, the legislative 
vehicle to be used for the Foreclosure Prevention Act of 2008.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
