# House of Representatives

```
[[Page H1157]]

House of Representatives

  The House met at 10 a.m.
  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God, You reveal Yourself to people through Your creation and 
even more so in Your word.
  By Your grace, open the minds and hearts of Your people, especially 
leaders in government, business, and homes, that they may receive and 
take unto themselves Your good news, which can create peace and 
reconciliation in them and they in turn may bring peace, hope, and 
stability to this Nation and thereby to the entire world.
  Because You are doing this, to You be all honor, glory, and 
thanksgiving, now and forever.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
