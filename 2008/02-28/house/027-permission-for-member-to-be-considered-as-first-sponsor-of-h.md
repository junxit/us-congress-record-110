# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 840

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 840

  Ms. MOORE of Wisconsin. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I 
may hereafter be considered the first sponsor of H.R. 840, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Julia Carson of Indiana, for 
the purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant 
to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Cohen). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentlewoman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.
```
