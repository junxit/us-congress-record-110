# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.Res. 24

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       239. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     State of Hawaii, relative to Senate Resolution No. 24 urging 
     the President of the United States to agree to an economy-
     wide reduction in its greenhouse gas emissions and to commit 
     the United States to a binding international treaty that 
     would result in a significant and rapid global reduction in 
     atmospheric greenhouse gas concentration; to the Committee on 
     Foreign Affairs.
```
