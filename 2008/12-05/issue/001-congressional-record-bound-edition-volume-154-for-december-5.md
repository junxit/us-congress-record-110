# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 154 for December 5, 2008

```
[[Page 24442]]

                    SENATE--Friday, December 5, 2008

            (Legislative day of Thursday, November 20, 2008)
  The Senate met at 11 a.m., on the expiration of the recess, and was 
called to order by the Honorable Benjamin L. Cardin, a Senator from the 
State of Maryland.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The assistant legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                 Washington, DC, December 5, 2008.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Benjamin L. Cardin, a Senator from the State of Maryland, to 
     perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. CARDIN thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



            RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, DECEMBER 8, 2008, AT 3 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 3 p.m., on Monday, December 8, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 28 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Monday, December 8, 2008, at 3 p.m.
```
