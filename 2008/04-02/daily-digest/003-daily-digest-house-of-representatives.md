Tom Lantos and Henry J. Hyde United States Global Leadership Against 
HIV/AIDS, Tuberculosis, and Malaria Reauthorization Act of 2008: The 
House passed H.R. 5501, to authorize appropriations for fiscal years 
2009 through 2013 to provide assistance to foreign countries to combat 
HIV/AIDS, tuberculosis, and malaria, by a recorded vote of 308 ayes to 
116 noes, Roll No. 158.
  Pages H1904-42
  Rejected the Ryan (WI) motion to recommit the bill to the Committee 
on Foreign Affairs with instructions to report the same back to the 
House forthwith with amendments, by a yea-and-nay vote of 175 yeas to 
248 nays, Roll No. 157.
Page H1939-41
Accepted:
  Blumenauer amendment (No. 1 printed in H. Rept. 110-562) that adds 
safe drinking water to nutrition and income security on the list of 
programs for which direct linkages are encouraged;
Pages H1934-35
  Fortenberry amendment (No. 2 printed in H. Rept. 110-562) that 
ensures that audits by the Inspector General include information on 
subcontractors;
Pages H1935-36
  McCollum amendment (No. 3 printed in H. Rept. 110-562) that adds 
Malawi, Swaziland and Lesotho to the list of new ``focus'' countries 
proposed to be added in the bill; and
Pages H1936-37
  Carson amendment (No. 4 printed in H. Rept. 110-562) that directs the 
Coordinator of United States Government Activities to Combat HIV/AIDS 
Globally and the Administrator of the United States Agency for 
International Development to expand their plan for strengthening health 
systems of host countries by allowing for postsecondary educational 
institutions, particularly in Africa, to collaborate with United States 
postsecondary educational institutions and specifically historically 
black colleges and universities (by a recorded vote of 415 ayes to 10 
noes, Roll No. 156).
Pages H1937-39
  Agreed that the Clerk be authorized to make technical and conforming 
changes to reflect the actions of the House.
