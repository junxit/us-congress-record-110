# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
LONG-TERM BUDGET OUTLOOK
Committee on the Budget: Committee concluded a hearing to examine the 
long-term federal budget outlook, focusing on action that is needed to 
avoid the possibility of a serious economic disruption in the future, 
after receiving testimony from David M. Walker, Comptroller General, 
Government Accountability Office.

[[Page D56]]


NOMINATION
Committee on Finance: Committee concluded a hearing to examine the 
nomination of Douglas H. Shulman, of the District of Columbia, to be 
Commissioner of Internal Revenue, Department of the Treasury, after the 
nominee testified and answered questions in his own behalf.
```
