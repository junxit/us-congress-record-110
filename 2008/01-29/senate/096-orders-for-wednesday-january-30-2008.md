# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 30, 2008

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 30, 2008

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand

[[Page S458]]

adjourned until 10 a.m. tomorrow, Wednesday, January 30; that after the 
prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and that there then be a 
period for the transaction of morning business with Senators permitted 
to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
