# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period of morning business for up to 1 hour, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time 
equally divided, with the Republican leader controlling the first half 
and the majority leader controlling the final half.
  The Senator from Florida is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
