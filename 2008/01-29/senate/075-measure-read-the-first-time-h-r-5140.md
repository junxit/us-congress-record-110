# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 5140

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 5140

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 5140 is now here and 
at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5140) to provide economic stimulus through 
     recovery rebates to individuals, incentives for business 
     investment, and an increase in conforming and FHA loan 
     limits.

  Mr. REID. I ask, Mr. President, that further work on this matter be 
terminated now, so I object to its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
