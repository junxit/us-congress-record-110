# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2557

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2557

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I understand that S. 2557, introduced 
earlier today by Senator Reid, is at the desk, and I ask for its first 
reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title for 
the first time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2557) to extend the Protect America Act of 2007 
     until July 1, 2009.

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. I now ask for its second reading, and I object to my 
own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
