# Text of Senate Amendment 3939

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

```
  SA 3939. Mr. BOND submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 3911 proposed by Mr. Rockefeller (for himself and Mr. 
Bond) to the bill S. 2248, to amend the Foreign Intelligence 
Surveillance Act of 1978, to modernize and streamline the provisions of 
that Act, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       On page 63, strike lines 7 through 9, and insert the 
     following:
       (D) in paragraph (3), as redesignated by subparagraph (B) 
     of this paragraph--
       (i) by inserting ``and'' at the end;
       (ii) by striking subparagraphs (B) and (C) and inserting 
     the following:
       ``(B) the premises or property to be searched is or is 
     about to be owned, used, possessed by, or is in transit to or 
     from a foreign power or an agent of a foreign power;''.
                                 ______
```
