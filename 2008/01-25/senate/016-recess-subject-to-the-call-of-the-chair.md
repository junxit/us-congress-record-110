# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:04 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 12:07 p.m., when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer (Ms. Klobuchar).
  Mr. BOND. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
