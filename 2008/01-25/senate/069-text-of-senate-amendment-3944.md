# Text of Senate Amendment 3944

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

```
  SA 3944. Mr. BOND submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 3911 proposed by Mr. Rockefeller (for himself and Mr. 
Bond) to the bill S. 2248, to amend the Foreign Intelligence 
Surveillance Act of 1978, to modernize and streamline the provisions of 
that Act, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       Strike section 103 and insert the following:

     SEC. 103. CLARIFICATION OF THE DEFINITION OF UNITED STATES 
                   PERSON.

       Subsection (i) of section 101 of the Foreign Intelligence 
     Surveillance Act of 1978 (50 U.S.C. 1801) is amended by 
     striking ``, as defined in subsection (a)(1), (2), or (3)''.
                                 ______
```
