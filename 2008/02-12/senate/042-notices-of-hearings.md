# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM], Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


               COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND NATURAL RESOURCES

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that an oversight hearing has 
been scheduled before the Senate Committee on Energy and Natural 
Resources. The hearing will be held on Tuesday, February 26, 2008, at 
10 a.m., in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of this oversight hearing is to receive testimony on U.S. 
oil inventory policies, including the Strategic Petroleum Reserve 
policies.
  Because of the limited time available for the hearing, witnesses may 
testify by invitation only. However, those wishing to submit written 
testimony for the hearing record may do so by sending it to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, United States Senate, 
Washington, DC 20510-6150, or by e-mail to

  For further information, please contact Tara Billingsley at (202) 
224-4756 or Rosemarie Calabro at (202) 224-5039.


               COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND NATURAL RESOURCES

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the
```
