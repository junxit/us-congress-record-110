# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following the remarks that I will make and 
perhaps the Republican leader will make, we are going to resume 
consideration of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act and will 
immediately proceed to a series of rollcall votes in relation to the 
remaining amendments and cloture on the bill. The managers are working 
on a couple of amendments to see if they can be accepted by voice vote. 
But there could be as many as nine rollcall votes. If we have not 
completed voting on these items prior to the caucus time, we will 
resume votes after the recess.
```
