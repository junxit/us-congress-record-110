# SENATE RESOLUTION 448--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS FOR THE 110TH CONGRESS

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 448--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS FOR THE 110TH 
                                CONGRESS

   Mr. McCONNELL submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 448

       Resolved, That the following be the minority membership on 
     the following committee for the remainder of the 110th 
     Congress, or until their successors are appointed:
       Committee on Foreign Relations: Mr. Lugar, Mr. Hagel, Mr. 
     Coleman, Mr. Corker, Mr. Voinovich, Ms. Murkowski, Mr. 
     DeMint, Mr. Isakson, Mr. Vitter, Mr. Barrasso.

                          ____________________
```
