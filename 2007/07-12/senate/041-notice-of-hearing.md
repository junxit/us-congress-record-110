# NOTICE OF HEARING

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               Committee on Energy and Natural Resources

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a legislative hearing has 
been scheduled before the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
  The hearing will be held on Thursday, July 19, 2007, at 9:30 a.m. in 
room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of this hearing is to receive testimony on S. 1634, a 
bill to implement further the Act approving the Covenant to Establish a 
Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands in Political Union with 
the United States of America, and for other purposes.
  Because of the limited time available for the hearing, witnesses may 
testify by invitation only. However, those wishing to submit written 
testimony for the hearing record should send two copies of their 
testimony to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, United 
States Senate, Washington, DC 20510-6150, or by email to 
<span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="5b3929322f353204293237373e293a1b3e353e293c2275283e353a2f3e753c342d">[email protected]</span>.
  For further information, please contact Allen Stayman at (202) 224-
7865 or Britni Rillera at (202) 224-1219.
```
