# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 13, 2007

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 1585

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 13, 2007

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9 a.m., 
Friday, July 13; that on Friday, following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired and the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later 
in the day; that the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 1585 and 
conduct debate on the Dorgan amendment, No. 2135, as amended, until 
9:30 a.m.; that the Senate proceed to vote in relation to the amendment 
at 9:30 without further intervening action or debate; and that of the 
time available until then, Senators Dorgan and Sununu each control 10 
minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. BROWN. If there is no further business today, I now ask unanimous 
consent the Senate stand adjourned under the previous order.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 7:06 p.m., adjourned until 
Friday, July 13, 2007, at 9 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
