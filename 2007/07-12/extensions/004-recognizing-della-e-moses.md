# RECOGNIZING DELLA E. MOSES

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING DELLA E. MOSES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 11, 2007

  Mr. GRAVES. Madam Speaker, I proudly ask you to join me in 
recognizing Della E. Moses of Elmo, Missouri. Della celebrated her 99th 
birthday on July 10, 2007 and it is my privilege to offer her my 
warmest regards on achieving this important milestone. Della is a fine 
citizen of Missouri and the Elmo community. It is an honor to represent 
Della in the

[[Page E1494]]

United States Congress, and I wish her all the best on this birthday 
and many more in the future.

                          ____________________
```
