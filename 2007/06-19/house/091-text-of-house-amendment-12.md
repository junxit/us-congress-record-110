# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** Tancredo, Thomas G. (T000458) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2764

```
H.R. 2764

                        Offered By: Mr. Tancredo

       Amendment No. 12: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be expended in violation of

[[Page H6737]]

     section 243(d) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 
     U.S.C. 1253(d)) (relating to discontinuing granting visas to 
     nationals of countries denying or delaying accepting aliens 
     removed from the United States).
```
