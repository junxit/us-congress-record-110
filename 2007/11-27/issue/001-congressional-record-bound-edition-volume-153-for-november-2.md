# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 153 for November 27, 2007

```
[[Page 31936]]

                   SENATE--Tuesday, November 27, 2007

             (Legislative day of Friday, November 16, 2007)
  The Senate met at 9 o'clock and 9 seconds a.m., on the expiration of 
the recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Jack Reed, a 
Senator from the State of Rhode Island.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                Washington, DC, November 27, 2007.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Jack Reed, a Senator from the State of Rhode Island, to 
     perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. REED thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro tempore.

                          ____________________



          RECESS UNTIL 9:30 A.M., THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 29, 2007

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Thursday, November 29, 2007, at 9:30 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9 o'clock and 36 seconds a.m., recessed 
until Thursday, November 29, 2007, at 9:30 a.m.
```
