# RECESS UNTIL 9:30 A.M., THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 29, 2007

```
          RECESS UNTIL 9:30 A.M., THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 29, 2007

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Thursday, November 29, 2007, at 9:30 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9 o'clock and 36 seconds a.m., recessed 
until Thursday, November 29, 2007, at 9:30 a.m.
```
