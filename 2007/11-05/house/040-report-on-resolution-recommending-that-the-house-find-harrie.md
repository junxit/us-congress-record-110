# REPORT ON RESOLUTION RECOMMENDING THAT THE HOUSE FIND HARRIET MIERS AND JOSHUA BOLTEN IN CONTEMPT OF CONGRESS

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION RECOMMENDING THAT THE HOUSE FIND HARRIET MIERS AND 
                 JOSHUA BOLTEN IN CONTEMPT OF CONGRESS

  Mr. CONYERS from the Committee on the Judiciary, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 110-423) on the resolution recommending 
that the House of Representatives find Harriet Miers and Joshua Bolten, 
Chief of Staff, White House, in contempt of Congress for refusal to 
comply with subpoenas duly issued by the Committee on the Judiciary, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
