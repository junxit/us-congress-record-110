# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1808, H.R. 2779

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House reports that on November 1, 
2007 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills.

       H.R. 1808. To designate the Department of Veterans Affairs 
     Medical Center in Augusta, Georgia, as the ``Charlie Norwood 
     Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center.''
       H.R. 2779. To recognize the Navy UDT-SEAL Museum in Fort 
     Pierce, Florida, as the official national museum of Navy 
     SEALS and their predecessors.

                          ____________________
```
