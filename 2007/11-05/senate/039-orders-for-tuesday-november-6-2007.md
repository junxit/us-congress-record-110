# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2007

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2419

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2007

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it

[[Page S13938]]

stand adjourned until 10 a.m. Tuesday, November 6; that on Tuesday, 
following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two 
leaders reserved for their use later in the day; that there then be a 
period for the transaction of morning business for 60 minutes with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each and the 
time equally divided and controlled between the leaders or their 
designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half and the 
majority controlling the final half; that at the close of morning 
business, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 2419; further, that 
on Tuesday, the Senate stand in recess from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for 
the respective party conference meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
