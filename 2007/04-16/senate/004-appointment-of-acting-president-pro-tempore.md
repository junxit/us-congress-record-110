# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read a communication to the 
Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                    Washington DC, April 16, 2007.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule 1, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Maria Cantwell, a Senator from the State of Washington, to 
     perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Ms. CANTWELL thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________
```
