# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE DUNCAN HUNTER, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
   COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE DUNCAN HUNTER, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Duncan Hunter, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, April 4, 2007.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to formally notify you, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I have been served with a judicial 
     subpoena for documents issued by the United States District 
     Court for the District of Columbia.
       After consulting with the Office of General Counsel, I will 
     make the determinations required by House Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,
                                                    Duncan Hunter,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```
