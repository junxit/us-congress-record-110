# RECOGNIZING DOMINICK ESTEBAN FOR ACHIEVING THE RANK OF EAGLE SCOUT

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
   RECOGNIZING DOMINICK ESTEBAN FOR ACHIEVING THE RANK OF EAGLE SCOUT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 16, 2007

  Mr. GRAVES. Madam Speaker, I proudly pause to recognize Dominick 
Esteban, a very special young man who has exemplified the finest 
qualities of citizenship and leadership by taking an active part in the 
Boy Scouts of America, Troop 214, and in earning the most prestigious 
award of Eagle Scout.
  Dominick has been very active with his troop, participating in many 
scout activities. Over the many years Dominick has been involved with 
scouting, he has not only earned numerous merit badges, but also the 
respect of his family, peers, and community.
  Madam Speaker, I proudly ask you to join me in commending Dominick 
Esteban for his

[[Page E736]]

accomplishments with the Boy Scouts of America and for his efforts put 
forth in achieving the highest distinction of Eagle Scout.

                          ____________________
```
