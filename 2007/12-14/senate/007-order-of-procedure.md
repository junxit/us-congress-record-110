# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, the order now before the Senate indicates we 
are going to move to the FHA bill as soon as we finish the farm bill. 
One of the key players on the Republican side is not going to be 
available this afternoon.
  I would therefore ask unanimous consent that we go to the FHA bill 
before we do the farm bill. There is preliminary work on the farm bill 
to sort out germane and nongermane amendments anyway.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
