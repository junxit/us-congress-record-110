# CORRECTING THE ENROLLMENT OF THE BILL H.R. 1585

```
            CORRECTING THE ENROLLMENT OF THE BILL H.R. 1585

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to the consideration of H. Con. Res. 269, which the clerk will 
report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 269) directing the 
     Clerk of the House of Representatives to correct the 
     enrollment of the bill H.R. 1585.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the concurrent 
resolution is agreed to and the motion to reconsider is considered made 
and laid upon the table.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 269) was agreed to.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Alaska is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
