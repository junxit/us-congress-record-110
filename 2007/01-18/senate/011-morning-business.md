# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
first hour under the control of the majority leader or his designee and 
the second hour under the control of the Republican leader or his 
designee.
  The Senator from Colorado is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
