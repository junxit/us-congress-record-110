# The House met at 10 a.m. The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following prayer: The love of the Lord is perfect; it gives life to the soul. The word of the Lord can be trusted; it gives wisdom to all. The command of the Lord is clear; it gives light to the eye. Those who love their neighbors fulfill the law, for the whole law is summed up in the command to love. So the command of the Lord is clear. Let us embrace it with our whole heart both now and forever. Amen.
