# MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1348 AND H.R. 2080

```
          MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1348 AND H.R. 2080

  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I understand there are two bills at the 
desk, and I ask for their first reading, en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered. The clerk 
will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1348) to provide for comprehensive immigration 
     reform and for other purposes.
       A bill (H.R. 2080) to amend the District of Columbia Home 
     Rule Act to conform the District charter to revisions made by 
     the Council of the District of Columbia relating to public 
     education.

  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I ask for a second reading en bloc, and I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bills will be read for 
the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
