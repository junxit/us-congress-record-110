# TRIBUTE TO KATE MARTIN

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO KATE MARTIN

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I rise to recognize Kate Martin, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Kate is a graduate of Ellendale High School in Ellendale, ND. 
Currently she is attending the University of North Dakota, where she is 
majoring in marketing and is pursuing a minor in international 
business. She is also active in her sorority Kappa Alpha Theta. She is 
a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her 
internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Kate for 
all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the 
years to come.

                          ____________________
```
