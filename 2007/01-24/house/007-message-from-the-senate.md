# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 41, H.R. 475

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill and a concurrent 
resolution of the House of the following titles:
       H.R. 475. An act to revise the composition of the House of 
     Representatives Page Board to equalize the number of members 
     representing the majority and minority parties and to include 
     a member representing the parents of pages and a member 
     representing former pages, and for other purposes.
       H. Con. Res. 41. Concurrent resolution providing for a 
     conditional adjournment of the House of Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
