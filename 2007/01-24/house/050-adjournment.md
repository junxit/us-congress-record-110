# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Bartlett, Roscoe G. (B000208) [R-MD]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 41

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. BARTLETT of Maryland. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent 
Resolution 41, 110th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 41, 
110th Congress, the House stands adjourned until 2 p.m. on Monday, 
January 29, 2007.

Concurrent Resolution 41, the House adjourned until Monday, January 29, 
2007, at 2 p.m.
```
