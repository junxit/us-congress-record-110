     Housing, and Urban Affairs.

     Justice Programs, Department of Justice, transmitting, 
     pursuant to law, the report of a rule entitled ``Grants for 
     Correctional Facilities'' (RIN1121-AA41) received on January 
     23, 2007; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
