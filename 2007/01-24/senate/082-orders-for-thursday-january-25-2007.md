# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 25, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 25, 2007

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m., Thursday, January 
25; that on Thursday, following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 2, as provided 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
