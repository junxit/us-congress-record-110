# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 
minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the time equally divided and controlled

[[Page 27615]]

between the two leaders or their designees, with the Republicans 
controlling the first half and the majority controlling the final half.
  The Senator from Florida.

                          ____________________
```
