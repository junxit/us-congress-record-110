# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 19, 2007

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 3043

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 19, 2007

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m., 
Friday, October 19; that on that day, following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 
3043.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
