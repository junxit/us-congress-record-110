# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 2102, S. 2179, S. 2180, S. 2184, S. 2185, H.R. 3678

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bills were read the second time, and placed on the 
calendar:

       H.R. 2102. An act to maintain the free flow of information 
     to the public by providing conditions for the federally 
     compelled disclosure of information by certain persons 
     connected with the news media.
       H.R. 3678. An act to amend the Internet Tax Freedom Act to 
     extend the moratorium on certain taxes relating to the 
     Internet and to electronic commerce.
       S. 2179. A bill to authorize certain programs and 
     activities in the Forest Service, the Department of the 
     Interior, and the Department of Energy, and for other 
     purposes.
       S. 2180. A bill to authorize certain programs and 
     activities in the Department of the Interior, the Forest 
     Service, and the Department of Energy, and to amend the 
     Compact of Free Association Amendments Act of 2003, and for 
     other purposes.
       S. 2184. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to permanently allow penalty-free withdrawals from retirement 
     plans for individuals called to active duty for at least 179 
     days.
       S. 2185. A bill to permanently extend the current marginal 
     tax rates.

                          ____________________
```
