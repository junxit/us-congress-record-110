# SENATE RESOLUTION 98--PROVIDING FOR MEMBERS ON THE PART OF THE SENATE OF THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING AND THE JOINT COMMITTEE OF CONGRESS ON THE LIBRARY

**Measures:** S.Res. 98

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 98--PROVIDING FOR MEMBERS ON THE PART OF THE SENATE 
OF THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING AND THE JOINT COMMITTEE OF CONGRESS 
                             ON THE LIBRARY

  Mrs. FEINSTEIN submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 98

       Resolved, That the following named Members be, and they are 
     hereby, elected members of the following joint committees of 
     Congress:
       Joint Committee on Printing: Mrs. Feinstein, Mr. Inouye, 
     Mrs. Murray, Mr. Bennett, and Mr. Chambliss.
       Joint Committee of Congress on the Library: Mrs. Feinstein, 
     Mr. Dodd, Mr. Schumer, Mr. Bennett, and Mr. Stevens.

                          ____________________
```
