# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business for up to 60 minutes, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
first 30 minutes under the control of the majority and the second 30 
minutes under the control of the Republican leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Washington is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
