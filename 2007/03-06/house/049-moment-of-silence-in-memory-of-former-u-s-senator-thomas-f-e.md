# MOMENT OF SILENCE IN MEMORY OF FORMER U.S. SENATOR THOMAS F. EAGLETON

**Speaking:** Carnahan, Russ (C001060) [D-MO]

```
 MOMENT OF SILENCE IN MEMORY OF FORMER U.S. SENATOR THOMAS F. EAGLETON

  (Mr. CARNAHAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. CARNAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I want to advise and remind the Members of 
the passing of former U.S. Senator Thomas F. Eagleton of Missouri over

in his memory.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. All Members will rise.
```
