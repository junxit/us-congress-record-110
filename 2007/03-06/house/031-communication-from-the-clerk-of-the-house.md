# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 16, H.Con.Res. 47, S. 743

```
[[Page H2172]]
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                         House of Representatives,
                                    Washington, DC, March 5, 2007.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, U.S. House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, the Clerk received the following message 
     from the Secretary of the Senate on March 2, 2007, at 12:30 
     pm:
       That the Senate passed S. 743.
       That the Senate agreed to without amendment H. Con. Res. 
     47.
       That the Senate agreed to S. Con Res. 16.

     Appointments:
       British-American Interparliamentary Group
       North Atlantic Treaty Organization Parliamentary Assembly
       Canada-United States Interparliamentary Group
       Mexico-United States Interparliamentary Group
       With best wishes, I am,
           Sincerely,
                                               Lorraine C. Miller,
     Clerk of the House.

                          ____________________
```
