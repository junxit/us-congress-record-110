# COMMUNICATION FROM THE OFFICE OF THE SERGEANT AT ARMS

```
         COMMUNICATION FROM THE OFFICE OF THE SERGEANT AT ARMS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Don Kellaher, Assistant Sergeant at Arms, Office of 
the Sergeant at Arms, U.S. House of Representatives:

                                   Office of the Sergeant at Arms,


                                U.S. House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, March 19, 2007.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, U.S. House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to formally notify you, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I have been served with an 
     administrative subpoena for testimony issued by the Office of 
     Compliance of the U.S. House of Representatives.
       After consulting with the Office of General Counsel, I will 
     make the determinations required by House Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Don Kellaher,
     Assistant Sergeant at Arms.

                          ____________________
```
