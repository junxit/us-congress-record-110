# TRIBUTE TO GRAYSON LEE SHREVE

```
                     TRIBUTE TO GRAYSON LEE SHREVE

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. SHELLEY MOORE CAPITO

                            of west virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, December 6, 2007

  Mrs. CAPITO. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Grayson Lee 
Shreve of Buckhannon, WV, who recently attained the rank of Eagle Scout 
on October 23, 2007.
  Grayson is a member of Boy Scout Troop 106 and is sponsored by Sand 
Run Baptist Church in Buckhannon, WV. During his years in scouting, he 
has received 43 Merit Badges and achieved the rank of Life Scout. He 
was awarded the prestigious Order of the Arrow and the Arrow of Light. 
For his service project to obtain Eagle Scout, Grayson restored and 
constructed a screened picnic shelter for a local church.
  His many accomplishments demonstrate the high standards held by those 
who achieve Eagle Scout. Grayson has been a consistent Honor Roll 
student and has been a member of the Gifted Program since elementary 
school. He currently attends Buckhannon-Upshur High School where he is 
involved in BUMS, the school news channel.
  Grayson is truly representative of the Scout Oath, ``to do duty to 
God and country.'' I extend my congratulations to Grayson and his 
parents, Leslie Shreve II and Norma Hinkle. Upshur County is fortunate 
to have such an accomplished young leader.

                          ____________________
```
