# A PROCLAMATION HONORING COACH MIKE FELTON FOR COACHING THE GARAWAY GOLF TEAM TO WINNING THE DIVISION III STATE GOLF TOURNAMENT

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING COACH MIKE FELTON FOR COACHING THE GARAWAY GOLF 
         TEAM TO WINNING THE DIVISION III STATE GOLF TOURNAMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, December 6, 2007

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:

[[Page 33481]]

  Whereas, Coach Mike Felton showed hard work and dedication to the 
sport of golf; and
  Whereas, Mike Felton was a leader and mentor for the Garaway Golf 
Team; and
  Whereas, Coach Felton has been a role model for sportsmanship on and 
off of the greens: Now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Coach Mike Felton for 
leading the Garaway Golf Team to winning the Division III State Golf 
Tournament. We recognize the tremendous hard work and leadership he has 
demonstrated during the 2007 golf season.

                          ____________________
```
