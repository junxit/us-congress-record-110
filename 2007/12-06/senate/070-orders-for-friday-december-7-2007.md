# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, DECEMBER 7, 2007

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, DECEMBER 7, 2007

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it stand adjourned until 9 a.m., Friday, December 7; 
that on tomorrow, following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour deemed expired, the 
time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in the day, and 
the Senate then proceed to the House message on H.R. 6, as under a 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
