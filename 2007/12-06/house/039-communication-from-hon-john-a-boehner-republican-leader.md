       COMMUNICATION FROM HON. JOHN A. BOEHNER, REPUBLICAN LEADER

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

     The Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, U.S. Capitol,

     Commission.

       Mr. Daniel M. Slane of Ohio.
       These individuals have expressed interest in serving in 
     this capacity and I am pleased to fulfill their requests.
           Sincerely,
                                                  John A. Boehner,
                                                Republican Leader.
