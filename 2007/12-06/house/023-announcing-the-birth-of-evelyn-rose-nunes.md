# ANNOUNCING THE BIRTH OF EVELYN ROSE NUNES

```
               ANNOUNCING THE BIRTH OF EVELYN ROSE NUNES

  (Mr. DREIER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. DREIER. Mr. Speaker, I am very pleased to announce to the House 
that our distinguished colleague Devin Nunes and his wife, Elizabeth, 
are now the proud parents of 6 pound, 13 ounce Evelyn Rose Nunes, who 
was born at 3:31 yesterday morning. I think all of us want to extend 
hardy congratulations to them. I am happy to report that mother and 
baby are doing very well.

                          ____________________
```
