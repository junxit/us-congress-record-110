# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  A bill and a concurrent resolution of the Senate of the following 
titles were taken from the Speaker's table and, under the rule, 
referred as follows:

       S. 2106. An act to provide nationwide subpoena authority 
     for actions brought under the September 11 Victim 
     Compensation Fund of 2001; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       S. Con. Res. 45. Concurrent resolution commending the Ed 
     Block Courage Award Foundation for its work in aiding 
     children and families affected by child abuse, and 
     designating November 2007 as National Courage Month; to the 
     Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.
```
