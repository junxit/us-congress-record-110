# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3554

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3554

```
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3554

  Mr. KING of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, due to an error in my office, the name 
John Salazar was added to the bill H.R. 3554, and I would ask unanimous 
consent that his name be removed from H.R. 3554.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Iowa?
  There was no objection.
```
