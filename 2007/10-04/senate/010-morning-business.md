# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Nelson, Bill (N000032) [D-FL]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 
minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each and the time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees, with the Republicans controlling the first 
half and the majority controlling the final half.
  Mr. NELSON of Florida. I ask unanimous consent that the time I have 
used not be charged against the majority's time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  The Senator from Texas.

                          ____________________
```
