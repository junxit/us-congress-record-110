# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 800, EMPLOYEE FREE CHOICE ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 203, H.R. 800

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 800, EMPLOYEE 
                            FREE CHOICE ACT

  Ms. Sutton, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-26) on the resolution (H. Res. 203) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 800) to amend the National Labor 
Relations Act to establish an efficient system to enable employees to 
form, join, or assist labor organizations, to provide for mandatory 
injunctions for unfair labor practices during organizing efforts, and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
