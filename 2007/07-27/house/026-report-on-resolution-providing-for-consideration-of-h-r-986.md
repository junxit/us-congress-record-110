# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 986, EIGHTMILE WILD AND SCENIC RIVER ACT

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 986, EIGHTMILE 
                       WILD AND SCENIC RIVER ACT

  Ms. SLAUGHTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-264) on the resolution (H. Res. 580) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 986) to amend the Wild and Scenic 
Rivers Act to designate certain segments of the Eightmile River in the 
State of Connecticut as components of the National Wild and Scenic 
Rivers System, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
