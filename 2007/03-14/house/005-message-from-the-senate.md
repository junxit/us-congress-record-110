# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 1129

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 1129. An act to provide for the construction, 
     operation, and maintenance of an arterial road in St. Louis 
     County, Missouri.

  The message also announced that pursuant to section 276n of title 22, 
United States Code, as amended, the Chair, on behalf of the President 
pro tempore, appoints the following Senator as Chairman of the United 
States-China Interparliamentary Group conference during the One Hundred 
Tenth Congress:
  The Senator from Hawaii (Mr. Inouye).
  The message also announced that pursuant to section 276n of title 22, 
United States Code, as amended, the Chair, on behalf of the President 
pro tempore, appoints the following Senator as Vice Chairman of the 
United States-China Interparliamentary Group conference during the One 
Hundred Tenth Congress:
  The Senator from Alaska (Mr. Stevens).
  The message also announced that pursuant to section 276d-276g of 
title 22, United States Code, as amended, the Chair, on behalf of the 
Vice President, appoints the following Senator as Chairman of the 
Senate Delegation to the Canada-United States Interparliamentary Group 
conference during the One Hundred Tenth Congress:
  The Senator from Minnesota (Ms. Klobuchar).

                          ____________________
```
