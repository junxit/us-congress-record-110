# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JUNE 8, 2007, TO MONDAY, JUNE 11, 2007

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

```
HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JUNE 8, 2007, 
                        TO MONDAY, JUNE 11, 2007

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 4 p.m. tomorrow, and, further,

on Monday, June 11, for morning-hour debate.
```
