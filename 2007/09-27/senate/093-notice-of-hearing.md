# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Biden, Joseph R., Jr. (B000444) [D-DE]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                     committee on foreign relations

  Mr. BIDEN. Mr. President, today the Committee on Foreign Relations 
held a hearing to review the Convention on the Law of the Sea and the 
Agreement Relating to the Implementation of Part XI of the Convention 
(Treaty Doc. 103-39). The Committee heard testimony from 
representatives of the executive branch.
  On Thursday, October 4, 2007, at 9:30 a.m. in SD-419, the Committee 
will conduct another hearing on the Convention on the Law of the Sea. 
Witnesses from outside the government will present testimony. 
Interested parties who have not been invited to testify may submit 
written testimony until the close of business on October 5, 2007 by 
sending it electronically to <span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="e4888b97a4828b96818d838aca97818a859081ca838b92">[email protected]</span> or by faxing it to 
the Committee's Executive Clerk, Gail Coppage, at (202) 228-3612.

                          ____________________
```
