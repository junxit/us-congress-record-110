# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2007

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 1585

```
                 ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2007

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10:30 
a.m., Friday, September 28; that on Friday, following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders reserved for their 
use later in the day; that the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 
1585.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
