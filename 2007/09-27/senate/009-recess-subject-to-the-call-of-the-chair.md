# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess subject to the call of the Chair for 2 minutes so that 
we may bring in a distinguished visitor.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 6:12 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair, until 6:14 p.m. and reassembled when called 
to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Whitehouse).

                          ____________________
```
