# HONORING JUDGE RICHARD ARNOLD

```
                     HONORING JUDGE RICHARD ARNOLD

  (Mr. SNYDER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. SNYDER. Mr. Speaker, people in Arkansas who knew of Judge Richard 
Arnold admired and respected his great legal mind, his integrity, and 
his remarkable attributes as a human being. Everyone who personally 
knew him liked him. Not even those who disagreed with him found fault 
with his judicial demeanor nor his legal analysis.
  Now we have an opportunity to honor this great man. Tomorrow in 
Little Rock will be the formal dedication of the Richard Sheppard 
Arnold United States Courthouse, a wonderful new facility. Not only 
will this building be a great site for justice in central Arkansas, but 
it will be a lasting tribute to Judge Arnold. And on this day also we 
honor his wonderful wife, Kay Kelley Arnold, who will be in attendance 
at tomorrow's dedication.

                          ____________________
```
