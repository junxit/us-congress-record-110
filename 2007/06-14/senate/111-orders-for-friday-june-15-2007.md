# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 15, 2007

**Speaking:** Nelson, Bill (N000032) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 6

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 15, 2007

  Mr. NELSON of Florida. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 
9:30 a.m., Friday, June 15; that on Friday, following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of 
H.R. 6, comprehensive energy legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
