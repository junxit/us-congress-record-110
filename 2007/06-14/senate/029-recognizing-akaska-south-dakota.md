# RECOGNIZING AKASKA, SOUTH DAKOTA

```
                    RECOGNIZING AKASKA, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I wish to recognize Akaska, 
SD. The town of Akaska will celebrate the 100th anniversary of its 
founding this year.
  Since its beginning in 1907, Akaska has been a strong reflection of 
South Dakota's values and traditions. As they celebrate this milestone 
anniversary, I am confident that Akaska will continue to thrive and 
succeed for the next 100 years.
  I would like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Akaska on 
their anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
