# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, June 14, 2007, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 676. An act to provide that the Executive Director of 
     the Inter-American Development Bank or the Alternate 
     Executive Director of the Inter-American Development Bank may 
     serve on the Board of Directors of the Inter-American 
     Foundation.
       S. 1537. An act to authorize the transfer of certain funds 
     from the Senate Gift Shop Revolving Fund to the Senate 
     Employee Child Care Center.
```
