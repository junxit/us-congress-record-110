# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** Donnelly, Joe (D000607) [D-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2642

```
H.R. 2642

                        Offered By: Mr. Donnelly

       Amendment No. 12: Page 44, after line 22, insert the 
     following new section:
       Sec. __. Not later than 30 days after the date of the 
     enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Veterans Affairs 
     shall submit to Congress a report explaining what is being 
     done to implement the eight open recommendations made to the 
     Secretary by the Government Accountability Office for 
     improving the timeliness, accuracy and consistency of 
     disability claims processing by the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs, as recorded in a letter dated May 25, 2007, sent by 
     the Government Accountability Office to the President's 
     Commission on Care for America's Returning Wounded Warriors 
     (GAO-07-906R).
```
