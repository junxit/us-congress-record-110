# Text of House Amendment 14

**Speaking:** Pence, Mike (P000587) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2642

```
H.R. 2642

                         Offered By: Mr. Pence

       Amendment No. 14: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:
       Sec. 409. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act may be used for construction of a 
     structure or purchase of equipment for the purpose of 
     performing abortions.
```
