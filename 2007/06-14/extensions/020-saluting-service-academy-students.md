# SALUTING SERVICE ACADEMY STUDENTS

```
                   SALUTING SERVICE ACADEMY STUDENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM JOHNSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 14, 2007

  Mr. SAM JOHNSON of Texas. Madam Speaker, it is a tremendous honor to 
salute our soldiers, marines, sailors, and airmen of tomorrow--the 
service academy-bound students of the Third District of the Texas. This 
Congressional District is home to some of the best and the brightest 
young people. They are eager and ready to join the premier military 
force of the world. It is a privilege to send such fine young people on 
to our Nation's prestigious service academies.
  We lift them and their families up in prayer for their future service 
and sacrifices. I am so very proud of them. God bless them and God 
bless America. I salute them. The name of each appointee and hometown 
follows:
  United States Air Force Academy: Madison Schottleuther--McKinney; 
Taylor Soster--Allen; Grace St. Clair--Allen; Stephanie Stanford--
Plano; Drew Warwick--Plano; Michael Willcox--McKinney; Evan Wise--
Plano.
  United States Merchant Marine Academy: Bryce Doganer--Plano; F. Wade 
(Fred) Wheeler--Plano.
  United States Military Academy: Audrey Hansen--Plano; Kane Kimrey--
Rowlett; Anne Nagy--McKinney; Ryan Schubert--Plano; Tianyi Xin--Plano.
  United States Naval Academy: Erik DeSousa--Plano; Gregory Harrell--
Plano; Philip Niche--McKinney; Melanie Pribich--Plano; Ryan Szymaniak--
McKinney.

                          ____________________
```
