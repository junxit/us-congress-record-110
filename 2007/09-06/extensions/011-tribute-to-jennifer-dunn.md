# TRIBUTE TO JENNIFER DUNN

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JENNIFER DUNN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. SUE WILKINS MYRICK

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 6, 2007

  Mrs. MYRICK. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor the late Jennifer 
Dunn. Jennifer was a role model for those of us who were fortunate to 
serve in Congress with her. She blazed many paths in politics for other 
women to follow.
  Jennifer always gave of herself and was willing to reach out and help 
wherever it was needed.
  I am so thankful she was able to enjoy a happy marriage to Keith and 
spend time with her family these last few years. Our hearts go out to 
them in their loss. Please keep the family in your prayers.

                          ____________________
```
