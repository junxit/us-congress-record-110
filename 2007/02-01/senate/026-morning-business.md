# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until 
11:45 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each, with the first 30 minutes under the control of the 
Republicans and the second 30 minutes under the control of the 
majority.
  The Senator from Kentucky.
```
