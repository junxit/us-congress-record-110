# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. SALAZAR. On behalf of the majority leader, I ask unanimous 
consent the Senate now stand in recess subject to the call of the 
Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 2:29 p.m., recessed until 
3:26 p.m., and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Ms. Klobuchar).
  Mr. KENNEDY. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. KENNEDY. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
