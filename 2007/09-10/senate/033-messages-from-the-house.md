# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:05 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
agreed to the report of the committee on conference on the disagreeing 
votes of the two Houses on the amendment of the Senate to the bill 
(H.R. 2669) to provide for reconciliation pursuant to section 601 of 
the concurrent resolution on the budget for fiscal year 2008.
  The message also announced that the House passed the following bill, 
in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 2786. An act to reauthorize the programs for housing 
     assistance for Native Americans.
```
