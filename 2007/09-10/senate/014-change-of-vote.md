# CHANGE OF VOTE

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA]

```
                             CHANGE OF VOTE

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, on rollcall vote No. 320, I voted ``yea.'' 
It was my intention to vote ``nay.'' Therefore, I ask unanimous consent 
that I be permitted to change my vote since it will not affect the 
outcome of that vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
