# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 11, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3074

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 11, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. tomorrow 
morning, September 11; that on tomorrow, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders reserved for 
their use later in the day; that the Senate then resume consideration 
of H.R. 3074, the Transportation Appropriations Act; that the Senate 
stand in recess from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m. to accommodate the respective 
party conferences; further, that on Tuesday, in commemoration of the 
sixth anniversary of the September 11 attack, the Senate observe a 
moment of silence at 12 noon.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
