# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** McDermott, Jim (M000404) [D-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1908, H.R. 2669

```
[[Page E1853]]
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JIM McDERMOTT

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 10, 2007

  Mr. McDERMOTT. Madam Speaker, I was unable to vote on passage of the 
conference report on H.R. 2669. Were I able to, I would have voted 
``aye'' on this important bill.
  Furthermore, I was also unable to vote for final passage of H.R. 
1908. Were I able, I would have voted ``aye'' on this legislation, 
noting that while the bill is not perfect, the Congress must continue 
to consider legislation to reform our patent laws. I look forward to 
considering a more refined product when this bill is next before the 
House.
```
