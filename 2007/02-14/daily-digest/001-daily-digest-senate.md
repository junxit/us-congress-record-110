Morales Howard, to be United States District Judge for the Middle 
District of Florida; that debate run concurrently until 10:30 a.m., 
with the time equally divided and controlled between the Chairman and 
Ranking Member of the Committee on the Judiciary, or their designees; 
that at 10:30 a.m., Senate vote on the confirmations of the 
nominations.
