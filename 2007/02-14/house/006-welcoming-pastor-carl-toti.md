# WELCOMING PASTOR CARL TOTI

**Speaking:** Neugebauer, Randy (N000182) [R-TX]

```
                       WELCOMING PASTOR CARL TOTI

  (Mr. NEUGEBAUER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. NEUGEBAUER. Mr. Speaker, I am pleased to have Pastor Carl Toti 
with us today delivering the opening invocation for the United States 
House of Representatives.
  Pastor Toti currently serves as the senior pastor of Trinity Church 
in my hometown of Lubbock, Texas. He is a graduate of Rhema Bible 
Training Center. He has also earned a master's degree in theological 
studies from Vision University in Ramona, California.
  Just as Pastor Toti speaks to diverse audiences through his ministry, 
so does he today speak to a diverse audience here in our Congress. 
Although Members of this body come from different parts of the country 
and varying beliefs, I hope his words will unite and guide us all today 
as we conduct the business of the people of the United States of 
America.

                          ____________________
```
