# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 60 
minutes, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, 
and the time equally divided between the two sides.
```
