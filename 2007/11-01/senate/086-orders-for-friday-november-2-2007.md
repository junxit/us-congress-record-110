# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 2, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 2, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until tomorrow morning 
at 10 a.m.; that on Friday, following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and that

[[Page S13715]]

there then be a period for the transaction of morning business with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
