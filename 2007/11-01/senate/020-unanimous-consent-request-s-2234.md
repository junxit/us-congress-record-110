# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--S. 2234

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK], Baucus, Max (B000243) [D-MT]

**Measures:** S. 2234

```
                   UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--S. 2234

  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of S. 2234, a bill to extend the 
deduction for qualified tuition and related expenses and that the bill 
be read a third time and passed. I further ask that the bill then be 
held at the desk until the House companion arrives and that all after 
the enacting clause be stricken, the text of the Senate-passed bill be 
inserted, and the House bill, as amended, be read for a third time and 
passed.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. BAUCUS. Reserving the right to object, I might say to my good 
friend that this is another measure that will be considered in due 
course later this year. I must object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The Senator from Oklahoma.

                          ____________________
```
