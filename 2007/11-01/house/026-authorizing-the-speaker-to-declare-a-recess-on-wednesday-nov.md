# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 7, 2007, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY NICHOLAS SARKOZY, PRESIDENT OF THE FRENCH REPUBLIC

```
 AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 7, 
  2007, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY 
           NICHOLAS SARKOZY, PRESIDENT OF THE FRENCH REPUBLIC

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order at any time on Wednesday, November 7, 2007, for the Speaker to 
declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting His Excellency Nicholas Sarkozy, President 
of the French Republic.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Maryland?
  There was no objection.
```
