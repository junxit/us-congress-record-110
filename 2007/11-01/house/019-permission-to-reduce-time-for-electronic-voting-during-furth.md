# PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING FURTHER PROCEEDINGS TODAY

```
    PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING FURTHER 
                           PROCEEDINGS TODAY

  Mr. RAHALL. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, during further 
proceedings today in the House

[[Page 29004]]

and in the Committee of the Whole, the Chair be authorized to reduce to 
2 minutes the minimum time for electronic voting on any question that 
otherwise could be subjected to 5-minute voting under clause 8 or 9 of 
rule XX or under clause 6 of rule XVIII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from West Virginia?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
