# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 1808, H.R. 2779

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate had passed without amendment bills of the House of the 
following titles:

       H.R. 1808. An act to designate the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs Medical Center in Augusta, Georgia, as the ``Charlie 
     Norwood Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center''.
       H.R. 2779. An act to recognize the Navy UDT-SEAL Museum in 
     Fort Pierce, Florida, as the official national museum of Navy 
     SEALs and their predecessors.

                          ____________________
```
