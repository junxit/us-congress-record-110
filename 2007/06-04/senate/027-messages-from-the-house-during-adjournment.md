# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
               MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 2007, the 
Secretary of the Senate, on May 25, 2007, during the adjournment of the 
Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives announcing 
that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 2206. An act making emergency supplemental 
     appropriations and additional supplemental appropriations for 
     agricultural and other emergency assistance for the fiscal 
     year ending September 30, 2007, and for other purposes.

  Under the authority of the order of January 4, 2007, the enrolled 
bill was signed by the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd) during the 
adjournment of the Senate, on May 25, 2007.

                          ____________________
```
