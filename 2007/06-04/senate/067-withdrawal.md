# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
4, 2007, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       BRUCE P. JACKSON, OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, TO BE A 
     MEMBER OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE UNITED STATES 
     INSTITUTE OF PEACE FOR A TERM EXPIRING JANUARY 19, 2011, VICE 
     CHESTER A. CROCKER, TERM EXPIRED, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE 
     SENATE ON MARCH 12, 2007.
```
