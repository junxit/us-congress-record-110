# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** S. 1701

```
               MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 2007, the 
Secretary of the Senate, on July 13, 2007, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the House has passed the following bill, without 
amendment:

       S. 1701. An act to provide for the extension of 
     transitional medical assistance (TMA) and the abstinence 
     education program through the end of fiscal year 2007, and 
     for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
