# House of Representatives

```
[[Page H7779]]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

                        House of Representatives

  The House met at 4 p.m.
  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God, Your hardworking people deserve a just reward for their 
labor. In the workaday world of our society, most people look forward 
to some relaxation and enjoyment with family or friends over a weekend.
  Be with Members of Congress as they return to their families and 
their districts. Bless them with safe travel. May peace await them in 
their homes.
  Let them praise and thank You, Lord, as they worship with others who 
will renew them in faith and lift them up in prayer this weekend.
  Be Lord of the Sabbath to them both now and forever. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
