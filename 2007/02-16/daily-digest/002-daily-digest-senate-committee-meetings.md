PAYING FOR COLLEGE
Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions: Committee 
concluded a hearing to examine college affordability, focusing on 
higher education, higher costs and higher student debt, and the Higher 
Education Act and its amendments, after receiving testimony from Jon H. 
Oberg, former researcher, Department of Education, Rockville, Maryland; 
Suze Orman, CNBC, and Tamara Draut, Demos Institute, both of New York, 
New York; and Sandy Baum, Skidmore College, Washington, D.C., on behalf 
of the College Board.
