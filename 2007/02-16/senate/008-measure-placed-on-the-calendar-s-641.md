# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 641

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 641

```
                 MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 641

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that S. 641 is at the desk and 
due for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the bill by 
title for a second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 641) to express the sense of Congress that no 
     funds should be cut off or reduced for American troops in the 
     field which would result in undermining their safety or their 
     ability to complete their assigned missions.

  Mr. REID. I object to any further proceedings with respect to this 
bill at this time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard. The bill will 
be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
