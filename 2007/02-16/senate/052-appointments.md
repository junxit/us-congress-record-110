# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair announces, on behalf of the minority 
leader, pursuant to Public Law 105-83, the reappointment of the 
following Senator to serve as a member of the National Council on the 
Arts for a term of 2 years: The Senator from Utah (Mr. Bennett).
  The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, pursuant to Public Law 
94-304, as amended by Public Law 99-7, appoints the following Senators 
as members of the Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe 
(Helsinki) during the 110th Congress: the Senator from Oregon (Mr. 
Smith), the Senator from Georgia (Mr. Chambliss), the Senator from 
North Carolina (Mr. Burr), and the Senator from Kansas (Mr. Brownback).
```
