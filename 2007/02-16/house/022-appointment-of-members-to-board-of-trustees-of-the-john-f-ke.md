# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE JOHN F. KENNEDY CENTER FOR THE PERFORMING ARTS

```
  APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE JOHN F. KENNEDY 
                     CENTER FOR THE PERFORMING ARTS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to Section 2(a) of the National 
Cultural Center Act (20 U.S.C. 76h(a)), amended by Public Law 107-117, 
and the order of the House of January 4, 2007, the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of the following Members of the House to the 
Board of Trustees of the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing 
Arts:
  Mr. Kennedy, Rhode Island
  Ms. DeLauro, Connecticut
  Ms. Pryce, Ohio.
```
