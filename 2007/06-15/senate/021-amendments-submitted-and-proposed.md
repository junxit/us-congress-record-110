**Measures:** H.R. 6

```

       SA 1610. Mr. CARDIN (for himself, Ms. Mikulski, Mr. Dodd, 
     Mr. Kerry, Mr. Reed, Mr. Kennedy, Mr. Whitehouse, and Ms. 
     Snowe) proposed an amendment to amendment SA 1502 proposed by 
     Mr. Reid to the bill H.R. 6, to reduce our Nation's 
     dependency on foreign oil by investing in clean, renewable, 
     and alternative energy resources, promoting new emerging 
     energy technologies, developing greater efficiency, and 
     creating a Strategic Energy Efficiency and Renewables Reserve 
     to invest in alternative energy, and for other purposes.
```
