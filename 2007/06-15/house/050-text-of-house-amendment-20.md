# Text of House Amendment 20

**Speaking:** Harman, Jane (H000213) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2642

```
H.R. 2642

                         Offered By: Ms. Harman

       Amendment No. 20: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. 409. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to purchase light bulbs unless the light bulbs have 
     the ``ENERGY STAR'' designation.
```
