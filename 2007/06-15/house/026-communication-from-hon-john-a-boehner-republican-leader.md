# COMMUNICATION FROM HON. JOHN A. BOEHNER, REPUBLICAN LEADER

```
       COMMUNICATION FROM HON. JOHN A. BOEHNER, REPUBLICAN LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Hon. John A. Boehner, Republican Leader:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, June 14, 2007.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Pelosi: Pursuant to section 5(a) of the 
     Abraham Lincoln Bicentennial Commission Act (36 U.S.C. 101 
     note), I am pleased to re-appoint the Honorable Ray LaHood of 
     Illinois to the Abraham Lincoln Bicentennial Commission.
       Mr. LaHood has expressed interest in serving in this 
     capacity and I am pleased to fulfill his request.
           Sincerely,
                                                  John A. Boehner,
     Republican Leader.

                          ____________________
```
