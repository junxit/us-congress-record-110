# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** Musgrave, Marilyn N. (M001152) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2642

```
H.R. 2642

                       Offered By: Mrs. Musgrave

       Amendment No. 18: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available in this Act may be used for any action that is 
     related to or promotes the expansion of the boundaries or 
     size of the Pinon Canyon Maneuver Site in southeastern 
     Colorado.
```
