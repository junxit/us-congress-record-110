# MICHAEL W. SCHRAGG POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3446

```
                MICHAEL W. SCHRAGG POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3446) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 202 East Michigan Avenue in Marshall, 
Michigan, as the ``Michael W. Schragg Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
