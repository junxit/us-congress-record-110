# LOUISIANA ARMED SERVICES VETERANS POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 2089

```
             LOUISIANA ARMED SERVICES VETERANS POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 2089) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 701 Loyola Avenue in New Orleans, Louisiana, 
as the ``Louisiana Armed Services Veterans Post Office,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
