# CHARLES H. HENDRIX POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                CHARLES H. HENDRIX POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3518) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1430 South Highway 29 in Cantonment, Florida, 
as the ``Charles H. Hendrix Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
