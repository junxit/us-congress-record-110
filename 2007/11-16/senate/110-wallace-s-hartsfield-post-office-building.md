# WALLACE S. HARTSFIELD POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3572

```
               WALLACE S. HARTSFIELD POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3572) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 4320 Blue Parkway in Kansas City, Missouri, 
as the ``Wallace S. Hartsfield Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
