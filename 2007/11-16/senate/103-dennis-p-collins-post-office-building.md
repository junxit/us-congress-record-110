# DENNIS P. COLLINS POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3307

```
                 DENNIS P. COLLINS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3307) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 570 Broadway in Bayonne, New Jersey, as the 
``Dennis P. Collins Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
