# NATE DeTAMPLE POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                   NATE DeTAMPLE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3297) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 950 West Trenton Avenue in Morrisville, 
Pennsylvania, as the ``Nate DeTample Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
