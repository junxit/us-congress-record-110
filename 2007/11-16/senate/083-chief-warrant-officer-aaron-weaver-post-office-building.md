# CHIEF WARRANT OFFICER AARON WEAVER POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
        CHIEF WARRANT OFFICER AARON WEAVER POST OFFICE BUILDING

Postal Service located at 1400 Highway 41 North in Inverness, Florida, 
as the ``Chief Warrant Officer Aaron Weaver Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and
```
