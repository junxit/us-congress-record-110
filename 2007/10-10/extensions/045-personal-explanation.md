**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.Res. 32, H.R. 400

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 10, 2007

of H. Res. 32. I also missed rollcall vote No. 950 on passage of H.R. 
400, the War Profiteering Prevention Act of 2007. Had I been present, I
```
