# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN SULLIVAN

                              of oklahoma

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 10, 2007

  Mr. SULLIVAN. Madam Speaker, I rise to state for the record that I 
missed roll call vote 532 to H.R. 2764 taken on June 21, 2007. Had I 
been present for this vote, I would have voted aye.
  As an ardent supporter of the rights of the unborn, I stand in strong 
support of H. AMDT. 364, which would restore the President's emergency 
plan for AIDS relief authorization provision requiring 33 percent of 
HIV/AIDS prevention funding to be spent on abstinence and fidelity 
promotion programs.
```
