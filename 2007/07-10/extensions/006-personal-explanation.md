# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Sullivan, John (S001155) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 2764

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN SULLIVAN

                              of oklahoma

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 10, 2007

  Mr. SULLIVAN. Madam Speaker, I rise to state for the record that I 
missed rollcall vote 541 to H.R. 2764 taken on June 22, 2007. Had I 
been present for this vote, I would have voted ``aye.''
  I stand in strong support of H. Amdt. 390, which would prohibit the 
use of funds for travel by the Speaker of the House of Representatives 
to countries that are state sponsors of terrorism.
```
