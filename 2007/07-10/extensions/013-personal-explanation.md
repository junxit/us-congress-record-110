# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Sullivan, John (S001155) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 2764

```
[[Page E1474]]
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN SULLIVAN

                              of oklahoma

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 10, 2007

  Mr. SULLIVAN. Madam Speaker, I rise to state for the record that I 
missed rollcall vote 542 to H.R. 2764 taken on June 21, 2007. Had I 
been present for this vote, I would have voted ``nay.''
  As an ardent supporter of the rights of the unborn, I am strongly 
opposed to this legislation, which weakens existing Federal policies 
and laws on abortion.
```
