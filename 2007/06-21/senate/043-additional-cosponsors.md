                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 21

  At the request of Mr. Reid, the name of the Senator from New Mexico 
(Mr. Bingaman) was added as a cosponsor of S. 21, a bill to expand 
access to preventive health care services that help reduce unintended 
pregnancy, reduce abortions, and improve access to women's health care.

(Mr. Allard), the Senator from Mississippi (Mr. Cochran) and the

multiple and discriminatory taxes on electronic commerce permanent.
