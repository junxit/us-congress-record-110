# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 57, H.R. 692

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House reports that on June 20, 2007, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills.

       H.R. 57. To repeal certain sections of the Act of May 26, 
     1936, pertaining to the Virgin Islands.
       H.R. 692. To amend title 4, United States Code, to 
     authorize the Governor of a State, territory, or possession 
     of the United States to order that the National flag be flown 
     at half-staff in that State, territory, or possession in the 
     event of the death of a member of the Armed Forces from that 
     State, territory, or possession who dies while serving on 
     active duty.
```
