# Text of House Amendment 63

**Speaking:** Pence, Mike (P000587) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2764

```
H.R. 2764

                         Offered By: Mr. Pence

       Amendment No. 63: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to provide a United States contribution to the United 
     Nations Relief and Works Agency (UNRWA).
```
