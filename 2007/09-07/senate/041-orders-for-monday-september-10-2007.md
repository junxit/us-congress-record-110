# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2007

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
                 ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2007

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
Monday, September 10; that on Monday, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then proceed to 
executive session, as provided under a previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
