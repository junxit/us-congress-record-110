# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Edward M. (K000105) [D-MA]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      Committee on Armed Services

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Armed Services be authorized to meet during the session of 
the Senate on Friday, September 7, at 9 a.m. in open session, and 
possibly closed session, to receive a report on the Government 
Accountability Office's assessment of 18 Iraq benchmarks.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
