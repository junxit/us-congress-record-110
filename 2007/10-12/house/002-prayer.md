# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God, may the covenant You have made with Your people assure us 
once more of Your faithful love. On this day and throughout this 
weekend of religious observance, may we prove faithful on our part to 
Your love and Your commandments.
  Create in us as Your very own a sense of clear vision and confidence. 
Show us the path we should follow in the huge maze of freedoms 
protected by the Constitution of these United States. May the choices 
we make manifest integrity, wisdom, and love in us and bring greater 
understanding, unity, and peace to our world.
  We praise You for Your guidance and protection both now and forever. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
