                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:45 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Chiappardi, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House

of the Senate:

       H.R. 2316. An act to provide more rigorous requirements 
     with respect to disclosure and enforcement of lobbying laws 
     and regulations, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 2317. An act to amend the Lobbying Disclosure Act of

     reports on contributions bundled for certain recipients, and
