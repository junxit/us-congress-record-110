# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 4, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1348

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 4, 2007

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 2:30 
Monday, June 4; that on that day, following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, and the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later 
in the day; that there then be a period of morning business for 60 
minutes, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, 
and that the time be equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees; that upon conclusion of morning business, 
the Senate resume consideration of S. 1348, the immigration 
legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
