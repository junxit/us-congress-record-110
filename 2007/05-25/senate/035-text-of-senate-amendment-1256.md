# Text of Senate Amendment 1256

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
  SA 1256. Mr. REID (for Mr. Dorgan) proposed an amendment to the bill 
S. 398, to amend the Indian Child Protection and Family Violence 
Prevention Act to identify and remove barriers to reducing child abuse, 
to provide for examinations of certain children, and for other 
purposes; as follows:

       On page 20, strike lines 10 through 13 and insert the 
     following:
       (a) Offenses Committed Within Indian Country.--Section 
     1153(a) of title 18, United States Code, is amended by 
     striking ``felony child abuse or neglect'' and inserting 
     ``felony child abuse, felony child neglect''.

                          ____________________
```
