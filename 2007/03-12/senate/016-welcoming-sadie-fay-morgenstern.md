# WELCOMING SADIE FAY MORGENSTERN

**Speaking:** Crapo, Mike (C000880) [R-ID]

```
                    WELCOMING SADIE FAY MORGENSTERN

● Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, today I offer a most heartfelt 
welcome to a bright young lady who just made her entrance into this 
world--Sadie Fay Morgenstern. Sadie was born just over a week ago on 
March 4, 2007. She joins her big sister Sydney and parents, Andrew and 
Beth Morgenstern. I understand that little Sadie is proving to be 
alert, happy, and content. Undoubtedly, she will grow into a healthy, 
fun-loving and curious young lady, traits she will share with her older 
sister, Sydney. I am honored to share this news of the birth of a 
happy, healthy baby into a loving family, and I wish them the best. 
Thank you for joining me today in sending best wishes to the blessed 
and growing Morgenstern family.●

                          ____________________
```
