# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. REID. If there is no further business to come before the Senate--
I now ask the Republican leader if he has any business to bring before 
the Senate?

look forward to wrapping up the 9/11 bill sometime in the early

under the previous order.

Tuesday, March 13, 2007, at 10 a.m.
```
