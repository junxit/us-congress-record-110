# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on March 
12, 2007 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       WILLIAM HERBERT HEYMAN, OF NEW YORK, TO BE A DIRECTOR OF 
     THE SECURITIES INVESTOR PROTECTION CORPORATION FOR A TERM 
     EXPIRING DECEMBER 31, 2008, VICE THOMAS WATERS GRANT, TERM 
     EXPIRED, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 29, 2007.
```
