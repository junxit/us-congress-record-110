# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 276d-276g, as amended, appoints the following 
Senator as chairman of the Senate delegation to the Canada-U.S. 
Interparliamentary Group conference during the 110th Congress: the 
Honorable Amy Klobuchar of Minnesota.
  The Chair, on behalf of the President pro tempore, pursuant to 22 
U.S.C. 276n, as amended, appoints the following Senator as chairman of 
the U.S.-China Interparliamentary Group conference during the 110th 
Congress: the Honorable Daniel Inouye of Hawaii.
```
