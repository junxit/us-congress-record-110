# MOMENT OF SILENCE IN MEMORY OF THE LATE HONORABLE WILLIAM ANDERSON

**Speaking:** Davis, Lincoln (D000599) [D-TN]

```
   MOMENT OF SILENCE IN MEMORY OF THE LATE HONORABLE WILLIAM ANDERSON

  (Mr. LINCOLN DAVIS of Tennessee asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute.)
```
