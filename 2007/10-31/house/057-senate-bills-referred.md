# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 294, S. 2198, S. 2265

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 294. An act to reauthorize Amtrak, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure.
       S. 2198. An act to require the Architect of the Capitol to 
     permit the acknowledgment of God on flag certificates; to the 
     Committee on House Administration.
       S. 2265. An act to extend the existing provisions regarding 
     the eligibility for essential air service subsidies through 
     fiscal year 2008; to the Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure.
```
