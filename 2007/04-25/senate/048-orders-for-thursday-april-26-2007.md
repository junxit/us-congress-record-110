# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 26, 2007

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 26, 2007

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:15 
a.m., Thursday, April 26; that on Thursday following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders reserved for 
their use later in the day, with a period of morning business until 10 
a.m. with Senators permitted to speak therein; with the Senate 
proceeding to the conference report to accompany H.R. 1591, as provided 
for under a previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
