# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 
minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein, with the first 30 
minutes under the control of the majority and the final 30 minutes 
under the control of the Republicans.
  The Senator from Ohio is recognized.
```
