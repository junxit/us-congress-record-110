# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 327, H.Res. 680, H.R. 1808, H.R. 1955

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on motions to suspend the rules previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  Concurring in the Senate amendment to H.R. 327, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 1808, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Res. 680, by the yeas and nays.
  The vote on H.R. 1955 will be taken later today.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Remaining electronic votes will be conducted as 5-minute votes.
```
