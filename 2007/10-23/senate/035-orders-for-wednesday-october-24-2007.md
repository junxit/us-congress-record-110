# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 24, 2007

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 24, 2007

  Mrs. FEINSTEIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the

[[Page 27961]]

Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9 a.m., 
Wednesday, October 24; that on Wednesday, following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the 2 leaders reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then resume executive session and 
consideration of the Southwick nomination, with the time until 11 a.m. 
equally divided and controlled between the 2 leaders or their 
designees, and the time from 10:40 a.m. to 11 a.m. divided and 
controlled between the 2 leaders, with the majority leader controlling 
the final 10 minutes; that the Senate then proceed to vote on the 
motion to invoke cloture on the nomination at 11 a.m., as provided for 
under a previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
