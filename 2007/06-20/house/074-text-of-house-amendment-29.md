# Text of House Amendment 29

**Speaking:** Flake, Jeff (F000444) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2764

```
H.R. 2764

                         Offered By: Mr. Flake

       Amendment No. 29: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to fund nongovernmental organizations, specifically 
     named in the report accompanying the Act, outside of a 
     competitive bidding process.
```
