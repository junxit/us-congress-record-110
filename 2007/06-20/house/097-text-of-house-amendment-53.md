# Text of House Amendment 53

**Speaking:** Ros-Lehtinen, Ileana (R000435) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2764

```
H.R. 2764

                      Offered By: Ms. Ros-Lehtinen

       Amendment No. 53: At the end of the bill (before the short
```
