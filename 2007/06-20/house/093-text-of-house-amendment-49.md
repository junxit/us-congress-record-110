# Text of House Amendment 49

**Speaking:** Pence, Mike (P000587) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2764

```
H.R. 2764

                         Offered By: Mr. Pence

       Amendment No. 49: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:


  LIMITATION ON FUNDS FOR DISTRIBUTION OF ASSETS FROM LIQUIDATION OR 
                    DISSOLUTION OF ENTERPRISE FUNDS

       Sec. 6xx. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available under titles II through V of this Act may be used 
     to provide for the distribution of any assets from any 
     liquidation or dissolution of an Enterprise Fund, in whole or 
     in part, to an entity other than the United States Treasury.
```
