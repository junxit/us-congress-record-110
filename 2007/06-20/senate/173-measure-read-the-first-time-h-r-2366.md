# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2366

**Speaking:** Klobuchar, Amy (K000367) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2366

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2366

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 2366 has been 
received from the House and is at the desk. I ask for its first 
reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2366) to reauthorize the veterans 
     entrepreneurial development program of the Small Business 
     Administration, and for other purposes.

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I ask for its second reading and object 
to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
