# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 21, 2007

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 6

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 21, 2007

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10:30 
a.m. Thursday, June 21; that on Thursday, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved 
for their use later in the day; that the Senate then resume 
consideration of H.R. 6, as under the previous order; that Members have 
until 11 a.m. to file any germane second-degree amendments to the 
Baucus amendment No. 1704.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
