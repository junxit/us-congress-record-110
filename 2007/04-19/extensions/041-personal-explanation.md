# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JULIA CARSON

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 19, 2007

  Ms. CARSON. Madam Speaker, I regret that I was not able to cast votes 
on the evening of Tuesday, April 17, 2007 due to the cancellation of my 
scheduled flight from Indianapolis to Washington's Reagan National 
Airport and subsequent flight cancellations at Indianapolis. I 
understand that there was a backlog of eastbound travelers and limited 
flight options due to previous significant storm systems in the 
northeast.
  Had I been available to vote, I would have voted yes on: roll No. 
214; roll No. 215; roll No. 216; roll No. 217 and roll No. 218.
