# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. There will now be a period for 
morning business for 60 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the first 30 minutes controlled 
by the Republican leader or his designee, and the last 30 minutes 
controlled by the majority leader, or his designee.
  The Senator from Florida is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
