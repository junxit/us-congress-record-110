# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      COMMITTEE ON ARMED SERVICES

  Mr. KLOBUCHAR. Mr President, I ask unanimous consent that the

under the Logistics Civil Augmentation Program (LOCGAP) contract in 
Iraq.

research to U.S. competitiveness in science.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
