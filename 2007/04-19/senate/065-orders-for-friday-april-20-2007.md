# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, APRIL 20, 2007

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** S. 761

```
                   ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, APRIL 20, 2007

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
Friday, April 20; that on Friday following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day; that there then be a period of morning 
business until 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for 
up to 10 minutes each; that at 10:30 the Senate begin consideration of 
S. 761, the America COMPETES Act, as provided for under a previous 
order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
