# Text of House Amendment 30

**Speaking:** Boozman, John (B001236) [R-AR]

**Measures:** H.R. 3161

```
H.R. 3161

                        Offered By: Mr. Boozman

       Amendment No. 30: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to implement a National Animal Identification System 
     unless the participation by livestock owners in such a system 
     is voluntary.
```
