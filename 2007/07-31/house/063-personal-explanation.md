# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Weiner, Anthony D. (W000792) [D-NY], Wexler, Robert (W000314) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2347

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. WEINER. On rollcall 765, H.R. 2347, the Iran Sanctions Enabling 
Act, during the period of consideration of that bill Congressman Wexler 
and I were away from the floor, organizing efforts to stop the wrong-
headed arms sale to Saudi Arabia.
  Had I been present, I would have voted in favor, and believe we need 
to keep on sanctioning Iran.
  I yield to the gentleman from Florida.
  Mr. WEXLER. Madam Speaker, I, too, would like to be recognized as 
just expressing my support for H.R. 2347.
```
