# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Issa, Darrell (I000056) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3222

```
H.R. 3222

                          Offered By: Mr. Issa

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to disclose to the public the aggregate amount of 
     funds appropriated by Congress for the National Intelligence 
     Program (as defined in section 3(6) of the National Security 
     Act of 1947 (50 U.S.C. 401a(6))) for a fiscal year.
```
