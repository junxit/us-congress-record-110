# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF S. CON. RES. 21, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2008

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF S. CON. RES. 21, 
        CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2008

  Ms. CASTOR, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-121) on the resolution (H. Res. 370) providing 
for consideration of the Senate concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 21) 
setting forth the congressional budget for the United States Government 
for fiscal year 2008 and including the appropriate budgetary levels for 
fiscal years 2007 and 2009 through 2012, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
