# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under previous order, the hour of 12:30 p.m. 
having arrived, the Senate stands in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:27 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mrs. 
McCaskill).
```
