# ORDER FOR PRINTING OF TRIBUTES TO THE LATE PRESIDENT GERALD FORD

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
    ORDER FOR PRINTING OF TRIBUTES TO THE LATE PRESIDENT GERALD FORD

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that tributes to the 
late President Gerald Ford be printed as a Senate document and that 
Senators have until Thursday, February 15, of this year to submit 
tributes to the late President.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
