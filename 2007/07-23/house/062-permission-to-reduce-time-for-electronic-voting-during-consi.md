# PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3074

**Speaking:** Olver, John W. (O000085) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 558, H.R. 3074

```
PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING CONSIDERATION OF 
                               H.R. 3074

  Mr. OLVER. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, during 
consideration of H.R. 3074 pursuant to House Resolution 558, the Chair 
may reduce to 2 minutes the minimum time for electronic voting under 
clause 6 of rule XVIII and clauses 8 and 9 of rule XX.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Massachusetts?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
