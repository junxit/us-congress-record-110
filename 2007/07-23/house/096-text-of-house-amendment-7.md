# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Jordan, Jim (J000289) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 3074

```
H.R. 3074

                         Offered By: Mr. Jordan

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:
```
