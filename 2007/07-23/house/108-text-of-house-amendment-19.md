# Text of House Amendment 19

**Speaking:** Shays, Christopher (S001144) [R-CT]

**Measures:** H.R. 3074

```
H.R. 3074

                         Offered By: Mr. Shays

       Amendment No. 19: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Federal Aviation Administration to implement 
     the New York/New Jersey/Philadelphia Airspace Redesign 
     project of the Federal Aviation Administration.
```
