# Text of House Amendment 16

**Speaking:** Cardoza, Dennis A. (C001050) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3093

```
H.R. 3093

                        Offered By: Mr. Cardoza

       Amendment No. 16: Page 83, after line 6, insert the 
     following new section:
       Sec. 529. The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by reducing the amount made available for ``Bureau of 
     the Census--periodic censuses and programs'', by reducing the 
     amount made available for ``DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE--General 
     Administration--administrative review and appeals'', by 
     reducing the amount made available for ``DEPARTMENT OF 
     JUSTICE--General Administration--salaries and expenses'', and 
     by increasing the amount made available for ``Office on 
     Violence Against Women--violence against women prevention and 
     prosecution programs'' for the court-appointed special 
     advocate program, by $10,000,000, $2,350,000, $3,650,000, and 
     $16,000,000, respectively.
```
