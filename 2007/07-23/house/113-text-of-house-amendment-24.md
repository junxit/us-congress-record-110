# Text of House Amendment 24

**Speaking:** Walberg, Tim (W000798) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 3074

```
H.R. 3074

                        Offered By: Mr. Walberg

       Amendment No. 24: At the end of the bill, (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Department of Transportation to promulgate 
     regulations based solely on race, ethnicity, or sex.
```
