# Text of House Amendment 11

**Speaking:** Poe, Ted (P000592) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 3093

```
H.R. 3093

                          Offered By: Mr. Poe

       Amendment No. 11: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:

                TITLE VII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 701. None of the funds made available under this Act 
     shall be used by the Bureau of Prisons to incarcerate 
     Guillermo Falcon Hernandez.
```
