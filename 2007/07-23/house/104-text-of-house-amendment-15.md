# Text of House Amendment 15

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 3074

```
H.R. 3074

                        Offered By: Mr. Sessions

       Amendment No. 15: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following new section:
       Sec. 410. None of the funds made available by this Act 
     shall be used to support Amtrak's route with the highest 
     loss, measured by passenger per mile cost as based on the 
     National Railroad Passenger Corporation's September 2006 
     Financial Performance of Routes Report.
```
