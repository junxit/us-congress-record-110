# RECOGNIZING MEADOW, SOUTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                    RECOGNIZING MEADOW, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I wish to recognize Meadow, 
SD. The town of Meadow will celebrate the 100th anniversary of its 
founding this year.
  Meadow, located in Perkins County, was founded in 1907. Since its 
beginning, Meadow has been a strong reflection of South Dakota's values 
and traditions. As they celebrate this milestone anniversary, I am 
confident that Meadow will continue to thrive and succeed for the next 
100 years.
  I would like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Meadow on 
this milestone anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
