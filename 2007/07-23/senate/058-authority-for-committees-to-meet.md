# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 16

```
[[Page S9792]]
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                          committee on finance

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Finance be authorized to meet during the session of the Senate on 
Monday, July 23, 2007, at 5 p.m., in room 215 of the Dirksen Senate 
Office Building, to consider S.J. Res. 16, approving the renewal of 
import restrictions contained in the Burmese Freedom and Democracy Act 
of 2003.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
