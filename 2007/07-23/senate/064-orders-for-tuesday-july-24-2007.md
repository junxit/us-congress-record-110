# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 24, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1642

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 24, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m., 
Tuesday, July 24; that on Tuesday, following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, and the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later 
in the day; that the Senate then resume consideration of S. 1642, as 
under a previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I thank all Members for their cooperation 
today. We got a lot done.

                          ____________________
```
