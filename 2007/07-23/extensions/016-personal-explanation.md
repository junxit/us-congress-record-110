# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TIM MURPHY

                            of pennslyvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 23, 2007

  Mr. TIM MURPHY of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, on Thursday, July 19, 
2007 my vote on the Amendment by Mr. Camp of Michigan to H.R. 3043, the 
Departments of Labor, Health and Human Services, and Education, and 
Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008 (rollcall vote 675) was 
recorded as a ``no'' vote when I intended to cast a ``yes'' vote. I 
wish to clarify on the record my support for seniors in Pennsylvania 
and across the Nation to access Medicare Advantage plans.
```
