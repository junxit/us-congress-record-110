# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. TOM UDALL

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 7, 2007

  Mr. UDALL of New Mexico. Madam Speaker, on March 13th I was 
unavoidably detained and missed rollcall vote numbers 121 and 122. 
Rollcall vote 121 was final passage of House Resolution 98, honoring 
the life and achievements of the late Dr. John Garang de Mabior, and 
had I been present, I would have voted, ``aye.'' Rollcall vote 122 was 
final passage of House Resolution 149, supporting the goals of 
International Women's Day, and had I been present, I would have voted, 
``aye.''
```
