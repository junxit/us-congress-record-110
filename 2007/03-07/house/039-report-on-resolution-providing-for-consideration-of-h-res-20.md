**Measures:** H.Res. 202, H.Res. 219

```
   REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. RES. 202, 
       PROVIDING FOR EXPENSES OF CERTAIN COMMITTEES OF HOUSE OF 
                   REPRESENTATIVES IN 110TH CONGRESS

report (Rept. No. 110-34) on the resolution (H. Res. 219) providing for 
consideration of the resolution (H. Res. 202) providing for the

One Hundred Tenth Congress, which was referred to the House Calendar
```
