# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1495, WATER RESOURCES DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 2007

**Measures:** H.Res. 319, H.R. 1495

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1495, WATER 
                   RESOURCES DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 2007

  Mr. CARDOZA, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-100) on the resolution (H. Res. 319) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1495) to provide for the 
conservation and development of water and related resources, to 
authorize the Secretary of the Army to construct various projects for 
improvements to rivers and harbors of the United States, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
