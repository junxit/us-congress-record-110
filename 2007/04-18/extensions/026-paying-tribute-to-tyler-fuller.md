# PAYING TRIBUTE TO TYLER FULLER

```
                     PAYING TRIBUTE TO TYLER FULLER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JON C. PORTER

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 18, 2007

  Mr. PORTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Tyler Fuller, a 7-
year-old international BMX champion.
  Tyler, a two-time Union Cycliste Internationale BMX champion, learned 
to ride a bike at the age of 2 and began BMX racing at the age of 3. 
When he was 5 years old, Tyler joined the Redman Yamaha Factory Team 
and has been racing for them since that time.

[[Page 9296]]

Tyler has competed in events around the world and his natural ability 
and dedication to the sport have earned him recognition as one of the 
top four BMX racers in his age group.
  Madam Speaker, I am proud to honor Tyler Fuller. His talent, drive, 
and passion are commendable and will serve him well. I wish him 
continued success in his future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
