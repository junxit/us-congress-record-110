# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Ross). Pursuant to section 201(a)(2) of 
the Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control Act of 1974 (2. U.S.C. 
601), and the order of the House of January 4, 2007, the Chair 
announces the Speaker and President pro tempore of the Senate jointly 
appointed Dr. Peter R. Orszag as Director of the Congressional Budget 
Office, effective January 18, 2007, for the term expiring January 3, 
2011.
```
