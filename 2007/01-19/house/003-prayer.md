# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  America has put her trust in the love God has for us and has shown us 
throughout our history.
  Those who live in such love live in God, and God lives in them.
  So let us love one another. For then the love that comes from God 
living in us will reach out to a waiting world and all will be drawn in 
to love both now and forever. Amen.
```
