# Text of Senate Amendment 1149

**Speaking:** McCaskill, Claire (M001170) [D-MO]

```
  SA 1149. Mrs. McCASKILL submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by her to the bill S. 1348, to promote for comprehensive 
immigration reform and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       On page 281, between lines 40 and 41, insert the following:
       (vi) Misdemeanor offense.--The alien shall admit to a 
     misdemeanor offense for being in the United States illegally, 
     and such offense shall be punishable by at least 416 hours of 
     community service.
                                 ______
```
