# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Maloney, Carolyn B. (M000087) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1427

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. CAROLYN B. MALONEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 21, 2007

  Mrs. MALONEY of New York. Madam Speaker, I regret that I was unable 
to vote on six amendments to H.R. 1427, the Federal Housing Finance 
Reform Act of 2007.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 
378, the amendment offered by Mr. Bachus. I would have voted ``no'' on 
rollcall vote No. 379, the amendment offered by Mr. Hensarling. I would 
have voted ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 380, the amendment offered by 
Mr. McHenry. I would have voted ``aye'' on rollcall vote No. 381, the 
amendment offered by Mr. Kanjorski. I would have voted ``no'' on 
rollcall vote No. 382, the amendment offered by Mr. Roskam. I would 
have voted ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 383, the amendment offered by 
Mr. Garrett.

                          ____________________
```
