# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House reports that on September 19, 
2007 she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills.

       H.R. 954. To designate the facility of the United States 
     Postal Service located at 365 West 125th Street in New York, 
     New York, as the ``Percy Sutton Post Office Building''.
       H.R. 2669. To provide for reconciliation pursuant to 
     section 601 of the concurrent resolution on the budget for 
     fiscal year 2008.
       H.R. 3218. To designate a portion of Interstate Route 395 
     located in Baltimore, Maryland, as ``Cal Ripken Way''.

                          ____________________
```
