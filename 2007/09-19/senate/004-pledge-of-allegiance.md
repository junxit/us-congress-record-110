# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

follows:

  I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of America, and 
to the Republic for which it stands, one nation under God, indivisible, 
with liberty and justice for all.
```
