# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 10, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 10, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m., 
Tuesday, April 10; that on Tuesday, following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date; the morning hour be 
deemed expired; and the time of the two leaders be reserved for use 
later in the day; that there then be a period of morning business for 
60 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each; 
with the first 30 minutes under the control of the Republicans, and the 
final 30 minutes under the control of the majority; that following 
morning business, the Senate proceed to consider the two stem cell 
bills, as provided for under a previous order entered by the President 
earlier today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
