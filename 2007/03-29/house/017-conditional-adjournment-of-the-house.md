# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE

```
                  CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE

  Ms. HOOLEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns on the legislative day of Thursday, March 29, 2007, or Friday, 
March 30, 2007, on a motion offered pursuant to this order, it adjourn 
to meet at 12:30 p.m. on the third constitutional day thereafter, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 103, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Oregon?
  Mr. PRICE of Georgia. I object, Mr. Speaker.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
