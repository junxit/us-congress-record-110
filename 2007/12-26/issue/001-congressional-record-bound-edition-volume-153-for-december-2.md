# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 153 for December 26, 2007

```
[[Page 36502]]




                  SENATE--Wednesday, December 26, 2007

            (Legislative day of Wednesday, December 19, 2007)
  The Senate met at 9:30 and 2 seconds a.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Jim Webb, a Senator 
from the State of Virginia.

                          ____________________



            RECESS UNTIL 10 A.M., FRIDAY, DECEMBER 28, 2007

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Friday, December 28, 2007, at 10 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:30 and 11 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Friday, December 28, 2007, at 10 a.m.
```
