# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1591, H.R. 1681

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House reports that on May 1, 2007, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills.

       H.R. 1591. Making emergency supplemental appropriations for 
     the fiscal year ending September 30, 2007, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 1681. To amend the Congressional Charter of The 
     American National Red Cross to modernize its governance 
     structure, to enhance the ability of the board of governors 
     of The American National Red Cross in the 21st century, and 
     for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
