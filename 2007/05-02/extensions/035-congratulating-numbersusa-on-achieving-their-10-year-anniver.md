# CONGRATULATING NUMBERSUSA ON ACHIEVING THEIR 10-YEAR ANNIVERSARY

```
    CONGRATULATING NUMBERSUSA ON ACHIEVING THEIR 10-YEAR ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS G. TANCREDO

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Wednesday, May 2, 2007

  Mr. TANCREDO. Madam Speaker, I rise today to offer my sincere 
congratulations to NumbersUSA on achieving its 10-year anniversary. For 
the past decade, NumbersUSA has worked to advocate for common-sense 
immigration reforms.
  Formed in 1997 by Roy Beck, NumbersUSA has grown dramatically from 
300 a decade ago to over 300,000 activist members today. They have 
provided a voice for the hundreds of thousands of Americans that expect 
their government to enforce our immigration laws and pursue policies 
that enhance the security, prosperity, and overall well-being of our 
Nation.
  NumbersUSA honors our immigrant heritage and our commitment to the 
rule of law through its work toward a more sustainable national 
immigration policy.
  So once again, Madam Speaker, I would like to congratulate Mr. Beck 
and the staff and members of NumbersUSA on the achievement of this 
important milestone, and wish them the best of luck in the future.

                          ____________________
```
