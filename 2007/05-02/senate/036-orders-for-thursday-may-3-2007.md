# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 3, 2007

```
                    ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 3, 2007

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 
a.m., Thursday, May 3; that on Thursday, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed to have expired, and the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day; that the Senate then resume 
consideration of S. 1082 and there be an hour of debate prior to a vote 
on the motion to invoke cloture on the Dorgan amendment No. 990, with 
the time equally divided and controlled between Senator Dorgan and the 
Republican leader or his designee; that upon the use or yielding back 
of the time, the Senate proceed to vote on that motion to invoke 
cloture; and that Members have until 10 a.m. to file any second-degree 
amendments.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
