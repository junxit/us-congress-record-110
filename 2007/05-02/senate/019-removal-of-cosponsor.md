# REMOVAL OF COSPONSOR

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

**Measures:** S. 1038, S. 1083

```
                          REMOVAL OF COSPONSOR

  Mr. CORNYN. Madam President, I ask that Senator Pete Domenici be 
removed as a cosponsor to S. 1038, the Workforce Health Improvement 
Act, and added as a cosponsor to S. 1083, the SKIL Act. Let the Record 
reflect that due to a clerical error Senator Domenici was inadvertently 
added as a co-sponsor to the Wokforce Health Improvement Act.
```
