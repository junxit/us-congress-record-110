# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Our Father in heaven, light of the world, give the Members of this 
body Your light. Shine Your light to help them see the truth. Shine 
Your light so they can see the path You desire them to travel. Shine 
Your light so they can see themselves as they truly are and not take 
for granted the freedoms they enjoy. Shine Your light so they may live 
expectantly, open for what You will do or give. Shine Your light so 
they may see You in all Your majesty and love. Lord, fill this Chamber 
with the light of Your presence, enabling each Senator to discern and 
do Your will.
  We pray in Your radiant Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
