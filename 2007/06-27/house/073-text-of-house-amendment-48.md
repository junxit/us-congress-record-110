# Text of House Amendment 48

**Speaking:** Stearns, Cliff (S000822) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                        Offered By: Mr. Stearns

       Amendment No. 48: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) add the following:

                                TITLE IX

                     ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used by the Internal Revenue Service to implement a 
     spanish-language version of the ``Where's my Refund?'' 
     service.
```
