# Text of House Amendment 43

**Speaking:** Campbell, John (C001064) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                 Offered By: Mr. Campbell of California

       Amendment No. 43: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       None of the funds in this Act to the Small Business 
     Administration may be used for the Abraham Lincoln National 
     Airport Commission.
```
