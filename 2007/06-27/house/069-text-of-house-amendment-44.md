# Text of House Amendment 44

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                      Offered By: Mr. King of Iowa

       Amendment No. 44: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                TITLE IX--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to carry out section 241 of the Help America Vote Act 
     of 2002 (42 U.S.C. 15381).
```
