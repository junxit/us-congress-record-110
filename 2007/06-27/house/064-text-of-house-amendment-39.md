# Text of House Amendment 39

**Speaking:** Boozman, John (B001236) [R-AR]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                        Offered By: Mr. Boozman

       Amendment No. 39: Page 27, line 6, insert before the period 
     the following: ``: Provided further, that $6,000,000 shall 
     not be made available until the Director of the Office of 
     National Drug Control Policy certifies in writing that 
     regulations established for the designation of high intensity 
     drug trafficking areas include a requirement that the 
     Director, in considering whether to designate an area as a 
     high intensity drug trafficking area, shall consider whether 
     the area lies within a State that already receives assistance 
     under the High Intensity Drug Trafficking Areas program''.
```
