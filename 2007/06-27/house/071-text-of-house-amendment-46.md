# Text of House Amendment 46

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                      Offered By: Mr. King of Iowa

       Amendment No. 46: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                                TITLE IX

                     ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. None of the funds in this Act may be used to 
     employ workers described in section 274A(h)(3) of the 
     Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1324a(h)(3)).
```
