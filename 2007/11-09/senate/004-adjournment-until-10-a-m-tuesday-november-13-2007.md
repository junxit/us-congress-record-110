# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M., TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 2007

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M., TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 2007

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate now stands adjourned until the hour of 10 a.m. on Tuesday, 
November 13, 2007.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 o'clock and 48 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Tuesday, November 13, 2007, at 10 a.m.
```
