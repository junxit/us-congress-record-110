                                 S. 38

  At the request of Mr. Domenici, the name of the Senator from Iowa 
(Mr. Grassley) was added as a cosponsor of S. 38, a bill to require the

Operation Iraqi Freedom and Operation Enduring Freedom, and for other 
purposes.
