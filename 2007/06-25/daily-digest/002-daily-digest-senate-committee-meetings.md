EXCESSIVE SPECULATION IN THE NATURAL GAS MARKET
Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: Permanent 
Subcommittee on Investigations held a hearing to examine excessive 
speculation in the natural gas market, receiving testimony from Arthur 
Corbin, Municipal Gas Authority of Georgia, Kennesaw, on behalf of the 
American Public Gas Association; Paul Cicio, Industrial Energy 
Consumers of America, Washington, D.C.; Sean Cota, New England Fuel 
Institute, Watertown, Massachusetts; Vincent Kaminski, Rice University 
Jesse H. Jones Graduate School of Management, Houston, Texas; Michael 
Greenberger, University of Maryland School of Law, Baltimore; and Shane 
Lee, former Natural Gas Trader, Amaranth Advisors LLC, Calgary, 
Alberta, Canada.
   Committee will continue hearings on July 9, 2007.
