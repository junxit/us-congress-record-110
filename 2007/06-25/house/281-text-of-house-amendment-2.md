# Text of House Amendment 2

**Speaking:** Upton, Fred (U000031) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                         Offered By: Ms. Upton

       Amendment No. 2: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                 TITLE IX--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISION

       Sec. 901. None of the funds made available in this Act 
     (including funds made available in titles IV and VIII) may be 
     used to purchase light bulbs unless the light bulbs have the 
     ``ENERGY STAR'' or ``Federal Energy Management Program'' 
     designation.
```
