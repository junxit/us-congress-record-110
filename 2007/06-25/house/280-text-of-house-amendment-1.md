# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Garrett, Scott (G000548) [R-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                 Offered By: Mr. Garrett of New Jersey

       Amendment No. 1: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                TITLE IX--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be used by the Securities and Exchange Commission to 
     enforce the requirements of section 404 of the Sarbanes-Oxley 
     Act with respect to non-accelerated filers under section 
     210.2-02T of title 17, Code of Federal Regulations.
```
