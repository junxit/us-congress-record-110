# Text of House Amendment 142

**Speaking:** Hensarling, Jeb (H001036) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2643

```
H.R. 2643

                       Offered By: Mr. Hensarling

       Amendment No. 142: At the end of the bill (before the short
```
