# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 40, S. 1099

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill and a concurrent resolution of the 
following titles in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1099. An act to amend chapter 89 of title 5, United 
     States Code, to make individuals employed by the Roosevelt 
     Campobello International Park Commission eligible to obtain 
     Federal health insurance.
       S. Con. Res. 40. Concurrent resolution supporting the goals 
     and ideals of observing the National Day of Human Trafficking 
     Awareness on January 11 of each year to raise awareness of 
     and opposition to human trafficking.

                          ____________________
```
