# Text of House Amendment 22

**Speaking:** Jordan, Jim (J000289) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2643

```
H.R. 2643

                     Offered By: Mr. Jordan of Ohio

       Amendment No. 22: Page 111, after line 17, insert the 
     following:

                TITLE VI--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS
```
