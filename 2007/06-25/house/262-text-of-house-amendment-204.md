# Text of House Amendment 204

**Speaking:** Hensarling, Jeb (H001036) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2643

```
H.R. 2643

                       Offered By: Mr. Hensarling

       Amendment No. 204: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       None of the funds in this Act may be used for the city of 
     Sabinal, Texas, for a Wastewater Treatment Facility Project.
```
