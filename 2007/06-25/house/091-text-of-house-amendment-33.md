# Text of House Amendment 33

**Speaking:** Weiner, Anthony D. (W000792) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2643

```
H.R. 2643

                         Offered By: Mr. Weiner

       Amendment No. 33: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                TITLE VI--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 601. Not later than 6 months after the date of the 
     enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Interior shall 
     provide public access to the Statue of Liberty that is 
     substantially the same as that access granted before 
     September 11, 2001.
```
