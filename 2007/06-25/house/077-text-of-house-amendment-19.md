# Text of House Amendment 19

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2643

```
H.R. 2643

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 19: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                TITLE VI--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec.__. None of the funds made available in this Act may be 
     used to limit outreach programs administered by the 
     Smithsonian Institution.
```
