# KENNETH T. WHALUM, SR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2587

```
              KENNETH T. WHALUM, SR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2587) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 555 South 3rd Street Lobby in Memphis, 
Tennessee, as the ``Kenneth T. Whalum, Sr. Post Office Building,'' was
```
