# ELEANOR McGOVERN POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 ELEANOR McGOVERN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2654) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 202 South Dumont Avenue in Woonsocket, South 
Dakota, as the ``Eleanor McGovern Post Office Building,'' was ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
