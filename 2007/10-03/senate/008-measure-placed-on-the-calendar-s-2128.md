# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2128

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2128

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2128

  Mr. REID. It is my understanding that S. 2128 is at the desk and due 
for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bill by 
title for the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2128) to make the moratorium on Internet access 
     taxes and multiple and discriminatory taxes on electronic 
     commerce permanent.

  Mr. REID. I object to any further proceedings with respect to this 
bill.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard. The bill will 
be placed on the calendar.
```
