# ROBERT MERRILL POSTAL STATION

```
                     ROBERT MERRILL POSTAL STATION

  The bill (H.R. 2778) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 3 Quaker Ridge Road in New Rochelle, New 
York, as the ``Robert Merrill Postal Station,'' was ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
