# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for a 
period of 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each, and with the time equally divided and controlled between 
the two leaders or their designees.
  The Senator from New Mexico.

                          ____________________
```
