                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

             Week of December 10 through December 15, 2007

                             Senate Chamber

  On Monday, at 3 p.m., Senate will resume consideration on H.R. 2419, 
Farm Bill Extension Act.

legislative and executive business.
