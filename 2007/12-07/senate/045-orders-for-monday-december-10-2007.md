# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 10, 2007

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 10, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m., Monday, 
December 10; that on Monday, following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of Proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in 
the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of the farm bill, 
H.R. 2419.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
