# COMMUNICATION FROM STAFF MEMBER OF THE HONORABLE DANA ROHRABACHER, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
  COMMUNICATION FROM STAFF MEMBER OF THE HONORABLE DANA ROHRABACHER, 
                           MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Kathleen Hollingsworth, District Director, Office of 
the Honorable Dana Rohrabacher, Member of Congress:

                                                     July 6, 2007.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     The Speaker, U.S. House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I have been served with a subpoena, 
     issued by the Superior Court of Orange County, California, 
     for testimony in a criminal case.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the precedents and privileges of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                           Kathleen Hollingsworth,
     District Director.

                          ____________________
```
