# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 980

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 980

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I understand that H.R. 980 has been 
received from the House and is at the desk. I ask for its first 
reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title for 
the first time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 980) to provide collective bargaining rights 
     for public safety officers employed by States or their 
     political subdivisions.

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask for its second reading but object to 
my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
