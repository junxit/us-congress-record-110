# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1

```
                   UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to the consideration of Calendar No. S. 1, the ethics bill, at 11 a.m. 
tomorrow morning, January 9, for debate only until 2:15 p.m, with the 
time, until the Senate recesses for the party luncheons, equally 
divided and controlled between the leaders and their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
