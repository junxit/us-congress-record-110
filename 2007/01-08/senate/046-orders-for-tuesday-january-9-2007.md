# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JANUARY 9, 2007

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JANUARY 9, 2007

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m., Tuesday, January 9; 
that on Tuesday, following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and that there be a period of 
morning business until 11 a.m., with the Senator permitted to speak 
therein, with the first half controlled by the minority and the second 
half controlled by the majority; and that the Senate stand in recess 
from 12:30 until 2:15 for the party caucus luncheons.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
