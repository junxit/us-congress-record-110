# PAYING TRIBUTE TO DONNA VOLNER

```
                     PAYING TRIBUTE TO DONNA VOLNER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JON C. PORTER

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 24, 2007

  Mr. PORTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Mrs. Donna Volner, 
who is retiring from Clark County after 15 years of distinguished 
service.
  Donna, who is from Los Angeles, CA, began her career in Missouri 
where she initially served as a clerk/typist for Missouri State 
Welfare. During her tenure with the Missouri State Welfare, Donna 
exhibited her tireless dedication and her great abilities and by the 
end of her 14 years of service. As a result of her commitment and 
dedication to serving others, Donna was named County Director.
  Donna's work for Clark County began in the early 1990s, when she 
accepted a position as an eligibility worker for Clark County Social 
Services. In 1995, Donna transferred to the Neighborhood Justice 
Center, where she served the community for 12 years. After retiring 
from Clark County, Donna plans to continue her service to the Clark 
County Community as a volunteer for Clark County Social Services and at 
the local Ronald McDonald House.
  Madam Speaker, I am proud to honor Donna Volner. Her tireless work 
for Clark County has improved the lives of countless people. I thank 
her for her dedication and commitment to the community and wish her the 
best in her future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
