# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 417

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 417

```
     REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 417

  Mr. SCHIFF. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that Representative 
Xavier Becerra be removed as a cosponsor of H. Res. 417. Mr. Becerra 
was listed as a cosponsor due to a clerical error.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
