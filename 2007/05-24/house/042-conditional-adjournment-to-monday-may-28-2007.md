# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 28, 2007

**Speaking:** Crowley, Joseph (C001038) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 158

```
            CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 28, 2007

  Mr. CROWLEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today on a motion offered pursuant to this order, it adjourn 
to meet at 9:30 a.m. on Monday, May 28, 2007, unless it sooner has 
received a message from the Senate transmitting its concurrence on 
House Concurrent Resolution 158, in which case the House shall stand 
adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
