# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. KING of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to the order of the House of 
today, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Accordinly, pursuant to the previous order 
of the House of today, the House stands adjourned until 9:30 a.m. on 
Monday, May 28, 2007, unless it sooner has received a message from the 
Senate transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 158, in 
which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent 
resolution.
  Thereupon (at 8 o'clock and 16 minutes p.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until Monday, May 28, 2007, at 9:30 a.m., 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 158, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.

                          ____________________
```
