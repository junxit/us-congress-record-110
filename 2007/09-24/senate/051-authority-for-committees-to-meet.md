# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


               Committee on Energy and Natural Resources

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Energy and Natural Resources be authorized to hold a hearing during 
the session of the Senate on Monday, September 24, 2007, at 3 p.m. in 
room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building. The purpose of the 
hearing is to consider scientific assessments of the impacts of global 
climate change on wildfire activity in the United States.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
