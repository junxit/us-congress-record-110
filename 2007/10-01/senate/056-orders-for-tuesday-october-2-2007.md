# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 2, 2007

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 2, 2007

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m., 
Tuesday, October 2; that on Tuesday, following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and that there then be a period for the transaction 
of morning business for 60 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally divided and 
controlled between the two sides, with the Republicans controlling the 
first half and the majority controlling the final portion; that 
following morning business, the Senate proceed to H.R. 3222, as 
provided for under a previous order; that on Tuesday, the Senate stand 
in recess from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. in order to accommodate the 
respective party conference meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
