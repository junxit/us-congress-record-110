# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Green, Al (G000553) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 3121

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. AL GREEN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 1, 2007

  Mr. AL GREEN of Texas. Madam Speaker, today I was unavoidably delayed 
and missed the vote on final passage of H.R. 3121, The Flood Insurance 
Reform and Modernization Act of 2007 (Rollcall 921). Although H.R. 3121 
passed by a vote of 263-146, I respectfully request the opportunity to 
record my position. Had I been present I would have voted ``yea'' on 
Rollcall 921.
```
