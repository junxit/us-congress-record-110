# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Larson, John B. (L000557) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.Res. 56

```

  Mr. LARSON of Connecticut. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the 
Democratic Caucus, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 56) and ask 
for its immediate consideration.
```
