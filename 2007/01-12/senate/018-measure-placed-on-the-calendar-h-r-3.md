# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3

```
                 MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3

  Mr. REID. I understand that H.R. 3 is at the desk and ready for its 
second reading.

       A bill (H.R. 3) to amend the Public Health Service Act to
```
