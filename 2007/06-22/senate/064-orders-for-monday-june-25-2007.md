# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 25, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 800

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 25, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 1 p.m., Monday, 
June 25; that on Monday, following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate then resume consideration of the motion to proceed to 
H.R. 800, with the time until 7 p.m. for debate with respect to the 
motion, with the time equally divided and controlled between Senators 
Kennedy and Enzi or their designees; that at 7 p.m. Senator Sessions be 
recognized to speak for up to 1 hour.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
