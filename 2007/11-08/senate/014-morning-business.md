# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will now proceed to a period for the transaction of morning 
business for 60 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 
minutes each, with the time equally divided and controlled between the 
two leaders or their designees, with the majority controlling the first 
half and the Republicans controlling the final half.
  The Senator from Montana.
```
