# A FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 1495

```
                   A FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate having proceeded to reconsider the bill (H.R. 
1495) ``An Act to provide for the conservation and development of water 
and related resources, to authorize the Secretary of the Army to 
construct various projects for improvements to rivers and harbors of 
the United States, and for other purposes'', returned by the President 
of the United States with his objections, to the House in which it 
originated, and passed by the House on reconsideration of the same, it 
was
  Resolved, That the said bill pass, two-thirds of the Senators present 
having voted in the affirmative.
```
