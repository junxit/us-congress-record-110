# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 3355, HOMEOWNERS' DEFENSE ACT OF 2007

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 3355, 
                    HOMEOWNERS' DEFENSE ACT OF 2007

  Mr. KLEIN of Florida. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the
```
