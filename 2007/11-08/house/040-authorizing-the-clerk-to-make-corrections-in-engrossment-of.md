# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 3355, HOMEOWNERS' DEFENSE ACT OF 2007

**Speaking:** Klein, Ron (K000366) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 3355

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 3355, 
                    HOMEOWNERS' DEFENSE ACT OF 2007

  Mr. KLEIN of Florida. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Clerk be authorized to make technical corrections in the engrossment of 
H.R. 3355, to include corrections in spelling, punctuation, section 
numbering, cross-referencing, and amendatory instructions, and the 
insertion of appropriate headings.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Tauscher). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Florida?
```
