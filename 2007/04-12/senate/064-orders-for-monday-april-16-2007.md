# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 16, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 372

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 16, 2007

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it stand adjourned until 2 p.m. on Monday, April 16, 
and on Monday, following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that there then be a period of morning business for 60 minutes 
with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each; that at the 
close of morning business the Senate resume consideration of S. 372, as 
provided for under a previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
