# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2895, NATIONAL AFFORDABLE HOUSING TRUST FUND ACT OF 2007

**Measures:** H.Res. 720, H.R. 2895

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2895, NATIONAL 
               AFFORDABLE HOUSING TRUST FUND ACT OF 2007

  Ms. SLAUGHTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-369) on the resolution (H. Res. 720) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2895) to establish the National 
Affordable Housing Trust Fund in the Treasury of the United States to 
provide for the construction, rehabilitation, and preservation of 
decent, safe, and affordable housing for low-income families, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
