# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3056, TAX COLLECTION RESPONSIBILITY ACT OF 2007

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3056, TAX 
                 COLLECTION RESPONSIBILITY ACT OF 2007

  Ms. SLAUGHTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-368) on the resolution (H. Res. 719) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3056) to amend the Internal Revenue 
Code of 1986 to repeal the authority of the Internal Revenue Service to 
use private debt collection companies, to delay implementation of 
withholding taxes on government contractors, to revise the tax rules on 
expatriation, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
