# IN HONOR OF THE DRIFTERS, INC.

```
                     IN HONOR OF THE DRIFTERS, INC.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FRANK PALLONE, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 9, 2007

  Mr. PALLONE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor and recognize the 
services of The Drifters, Inc.
  This organization was founded in Texas in 1954 to enhance the 
universal image of womanhood and serve as role models to women 
everywhere. The Drifters have chapters in 31 States in the United 
States. They have continued to provide for the youth and senior 
citizens within our society.
  The Drifters reach out to educational institutions by awarding 
scholarships to local area high school students. Every year the 
Drifters choose one university to give scholarship money to promising 
students. They devote their time and energy during the holidays by 
delivering gift baskets to the underserved. Amongst the numerous 
charitable events, the women of the Drifters are actively involved with 
aiding the homeless and battered women.
  Madam Speaker, I hope my colleagues will join me in expressing 
sincere appreciation to the Drifters. These women work hard to provide 
for future generations and empower individuals throughout the United 
States.
```
