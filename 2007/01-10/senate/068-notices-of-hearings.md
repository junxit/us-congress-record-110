# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM]

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


               Committee on Energy and Natural Resources

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I wish to inform the Senate and the 
Public that the committee on Energy and Natural Resources will hold a 
hearing during the session of the Senate on Wednesday, January 10, 2007 
at 9:45 a.m. in room SD-G50 of the Dirksen Building.
  The purpose of the hearing is to receive testimony on the global oil 
balance and its implications for U.S. economic and national security.
```
