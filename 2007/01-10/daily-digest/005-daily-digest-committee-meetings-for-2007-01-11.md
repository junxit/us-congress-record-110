```
                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR THURSDAY,

                            JANUARY 11, 2007

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

p.m., 2118 Rayburn.
  Committee on Foreign Affairs, oversight hearing on the Next Steps 
in the Iran Crisis, 10 a.m., and an oversight hearing on Iraq, 2 
p.m., 2172 Rayburn.
```
