# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 12, 2007

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 12, 2007

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 1 p.m. 
Monday, February 12; that on Monday, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders reserved for 
their use later in the day; that there then be a period of morning 
business with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each; that on Monday, Members have until 2:30 p.m. to file first-degree 
amendments and that the mandatory quorum required under rule XXII be 
waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
