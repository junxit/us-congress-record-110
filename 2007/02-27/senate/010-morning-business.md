# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until the 
hour of 12:30 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the first 30 minutes will be controlled by 
the Republican leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Texas.

                          ____________________
```
