# REGARDING H.R. 3327

```
                          REGARDING H.R. 3327

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRAD SHERMAN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 3, 2007

  Mr. SHERMAN. Madam Speaker, I have cosponsored H.R. 3327 to amend the 
Animal Welfare Act to prohibit dog fighting ventures because of the 
terrible problem of dog fighting. The author of the bill has assured me 
that in the committee process the penalties imposed on spectators will 
be modified.
```
