# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--H.R. 1495

```
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--H.R. 1495

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at a time to be 
determined by the majority leader, following consultation with the 
Republican leader, the Senate proceed to the consideration of the 
conference report to accompany H.R. 1495, WRDA; that it be considered 
under the following limitations: that there be 4 hours of debate on the 
conference report with the time equally divided and controlled between 
the two leaders or their designees; that upon the use or yielding back 
of time, the Senate proceed to vote on adoption of the conference 
report, without intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. FEINGOLD. Mr. President, I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The Senator from Minnesota is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
