# THANKING STAFF

```
                             THANKING STAFF

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I extend my appreciation to our valiant 
staff. I wish them a very pleasant August. They worked so hard, along 
with us. We could not do our work without them. Everyone in this body 
here tonight--from our pages to our Parliamentarians to all the clerks, 
court reporters, police officers--I appreciate all the work they do.

                          ____________________
```
