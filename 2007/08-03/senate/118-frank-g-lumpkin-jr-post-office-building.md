# FRANK G. LUMPKIN, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
               FRANK G. LUMPKIN, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2309) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 3916 Milgen Road in Columbus, Georgia, as the 
``Frank G. Lumpkin, Jr. Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
