# GEORGE B. LEWIS POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  GEORGE B. LEWIS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2077) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 20805 State Route 125 in Blue Creek, Ohio, as 
the ``George B. Lewis Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
