# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--H.R. 3159

**Speaking:** Dodd, Christopher J. (D000388) [D-CT], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 3159

```
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--H.R. 3159

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
receives from the House H.R. 3159, the Dwell Time Act, the bill be 
considered as having been read three times, passed, and the motion to 
reconsider be laid on the table without intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. THUNE. I object.
```
