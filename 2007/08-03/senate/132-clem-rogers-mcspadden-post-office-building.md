# CLEM ROGERS McSPADDEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2127

```
               CLEM ROGERS McSPADDEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2127) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 408 West Street in Chelsea, Oklahoma, as the 
``Clem Rogers McSpadden Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
