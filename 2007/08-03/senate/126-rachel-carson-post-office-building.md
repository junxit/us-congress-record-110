# RACHEL CARSON POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1434

```
                   RACHEL CARSON POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1434) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 896 Pittsburgh Street in Springdale, 
Pennsylvania, as the ``Rachel Carson Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and
```
