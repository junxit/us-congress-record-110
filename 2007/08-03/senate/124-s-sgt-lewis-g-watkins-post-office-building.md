# S/SGT LEWIS G. WATKINS POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1335

```
              S/SGT LEWIS G. WATKINS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1335) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 508 East Main Street in Seneca, South 
Carolina, as the ``S/Sgt Lewis G. Watkins Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and
```
