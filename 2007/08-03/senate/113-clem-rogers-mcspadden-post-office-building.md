# CLEM ROGERS McSPADDEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
               CLEM ROGERS McSPADDEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2127) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 408 West 6th Street in Chelsea, Oklahoma, as 
the ``Clem Rogers McSpadden Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
