# DR. KARL E. CARSON POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2570

```
                DR. KARL E. CARSON POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2570) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 301 Boardwalk Drive in Fort Collins, 
Colorado, as the ``Dr. Karl E. Carson Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
