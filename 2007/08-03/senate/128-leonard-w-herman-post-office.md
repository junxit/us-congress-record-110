# LEONARD W. HERMAN POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 1722

```
                     LEONARD W. HERMAN POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 1722) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 601 Banyan Trail in Boca Raton, Florida, as 
the ``Leonard W. Herman Post Office,'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
