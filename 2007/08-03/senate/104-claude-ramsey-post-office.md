# CLAUDE RAMSEY POST OFFICE

```
                       CLAUDE RAMSEY POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 1260) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 6301 Highway 58 in Harrison, Tennessee, as 
the ``Claude Ramsey Post Office,'' was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
