```
             STAFF SERGEANT OMER `O.T.' HAWKINS POST OFFICE

Postal Service located at 14536 State Route 136 in Cherry Fork, Ohio, 
as the ``Staff Sergeant Omer `O.T.' Hawkins Post Office,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and
```
