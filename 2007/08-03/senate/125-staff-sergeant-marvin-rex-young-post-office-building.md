# STAFF SERGEANT MARVIN `REX' YOUNG POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1425

```
         STAFF SERGEANT MARVIN `REX' YOUNG POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1425) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 4551 East 52nd Street in Odessa, Texas, as 
the ``Staff Sergeant Marvin `Rex' Young Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
