# DOLPH BRISCOE, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                DOLPH BRISCOE, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2688) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 103 South Getty Street in Uvalde, Texas, as 
the ``Dolph S. Briscoe, Jr. Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
