# SENATE RESOLUTION 317--TO CONSTITUTE THE MINORITY PARTY'S MEMBERSHIP ON THE COMMITTEE ON VETERANS' AFFAIRS FOR THE REMAINDER OF THE 110TH CONGRESS OR UNTIL THEIR SUCCESSORS ARE CHOSEN

**Measures:** S.Res. 317

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 317--TO CONSTITUTE THE MINORITY PARTY'S MEMBERSHIP ON 
   THE COMMITTEE ON VETERANS' AFFAIRS FOR THE REMAINDER OF THE 110TH 
             CONGRESS OR UNTIL THEIR SUCCESSORS ARE CHOSEN

  Mr. McCONNELL submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:
       Resolved, That the following shall constitute the minority 
     party's appointments to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs 
     for the remainder of the 110th Congress or until their 
     successors are chosen: Mr. Burr, Mr. Specter, Mr. Craig, Mr. 
     Isakson, Mr. Graham, Mrs. Hutchison, Mr. Ensign.
```
