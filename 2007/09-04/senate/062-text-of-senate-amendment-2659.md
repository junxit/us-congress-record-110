# Text of Senate Amendment 2659

**Speaking:** Obama, Barack (O000167) [D-IL]

```
  SA 2659. Mr. OBAMA submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 2642, making appropriations for military 
construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2008, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 50, between lines 17 and 18, insert the following:
       Sec. 408.  None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act may be used to enter into a contract in 
     an amount greater than $5,000,000 or to award a grant in 
     excess of such amount unless the agency awarding the contract 
     or grant includes information on its Internet website 
     regarding whether the contract or grant recipient has been 
     the subject of any civil, criminal, or administrative 
     proceedings initiated or concluded by the Federal Government 
     or any State government during the most recent 5-year period.
                                 ______
```
