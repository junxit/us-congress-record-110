# MEASURES DISCHARGED AND PASSED--S. 171, H.R. 49, H.R. 335, H.R. 521, H.R. 433, H.R. 514, AND H.R. 577

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 49, S. 171, H.R. 335, H.R. 433, H.R. 514, H.R. 521, H.R. 577

```
 MEASURES DISCHARGED AND PASSED--S. 171, H.R. 49, H.R. 335, H.R. 521, 
                    H.R. 433, H.R. 514, AND H.R. 577

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that it be in order 
to discharge from the Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs 
Committee the following postal-naming bills and the Senate proceed en 
bloc to their consideration: S. 171, H.R. 49, H.R. 335, H.R. 521, H.R. 
433, H.R. 514, and H.R. 577.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bills be 
read three times, passed, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, en bloc; that the consideration of these items appear separately 
in the Record; and that any statements relating to the measures be 
printed in the Record, without intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
