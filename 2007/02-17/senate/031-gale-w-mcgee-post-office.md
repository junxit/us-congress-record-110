# GALE W. McGEE POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 335

```
                       GALE W. McGEE POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 335) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 152 North 5th Street in Laramie, Wyoming, as 
the ``Gale W. McGee Post Office'' was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
