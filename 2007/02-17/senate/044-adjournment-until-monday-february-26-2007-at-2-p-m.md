# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, FEBRUARY 26, 2007 AT 2 P.M.

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 67

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, FEBRUARY 26, 2007 AT 2 P.M.

  Mr. REID. If there is no further business to come before the Senate 
today, and the Republican leader has no further remarks, I now ask 
unanimous consent the Senate stand adjourned under the provisions of H. 
Con. Res. 67.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 3:27 p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, February 26, 2007, at 2 p.m.
```
