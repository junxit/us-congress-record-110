# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 614, S. 641

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 614

  At the request of Mr. Schumer, the name of the Senator from Michigan 
(Ms. Stabenow) was added as a cosponsor of S. 614, a bill to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code to double the child tax credit for the first 
year, to expand the credit dependent care services, to provide relief 
from the alternative minimum tax, and for other purposes.


                                 S. 641

  At the request of Mr. Gregg, the names of the Senator from Minnesota 
(Mr. Coleman), the Senator from Mississippi (Mr. Lott), the Senator 
from Georgia (Mr. Chambliss) and the Senator from South Dakota (Mr. 
Thune) were added as cosponsors of S. 641, a bill to express the sense 
of Congress that no funds should be cut off or reduced for American 
troops in the field which would result in undermining their safety or 
their ability to complete their assigned missions.

                          ____________________
```
