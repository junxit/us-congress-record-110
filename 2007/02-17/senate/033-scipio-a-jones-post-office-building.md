# SCIPIO A. JONES POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 433

```
                  SCIPIO A. JONES POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 433) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1700 Main Street in Little Rock, Arkansas, as 
the ``Scipio A. Jones Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
