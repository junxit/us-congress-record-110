# LANE EVANS POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 521

```
                    LANE EVANS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 521) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2633 11th Street in Rock Island, Illinois, as 
the ``Lane Evans Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
