# SERGEANT HENRY YBARRA III POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 577

```
             SERGEANT HENRY YBARRA III POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 577) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 3903 South Congress Avenue in Austin, Texas, 
as the ``Sergeant Henry Ybarra III Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and
```
