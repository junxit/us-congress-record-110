# GERALD R. FORD, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                GERALD R. FORD, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 49) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1300 North Frontage Road West in Vail, 
Colorado, as the ``Gerald R. Ford, Jr. Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
