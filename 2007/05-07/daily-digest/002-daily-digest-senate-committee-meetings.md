APPROPRIATIONS: NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human 
Services, Education, and Related Agencies concluded a hearing to 
examine proposed budget estimates for fiscal year 2008 for the National 
Institutes of Health: Frontiers of Science, after receiving testimony 
from Jeremy Mark Berg, Director, National Institute of General Medicine 
Sciences, Francis S. Collins, Director, National Human Genome Research 
Institute, Donald A.B. Lindberg, Director, National Library of 
Medicine, and Roderic I. Pettigrew, Director, National Institute of 
Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering, all of the National Institutes 
of Health, Department of Health and Human Services.
