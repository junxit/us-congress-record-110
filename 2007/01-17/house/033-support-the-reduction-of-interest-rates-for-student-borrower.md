# SUPPORT THE REDUCTION OF INTEREST RATES FOR STUDENT BORROWERS

**Speaking:** Green, Al (G000553) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 5

```
     SUPPORT THE REDUCTION OF INTEREST RATES FOR STUDENT BORROWERS

  (Mr. AL GREEN of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. AL GREEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, the foundation of our Nation is 
education. Generally speaking, those with better educations earn more, 
are healthier and live longer. The road to a better society is paved 
with education.
  I want more of our people to have the opportunity to travel the road 
to a better society. This is why I will support H.R. 5. It cuts 
interest rates on subsidized student loans in half from 6.8 percent to 
3.4 percent. It saves the typical borrower $4,400. It provides more 
opportunities for more Americans to have more opportunities.
  I will support H.R. 5.
```
