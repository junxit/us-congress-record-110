# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 18, 2007

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 18, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9 a.m. on 
Thursday, January 18; that on Thursday, following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and there then be a period of morning 
business with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the first hour under the control of the majority leader or 
his designee and the second hour under the control of the Republican 
leader or his designee.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
