# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Bennett, Robert F. (B000382) [R-UT]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 
60 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, 
with the first half of the time under the control of the Republicans 
and the second half of the time under the control of the majority.
  The Senator from Utah is recognized.
  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I be allowed 
to speak for up to 15 minutes.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
