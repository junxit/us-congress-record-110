# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 2, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1082

```
                   ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 2, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m., 
Wednesday, May 2; that on Wednesday, following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day; that there then be a period of morning business 
for 60 minutes, with the first half controlled by the majority and the 
final portion under the control of the Republicans; that at the close 
of morning business the Senate then resume consideration of S. 1082, 
and the mandatory quorum call under rule XXII be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
