# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII WITH RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 839

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII 
          WITH RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. WELCH of Vermont from the Committee on Rules (during the Special 
Order of Mr. Manzullo), submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 110-
471) on the resolution (H. Res. 839) waiving a requirement of clause 
6(a) of rule XIII with respect to consideration of certain resolutions 
reported from the Committee on Rules, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
