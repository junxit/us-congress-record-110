# A PROCLAMATION HONORING JOSEPH AGIN FOR WINNING THE DIVISION III STATE GOLF TOURNAMENT

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING JOSEPH AGIN FOR WINNING THE DIVISION III STATE 
                            GOLF TOURNAMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 4, 2007

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Joseph Agin showed hard work and dedication to the sport of 
golf; and
  Whereas, Joseph Agin was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Joseph Agin always displayed sportsmanship on and off of the 
green; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with his friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Joseph Agin on winning 
the Division III State Golf Tournament. We recognize the tremendous 
hard work and sportsmanship he has demonstrated during the 2007 golf 
season.

                          ____________________
```
