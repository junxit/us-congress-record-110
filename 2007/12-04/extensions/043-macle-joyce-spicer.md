# MACLE JOYCE SPICER

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                           MACLE JOYCE SPICER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 4, 2007

  Mr. GRAVES. Madam Speaker, I proudly ask you to join me in 
recognizing Macle Joyce Spicer of Guilford, Missouri.
  Macle will be celebrating her 80th Birthday on November 25th and it 
is my privilege to offer her my warmest regards on achieving this 
important milestone. Macle is a fine citizen of Missouri and the 
Guilford community. It is an honor to represent Macle in the United 
States Congress, and I wish her all the best on this birthday and many 
more in the future.
```
