# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period for morning business of 60 minutes, with the time 
equally divided and controlled between the 2 leaders or their designees 
and with Senators permitted to speak for 10 minutes each, with the 
Republicans controlling the first 30 minutes and the majority 
controlling the final 30 minutes.
  The Senator from Missouri is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
