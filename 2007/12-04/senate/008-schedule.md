# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, the Senate will conduct morning business for 
an hour with Republicans controlling the first half, the majority 
controlling the final portion. Following this period of morning 
business, the Senate will resume debate on the Peru trade bill. The 
limit on debate this morning will be about 90 minutes. Once this time 
is used, the Senate will recess until 2:15 this afternoon, and at that 
time there will be a vote on passage of the Peru trade bill.
```
