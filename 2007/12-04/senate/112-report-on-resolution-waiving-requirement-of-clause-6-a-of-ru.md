# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII WITH RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII 
          WITH RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

Order of Mr. Manzullo), submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 110-
```
