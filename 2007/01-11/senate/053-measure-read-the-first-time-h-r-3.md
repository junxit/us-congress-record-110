# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 3

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 3

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 3 has been received 
from the House and is at the desk.
  I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3) to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     provide for human embryonic stem cell research.

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask for its second reading and object to 
my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read the 
second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
