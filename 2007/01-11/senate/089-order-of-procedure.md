# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

**Measures:** S. 1

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Friday, 
January 12, after the reporting of S. 1, the Senate proceed to the 
consideration en bloc of amendments Nos. 1 and 10; and that the time 
until 9:50 a.m. run concurrently on both amendments, with the time 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees; that at 9:50 a.m., without further intervening action or 
debate, the Senate proceed to a vote on or in relation to amendment No. 
1, to be followed by a vote on or in relation to amendment No. 10; that 
no amendments be in order to either amendment, and that there be 2 
minutes of debate equally divided between the votes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I now ask unanimous consent that when the 
Kerry amendment No. 1 is reported tomorrow, it then be modified with 
the changes at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
