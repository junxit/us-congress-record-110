# THE RECOGNITION OF 25 YEARS OF SERVICE AWARDS FOR EMPLOYEES OF THE OFFICERS OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

```

                            of pennsylvania

                      Tuesday, September 18, 2007

  Mr. BRADY of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate and recognize outstanding employees of the Officers of the 
U.S. House of Representatives (Clerk of the House, Chief Administrative 
Officer, Inspector General, and Sergeant at Arms) who have completed 25 
years or more of service to the U.S. House of Representatives.
  In any organization, the most important resource is its dedicated 
employees, and without these employees, failure is certain. The 
employees we recognize today are acknowledged and commended for their 
hard work, contributions, and support of House Members, their staffs 
and constituents, and the overall operations of the House. These people 
have accomplished a great many things in a wide

[[Page 24675]]
```
