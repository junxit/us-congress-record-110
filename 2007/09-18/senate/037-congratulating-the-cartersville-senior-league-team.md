# CONGRATULATING THE CARTERSVILLE SENIOR LEAGUE TEAM

```
           CONGRATULATING THE CARTERSVILLE SENIOR LEAGUE TEAM

● Mr. ISAKSON. Mr. President, I wish to honor in the Record the 
Senior League team of Cartersville, GA, on their victory in the 2007 
Senior League World Series.
  These fine young men played outstanding baseball through the entire 
tournament, but in the World Series Championship game, they soared and 
played like true professionals. In their final game, Chris Huth pitched 
a complete game one-hitter and Cole Montgomery hit a three-run home run 
to lead their team to a dominating victory over the defending champion 
Falcon, Venezuela.
  These are special young men: Garison Boston, Ben Bridges, Trey 
Dickson, Brad Green, Taylor Greene, Tyler Higgins, Chris Huth, Tyler 
Linn, Levi Mauldin, Colton Montgomery, Cole Payne, Zack Philliber, Hank 
Stewart, and Tyler Williams. The men have brought great pride to their 
State, great pride to their parents, and great pride to the great city 
of Cartersville, GA.
  Their manager Eric Stewart and coaches Jeff Payne and Mark Montgomery 
each deserve strong recognition for guiding these young players to 
victory.
  I am pleased to join Senator Chambliss in acknowledging the great 
achievement of these young men and to extend my deepest congratulations 
to the 2007 Senior League World Series Champions, the Senior League 
team of Cartersville, GA.●

                          ____________________
```
