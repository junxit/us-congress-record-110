# PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK], Isakson, Johnny (I000055) [R-GA], Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.R. 1495

```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Let Mon Lee, 
a fellow from the U.S. Corps of Engineers, be allowed floor privileges 
for the duration of the Senate's consideration of the Water Resources 
Development Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. ISAKSON. I ask unanimous consent that Mike Quiello of my staff be 
granted the privileges of the floor for the duration of the debate on 
WRDA.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. CARDIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Mike Burke, a 
fellow in my office, be accorded the privileges of the floor for the 
duration of H.R. 1495.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
