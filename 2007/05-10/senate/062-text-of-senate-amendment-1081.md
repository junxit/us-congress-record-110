# Text of Senate Amendment 1081

**Speaking:** Vitter, David (V000127) [R-LA]

```
  SA 1081. Mr. VITTER submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 1495, to provide for the conservation and 
development of water and related resources, to authorize the Secretary 
of the Army to construct various projects for improvements to rivers 
and harbors of the United States, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 18, between lines 9 and 10, insert the following:
       (C) Houma navigation canal.--The Secretary shall spend not 
     more than $200,000 to maintain, pursuant to an exclusive 
     partnership agreement with the State of Louisiana, the Houma 
     Navigation Canal at dimensions consistent with the dimensions 
     of the lock as recommended in the reports referred to in 
     subparagraph (A).
                                 ______
```
