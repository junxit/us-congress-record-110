# PRINTING OF PROCEEDINGS HAD DURING RECESS

**Speaking:** Sestak, Joe (S001169) [D-PA]

```
               PRINTING OF PROCEEDINGS HAD DURING RECESS

  Mr. SESTAK. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the proceedings 
had during the recess be printed in the Congressional Record and that 
all Members and former Members who spoke during the recess have the 
privilege of revising and extending their remarks.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
