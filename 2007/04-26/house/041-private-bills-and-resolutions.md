# PRIVATE BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 2072

```
                     PRIVATE BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

        Mr. PATRICK MURPHY of Pennsylvania introduced a bill (H.R. 
     2072) to authorize and request the President to award the 
     Medal of Honor to Richard Gresko, of Newtown, Pennsylvania, 
     for acts of valor in the Republic of Vietnam on March 11 and 
     12, 1970, while serving as a lance corporal in the Marine 
     Corps during the Vietnam War; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Armed Services.
```
