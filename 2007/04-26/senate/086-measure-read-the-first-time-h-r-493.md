# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 493

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 493

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 493

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, H.R. 493 has been received from the House 
and is at the desk and due for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 493) to prohibit discrimination on the basis 
     of genetic information with respect to health insurance and 
     employment.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask for a second reading but object to my 
own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
```
