# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 30, 2007

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 30, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until 2:45 p.m. on Monday, April 30; 
that on Monday, following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; that there then be a period of morning business until 
4:15 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each; that at 4:15, the Senate proceed to the consideration of 
Calendar No. 120, S. 1082, the FDA authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
