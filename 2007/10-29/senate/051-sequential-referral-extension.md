# SEQUENTIAL REFERRAL EXTENSION

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

```
                     SEQUENTIAL REFERRAL EXTENSION

  Ms. CANTWELL. Madam President, as in executive session, I ask 
unanimous consent that the sequential referral of the nomination of 
Julia L. Myers, of Kansas, to be an Assistant Secretary of Homeland 
Security, to the Committee on Judiciary be extended until November 2, 
2007; further that if the nomination is not reported at that time, the 
nomination then be automatically discharged and placed on the Executive 
Calendar.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
