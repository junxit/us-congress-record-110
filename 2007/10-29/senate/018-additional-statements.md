                  ACKNOWLEDGING THE LOUISIANA HONORAIR

● Mr. VITTER. Madam President, today I acknowledge and honor a 
very special group, the Louisiana HonorAir. Louisiana HonorAir is a 
not-for-profit

[[Page 28534]]

organization that flies as many as 200 World War II veterans up to 
Washington, DC, free of charge. On November 3, 2007, a group of 105 
veterans and their guardians will reach Washington on this very special 
program.
  While visiting Washington, DC, the veterans will tour sights, such as 
the Arlington National Cemetery, the Korean Memorial, and the World War 
II Memorial. The program provides many veterans with their only 
opportunity to see the great memorials dedicated to their service.
  Thus, today, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring these great 
Americans and thanking them for their devotion and service to our 
Nation.●
