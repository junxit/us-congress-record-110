# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 31, 2007

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 3963

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 31, 2007

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes it business today, it stand adjourned until 12 noon, 
Wednesday, October 31; that on Wednesday, following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders reserved for their 
use later in the day; that there then be a period of morning business 
for 60 minutes with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each, and the time be equally divided and controlled with the 
majority controlling the first half and the Republicans controlling the 
final half; that following morning business, the Senate resume 
consideration of the motion to proceed to H.R. 3963, the children's 
health insurance legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
