# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3920, TRADE AND GLOBALIZATION ASSISTANCE ACT OF 2007

**Measures:** H.Res. 781, H.R. 3920

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3920, TRADE 
                AND GLOBALIZATION ASSISTANCE ACT OF 2007

  Mr. HASTINGS of Florida (during Special Order of Mr. Shadegg), from 
the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 110-
417) on the resolution (H. Res. 781) providing for consideration of the 
bill (H.R. 3920) to amend the Trade Act of 1974 to reauthorize trade 
adjustment assistance, to extend trade adjustment assistance to service 
workers and firms, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
