# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2262, HARDROCK MINING AND RECLAMATION ACT OF 2007

**Measures:** H.Res. 780, H.R. 2262

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2262, HARDROCK 
                   MINING AND RECLAMATION ACT OF 2007

  Mr. HASTINGS of Florida (during Special Order of Mr. Shadegg), from 
the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 110-
416) on the resolution (H. Res. 780) providing for consideration of the 
bill (H.R. 2262) to modify the requirements applicable to locatable 
minerals on public domain lands, consistent with the principles of 
self-initiation of mining claims, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
