# REREFERRAL OF H.R. 2744, AIRLINE FLIGHT CREW TECHNICAL CORRECTIONS ACT

```
 REREFERRAL OF H.R. 2744, AIRLINE FLIGHT CREW TECHNICAL CORRECTIONS ACT

  Mr. YARMUTH. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committees 
on Education and Labor, House Administration and Oversight and 
Government Reform be discharged from further consideration of the bill 
(H.R. 2744) to amend the Family and Medical Leave Act of 1993 to 
clarify the eligibility requirements with respect to airline flight 
crews, and that the bill be rereferred to the Committee on Education 
and Labor.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Kentucky?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
