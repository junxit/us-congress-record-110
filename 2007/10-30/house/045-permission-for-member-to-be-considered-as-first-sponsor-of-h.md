# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 866

**Speaking:** McKeon, Howard P. "Buck" (M000508) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 866

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 866

  Mr. McKEON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 866, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Norwood of Georgia, for the purposes of 
adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of 
rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
