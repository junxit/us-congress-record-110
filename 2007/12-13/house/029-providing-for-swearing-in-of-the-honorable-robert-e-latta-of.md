# PROVIDING FOR SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE ROBERT E. LATTA, OF OHIO, AS A MEMBER OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Regula, Ralph (R000141) [R-OH]

```
PROVIDING FOR SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE ROBERT E. LATTA, OF OHIO, AS 
                         A MEMBER OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. REGULA. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the gentleman 
from Ohio, the Honorable Robert E. Latta, be permitted to take the oath 
of office today.
  His certificate of election has not arrived, but there is no contest 
and no question has been raised with regard to his election.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
Ohio?
  There was no objection.
```
