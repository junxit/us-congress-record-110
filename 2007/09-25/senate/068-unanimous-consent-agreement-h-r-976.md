# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 976

```
                 UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 976

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Wednesday, 
September 26, when cloture is filed on the motion to concur in the 
House amendments to the Senate amendments to H.R. 976, that it be 
considered to have been filed on Tuesday, and the mandatory quorum be 
waived, notwithstanding rule XXII.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
