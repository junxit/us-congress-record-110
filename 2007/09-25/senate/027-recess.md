# RECESS

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI], Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until 2:15 p.m. today.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:22 p.m., recessed until 
2:15 p.m. and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Carper).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
