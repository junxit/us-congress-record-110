# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2693, POPCORN WORKERS LUNG DISEASE PREVENTION ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 678, H.R. 2693

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2693, POPCORN 
                  WORKERS LUNG DISEASE PREVENTION ACT

  Ms. MATSUI, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-349) on the resolution (H. Res. 678) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2693) to direct the Occupational 
Safety and Health Administration to issue a standard regulating worker 
exposure to diacetyl, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
