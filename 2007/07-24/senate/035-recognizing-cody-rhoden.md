# RECOGNIZING CODY RHODEN

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        RECOGNIZING CODY RHODEN

 ● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I wish to recognize Cody 
Rhoden, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over 
the past several months.
  Cody is a graduate of Sunshine Bible Academy in Miller, SD. Currently 
he is attending Black Hills State University, where he is majoring in 
business administration. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to 
getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Cody for 
all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
