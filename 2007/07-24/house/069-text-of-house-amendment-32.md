# Text of House Amendment 32

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3074

```
H.R. 3074

                      Offered By: Mr. King of Iowa

       Amendment No. 32: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. 410. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for the Association of Community Organizations for 
     Reform Now (ACORN).
```
