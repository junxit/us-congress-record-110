# Text of House Amendment 25

**Speaking:** Pence, Mike (P000587) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3093

```
H.R. 3093

                         Offered By: Mr. Pence

       Amendment No. 25: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following new title:

                TITLE VII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 701. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to enforce the amendments made by subtitle A of title 
     II of Public Law 107-155.
```
