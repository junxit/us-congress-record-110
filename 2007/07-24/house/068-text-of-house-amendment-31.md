# Text of House Amendment 31

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3074

```
H.R. 3074

                      Offered By: Mr. King of Iowa

       Amendment No. 31: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be used to implement the provisions of subchapter IV of 
     chapter 31 of title 40, United States Code (relating to wage 
     rate requirements; commonly known as the Davis-Bacon Act).
```
