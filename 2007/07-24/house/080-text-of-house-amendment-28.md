# Text of House Amendment 28

**Speaking:** Capuano, Michael E. (C001037) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3093

```
H.R. 3093

                        Offered By: Mr. Capuano

       Amendment No. 28: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following:

                TITLE VII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 701. For grants for young witness assistance, as 
     authorized by section 1136 of the Violance Against Women and 
     Department of Justice Reauthorization Act of 2005 (Public Law 
     109-162), and the amount otherwise provided by this Act for 
     ``Department of Justice, General Administration, Salaries and 
     Expenses'' is hereby reduced by, $3,000,000.
```
