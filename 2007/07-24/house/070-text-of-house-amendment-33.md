# Text of House Amendment 33

**Speaking:** McHenry, Patrick T. (M001156) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 3074

```
H.R. 3074

                        Offered By: Mr. McHenry

       Amendment No. 33: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. ___. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to award a grant or contract based on the race, 
     ethnicity, or sex of the applicant or prospective contractor 
     or subcontractor.
```
