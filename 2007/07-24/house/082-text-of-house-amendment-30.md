# Text of House Amendment 30

**Speaking:** Sutton, Betty (S001174) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 3093

```
H.R. 3093

                         Offered By: Ms. Sutton

       Amendment No. 30: Page 85, add the following after line 24:

                TITLE VII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 701. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to implement any free trade agreement that enters 
     into force after the enactment of this Act unless the United 
     States Trade Representative has certified that jobs in the 
     United States will not be lost because of the agreement.
```
