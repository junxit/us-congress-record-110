# Text of House Amendment 29

**Speaking:** Sutton, Betty (S001174) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 3093

```
H.R. 3093

                         Offered By: Ms. Sutton

       Amendment No. 29: Page 85, add the following after line 24:

                TITLE VII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 701. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the United States Trade Representative for any 
     lobbying activities, or any lobbying activities that are 
     coordinated with private interests, for the purpose of 
     influencing Members of Congress or the public to support or 
     oppose a legislative proposal or free trade agreement that is 
     pending before the Congress.
```
