# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Conaway, K. Michael (C001062) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 3161

```
H.R. 3161

                        Offered By: Mr. Conaway

       Amendment No. 1: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following (and make such technical and 
     conforming changes as may be appropriate):

                  TITLE VIII--OTHER GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds appropriated, or otherwise made 
     available, in this Act may be used to carry out any amendment 
     to section 11(e)(6) of the Food Stamp Act of 1977 (7 U.S.C. 
     2020(e)(6)) made by H.R. 2419 as enacted by the 110th 
     Congress.
```
