# Text of House Amendment 21

**Speaking:** Upton, Fred (U000031) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 3161

```
H.R. 3161

                         Offered By: Mr. Upton

       Amendment No. 21: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to purchase light bulbs unless the light bulbs have 
     the ``ENERGY STAR'' or ``Federal Energy Management Program'' 
     designation.
```
