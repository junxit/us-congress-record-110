# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** Gingrey, Phil (G000550) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3161

```
H.R. 3161

                        Offered By: Mr. Gingrey

       Amendment No. 12: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. ___. None of the funds made available in this Act 
     under the heading ``DOMESTIC FOOD PROGRAMS--Food and 
     Nutrition Service--food stamp program'' may be used in 
     contravention of section 213A of the Immigration and 
     Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1183a).
```
