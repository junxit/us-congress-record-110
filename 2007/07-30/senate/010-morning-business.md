# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD]

**Measures:** S. 1899

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until 3 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees.

are printed in today's Record under ``Statements on Introduced Bills
```
