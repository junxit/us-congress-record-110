# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1, H.R. 3093

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:20 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3093. An act making appropriations for the Departments 
     of Commerce and Justice, and Science, and Related Agencies 
     for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2008, and for other 
     purposes.

  The message also announced that the House agrees to the report of the 
committee of conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on 
the amendment of the Senate to the act (H.R. 1) to provide for the 
implementation of the recommendations of the National Commission on 
Terrorist Attacks Upon the United States.

                          ____________________
```
