# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3121, FLOOD INSURANCE REFORM AND MODERNIZATION ACT OF 2007

**Measures:** H.Res. 683, H.R. 3121

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3121, FLOOD 
             INSURANCE REFORM AND MODERNIZATION ACT OF 2007

  Mr. Hastings of Florida, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 110-351) on the resolution (H. Res. 683) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3121) to

[[Page H10938]]

restore the financial solvency of the national flood insurance program 
and to provide for such program to make available multiperil coverage 
for damage resulting from windstorms and floods, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
