# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                              HON. TED POE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 26, 2007

  Mr. POE. Madam Speaker, due to the fact that I have been appointed by 
the President of the United States to be a Congressional Representative 
to the United Nations, I was in New York City and missed recorded votes 
on the House Floor on Monday, September 24, 2007 and Tuesday, September 
25, 2007.
  I ask that the Record reflect that had I been able to vote that day, 
I would have voted ``yes'' on rollcall vote Nos. 894, 895, 896, 897, 
898, 899, 900, 901, 905, and 907. I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall 
vote Nos. 902, 903, 904, and 906.
```
