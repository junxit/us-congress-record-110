# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 27, 2007

```
                ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 27, 2007

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it stand adjourned until 9 a.m. tomorrow morning, 
September 27; that on September 27, following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders reserved for their use 
later in the day; that the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 
1585 as provided under the previous order; that Members have until 
10:30 a.m. to file any germane second-degree amendments.
  I would say, because of the request of a number of Members, I will 
not use any leader time in the morning. We will move immediately to the 
legislation before this body and have the full 2 hours. I will not use 
any leader time in the morning.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
