# AUTHORIZING BUDGET COMMITTEE REPORTING

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 AUTHORIZING BUDGET COMMITTEE REPORTING

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Friday, 
March 16, notwithstanding an adjournment of the Senate, the Senate 
Budget Committee be permitted to report the concurrent budget 
resolution during the hours of 10 a.m. to 12 noon.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
