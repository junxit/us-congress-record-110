# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 
90 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each, with the first 30 minutes under the control of the 
Republicans and the second 30 minutes under the control of the majority 
and the last 30 minutes equally divided between the two leaders or 
their designees.
  The Senator from Georgia is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
