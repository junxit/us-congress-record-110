  Agreed to the Representative Tom Davis (VA) motion to recommit the

with an amendment and the amendment was agreed to.

  Matheson amendment (No. 1 printed in part B of H. Rept. 110-49) that

activity, with the intent of allowing Congress to review and comment on

  Castle amendment (No. 2 printed in part B of H. Rept. 110-49) that
