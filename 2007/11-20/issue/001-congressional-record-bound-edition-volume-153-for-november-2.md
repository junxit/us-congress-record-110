# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 153 for November 20, 2007

```
[[Page 31934]]

                   SENATE--Tuesday, November 20, 2007

             (Legislative day of Friday, November 16, 2007)
  The Senate met at 9:00 and 7 seconds a.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Jim Webb, a Senator 
from the State of Virginia.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                Washington, DC, November 20, 2007.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Jim Webb, a Senator from the State of Virginia, to perform 
     the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. WEBB thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro tempore.

                          ____________________



            RECESS UNTIL 10 A.M., FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 23, 2007

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Friday, November 23, 2007, at 10 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:00 and 37 seconds a.m. recessed until 
Friday, November 23, 2007, at 10 a.m.
```
