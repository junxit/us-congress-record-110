# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 22, 2007

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 22, 2007

  Mr. SANDERS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 
a.m., Thursday, March 22; that on Thursday, following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of 
S. Con. Res. 21.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
