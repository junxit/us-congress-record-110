# COMMUNICATION FROM DISTRICT REPRESENTATIVE OF HON. LEONARD L. BOSWELL, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
COMMUNICATION FROM DISTRICT REPRESENTATIVE OF HON. LEONARD L. BOSWELL, 
                           MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Ted Tran, District Representative of the Honorable 
Leonard L. Boswell, Member of Congress:
                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, March 15, 2007.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I have been served with a subpoena, 
     issued by the District Court for Polk County, Iowa, for 
     testimony in a criminal case.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the precedents and privileges of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                         Ted Tran,
     District Representative.

                          ____________________
```
