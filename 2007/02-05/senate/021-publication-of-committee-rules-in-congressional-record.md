# PUBLICATION OF COMMITTEE RULES IN CONGRESSIONAL RECORD

**Speaking:** Feinstein, Dianne (F000062) [D-CA]

```
         PUBLICATION OF COMMITTEE RULES IN CONGRESSIONAL RECORD

  Mrs. FEINSTEIN. Mr. President, I would like to take this opportunity 
to remind all Senate Committee chairmen that paragraph 2 of the 
Standing Rules of the Senate requires each Senate committee to adopt 
rules to govern its procedures. Under this rule, committee rules may 
not be inconsistent with the Rules of the Senate and must be published 
in the Congressional Record not later than March 1, 2007.
```
