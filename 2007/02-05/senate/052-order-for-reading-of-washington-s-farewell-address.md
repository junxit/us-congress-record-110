# ORDER FOR READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT]

```
           ORDER FOR READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

  Mr. SANDERS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding the resolution of the Senate of January 24, 1901, the 
traditional reading of Washington's Farewell Address take place on 
Monday, February 26, 2007, at 2 p.m.

[[Page S1581]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
