GENOCIDE
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Human Rights and the Law 
concluded a hearing to examine genocide and the rule of law, focusing 
on ongoing efforts against the perpetrators of genocide and other human 
rights violators, after receiving testimony from Sigal P. Mandelker, 
Deputy Assistant Attorney General, Criminal Division, Department of 
Justice; Lieutenant General Romeo A. Dallaire, Parliament of Canada, 
Ottawa, Ontario; Diane F. Orentlicher, American University Washington 
College of Law, Washington, D.C.; and Don Cheadle, Los Angeles, 
California.
