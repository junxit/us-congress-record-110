# PERMISSION TO OFFER AMENDMENTS OUT OF ORDER DURING CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1538, WOUNDED WARRIOR ASSISTANCE ACT OF 2007

**Speaking:** Skelton, Ike (S000465) [D-MO]

**Measures:** H.Res. 274, H.R. 1538

```
  PERMISSION TO OFFER AMENDMENTS OUT OF ORDER DURING CONSIDERATION OF 
           H.R. 1538, WOUNDED WARRIOR ASSISTANCE ACT OF 2007

  Mr. SKELTON. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during 
further consideration of H.R. 1538 in the Committee of the Whole 
pursuant to House Resolution 274, any of the amendments printed in 
House Report 110-78 may be considered at any time.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Missouri?
  There was no objection.
```
