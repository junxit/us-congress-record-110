# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Johnson, Timothy V. (J000285) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1, H.Res. 547, H.R. 980

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. JOHNSON of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, unfortunately this morning I 
was unable to cast my votes on H.R. 980, H. Res. 547, and the Motion to 
Instruct Conferees on H.R. 1 and wish the Record to reflect my 
intentions had I been able to vote.
  Had I been present for rollcall No. 633 on suspending the rules and 
passing H.R. 980, the Public Safety Employer-Employee Cooperation Act, 
I would have voted ``yea.''
  Had I been present for rollcall No. 634 on passing H. Res. 547, the 
rule providing for consideration of the Departments of Labor, Health 
and Human Services, and Education, and Related Agencies Appropriations 
for FY 2008, I would have voted ``nay.''
  Had I been present for rollcall No. 635 on the Motion to Instruct 
Conferees to H.R. 1, the Implementing the 9/11 Commission 
Recommendations Act, I would have voted ``yea.''

                          ____________________
```
