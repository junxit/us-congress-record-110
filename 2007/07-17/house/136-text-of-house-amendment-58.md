# Text of House Amendment 58

**Speaking:** Hensarling, Jeb (H001036) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 3043

```
H.R. 3043

                       Offered By: Mr. Hensarling

       Amendment No. 58: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       None of funds made available in this Act for the Centers 
     for Disease Control and Prevention may be used for the 
     Entertainment Education Program.
```
