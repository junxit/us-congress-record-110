# Text of House Amendment 23

**Speaking:** Musgrave, Marilyn N. (M001152) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 3043

```
H.R. 3043

                       Offered By: Mrs. Musgrave

       Amendment No. 23: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:
```
