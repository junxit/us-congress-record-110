# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** Flake, Jeff (F000444) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 3043

```
H.R. 3043

                         Offered By: Mr. Flake

       Amendment No. 18: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. ___. (a) Limitation on Use of Funds.--None of the 
     funds in this Act shall be available for Missouri State 
     University, Springfield, Missouri, for a college preparatory 
     pilot program .
       (b) Corresponding Reduction of Funds.--The amount otherwise 
     provided by this Act for ``Department of Education--
     Innovation and Improvement'' is hereby reduced by $100,000.
```
