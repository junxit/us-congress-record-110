# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Sanchez, Linda T. (S001156) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1851

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. LINDA T. SANCHEZ

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 17, 2007

  Ms. LINDA T. SANCHEZ of California. Madam Speaker, on Thursday, July 
12, 2007, the House of Representatives voted on H.R. 1851, the Section 
8 Voucher Reform Act of 2007. I was present for the series of Roll Call 
Votes 625 through 629. However my ``AYE'' vote on final passage of H.R. 
1851 (Roll Call Vote No. 629) was not recorded.
  Had the electronic voting tabulator recorded my vote, the record 
would have demonstrated an aye vote on final passage of H.R. 1851 (Roll 
Call Vote No. 629).
```
