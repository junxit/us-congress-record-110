# RECOGNIZING GARY, SOUTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                     RECOGNIZING GARY, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Gary, SD. The 
town of Gary will celebrate the 135th anniversary of its founding this 
year.
  Since its beginning, Gary has been a strong reflection of South 
Dakota's values and traditions. As they celebrate this milestone 
anniversary, I am confident that Gary will continue to thrive and 
succeed for the next 135 years.
  I would like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Gary on 
their anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
