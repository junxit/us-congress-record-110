# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 19, 2007

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 19, 2007

  Mr. KENNEDY. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m., 
Thursday, July 19; that on Thursday, following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired and the time for the two leaders reserved for their use 
later in the day; and the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 
2669.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
