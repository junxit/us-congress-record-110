# PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

```
[[Page S9496]]
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. HARKIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that David Azaria 
and Rachael Creswell of my staff be granted floor privileges for the 
duration of today's session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

Senator Webb's national security detainee, be given floor privileges 
for the duration of this bill.

  Mr. COBURN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that Major 
Thomas Rogers, a Department of Defense fellow in Senator Pete 
Domenici's office, be granted the privilege of the floor for the 
duration of the debate on the fiscal year 2008 Defense authorization 
bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
