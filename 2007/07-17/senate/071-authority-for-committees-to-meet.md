# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


            committee on small business and entrepreneurship

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship be authorized to meet 
during the session of the Senate in order to conduct a hearing entitled 
``Increasing Government Accountability and Ensuring Fairness in Small 
Business Contracting,'' on Wednesday, July 18, 2007, beginning at 2 
p.m. in room 428A of the Russell Senate Office Building.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                       special committee on aging

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Special 
Committee on Aging be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on Wednesday, July 18, 2007 from 10:30 a.m.-12:30 p.m. in 
Dirksen 628 for the purpose of conducting a hearing.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
