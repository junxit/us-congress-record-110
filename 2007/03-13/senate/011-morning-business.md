# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 
minutes, with the time to be equally divided between the leaders or 
their designees, and with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The Senator from Washington is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
