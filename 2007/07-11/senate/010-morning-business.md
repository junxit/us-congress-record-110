# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business until 10:30 a.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the first half of the time under the control of the 
majority and the second half of the time under the control of the 
minority.
  The Senator from Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
