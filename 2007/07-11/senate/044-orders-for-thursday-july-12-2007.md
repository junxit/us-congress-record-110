# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 12, 2007

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 12, 2007

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 
a.m., tomorrow morning, July 12; that on Thursday, following the prayer 
and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved 
for their use later in the day; that there then be a period of morning 
business for 30 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein, with 
the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees; that at the close of morning business, the Senate 
resume consideration of H.R. 1585, the Department of Defense 
authorization bill, and then proceed to the McCain or designee 
amendment, as provided for under a previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
