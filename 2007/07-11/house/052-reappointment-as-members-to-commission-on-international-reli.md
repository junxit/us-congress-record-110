# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION ON INTERNATIONAL RELIGIOUS FREEDOM

```
   REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION ON INTERNATIONAL RELIGIOUS 
                                FREEDOM

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Braley of Iowa). Pursuant to section 
201(b) of the International Religious Freedom Act of 1998 (22 USC 6431 
note), amended by section 681(b) of the Foreign Relations Authorization 
Act, Fiscal Year 2003 (22 USC 2651 note), and the order of the House of 
January 4, 2007, the Chair announces the Speaker's reappointment of the 
following members on the part of the House to the Commission on 
International Religious Freedom:
  Ms. Felice Gaer, Paramus, New Jersey, for a 2-year term ending May 
14, 2009, to succeed herself and upon the recommendation of the 
minority leader:
  Ms. Nina Shea, Washington, D.C., for a 2-year term ending May 14, 
2009, to succeed herself

                          ____________________
```
