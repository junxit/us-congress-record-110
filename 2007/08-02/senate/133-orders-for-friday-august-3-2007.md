# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, AUGUST 3, 2007

**Speaking:** Pryor, Mark L. (P000590) [D-AR]

```
                   ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, AUGUST 3, 2007

  Mr. PRYOR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 
a.m., Friday, August 3; that on Friday, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time of the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day; that the Senate then proceed to executive 
session, as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
