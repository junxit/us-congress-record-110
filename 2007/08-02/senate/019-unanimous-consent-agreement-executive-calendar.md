# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the vote on the judicial nomination of the judge from Oklahoma be 
modified for the vote to occur immediately after the Senate convenes 
tomorrow morning, Friday, under the same conditions provided under the 
previous order.
  I would say this has been cleared with Senator Leahy and Senate 
Specter.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
