``aye'' on Rollcall No. 796; I would have voted ``aye'' on Rollcall No. 
797; I would have voted ``no'' on Rollcall No. 798; I would have voted 
``aye'' on Rollcall No. 799; and I would have voted ``aye'' on Rollcall 
No. 800.
