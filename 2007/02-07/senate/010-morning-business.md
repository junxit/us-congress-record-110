# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period for the transaction of morning business until 2 p.m. 
with the time equally divided between the two leaders or their 
designees, alternating sides when appropriate, with the first 30 
minutes under the control of the minority, the second 30 minutes under 
the control of the majority, during which the Senator from New York, 
Mr. Schumer, and the Senator from Massachusetts, Mr. Kennedy, be 
recognized for 15 minutes each.
  The Senator from New Hampshire.

                          ____________________
```
