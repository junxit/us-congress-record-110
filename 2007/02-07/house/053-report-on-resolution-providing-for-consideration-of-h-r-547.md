# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 547, ADVANCED FUELS INFRASTRUCTURE RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 133, H.R. 547

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 547, ADVANCED 
           FUELS INFRASTRUCTURE RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT ACT

  Ms. SLAUGHTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-9) on the resolution (H. Res. 133) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 547) to facilitate the development of 
markets for alternative fuels and Ultra Low Sulfur Diesel fuel through 
research, development, and demonstration and data collection, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
