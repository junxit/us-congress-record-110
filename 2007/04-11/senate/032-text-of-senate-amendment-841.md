# Text of Senate Amendment 841

**Speaking:** Harkin, Tom (H000206) [D-IA]

```
  SA 841. Mr. HARKIN (for Mr. Lugar) proposed an amendment to the 
resolution S. Res. 76, calling on the United States Government and the 
international community to promptly develop, fund, and implement a 
comprehensive regional strategy in Africa to protect civilians, 
facilitate humanitarian operations, contain and reduce violence, and 
contribute to conditions for sustainable peace in eastern Chad, 
northern Central African Republic, and Darfur, Sudan; as follows:

       Amend the title so as to read: ``Calling on the United 
     States Government and the international community to promptly 
     develop, fund, and implement a comprehensive regional 
     strategy in Africa to protect civilians, facilitate 
     humanitarian operations, contain and reduce violence, and 
     contribute to conditions for sustainable peace in eastern 
     Chad, northern Central African Republic, and Darfur, Sudan.''

                          ____________________
```
