# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 63, IRAQ WAR RESOLUTION

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 63, H.Res. 157

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 63, 
                          IRAQ WAR RESOLUTION

  Ms. SLAUGHTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-12) on the resolution (H. Res. 157) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H. Con. Res. 63) disapproving of the 
decision of the President announced on January 10, 2007, to deploy more 
than 20,000 additional United States combat troops to Iraq, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
