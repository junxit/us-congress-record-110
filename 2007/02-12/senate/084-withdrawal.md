# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
February 12, 2007 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:

       Ellen C. Williams, of Kentucky, to be a Governor of the 
     United States Postal Service for a term expiring December 8, 
     2016. (Reappointment), which was sent to the Senate on 
     January 9, 2007.
```
