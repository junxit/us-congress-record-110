# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. WYDEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Energy and Natural Resources be authorized to hold a hearing during 
the session of the Senate on Monday, February 12, 2007, at 2:30 p.m. in 
room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  The dual purpose of this hearing is to receive recommendations on 
policies and programs to improve the energy efficiency of buildings and 
to expand the role of electric and gas utilities in energy efficiency 
programs.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
