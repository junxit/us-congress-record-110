# TRIBUTE TO JOANNA KURYLO

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JOANNA KURYLO

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOSEPH CROWLEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, February 12, 2007

  Mr. CROWLEY. Madam Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to one of my 
constituents, Joanna Kurylo of Queens, NY. I wish to recognize Ms. 
Kurylo for being awarded the 2007 New York State Dr. Martin Luther 
King, Jr. Humanitarian Youth Award. Joanna is a student at Christ the 
King Regional High School where she has achieved a high level of 
excellence and has already established herself as a community leader. 
While participating in a number

[[Page 3769]]

of extra-curricular school activities, Joanna was honored for her 
notable fundraising efforts which brought aid to the Darfur region and 
helped advance the battle against leprosy. This award is granted to 
five students throughout New York State each year.
  Madam Speaker, I join to wish Ms. Kurylo best wishes and good fortune 
in her future projects.

                          ____________________
```
