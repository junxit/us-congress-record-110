# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 22, 2007

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 22, 2007

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until Monday, 
October 22; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the 2 leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and then there be a period of morning business until 3 p.m., with the 
time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each; 
that at 3 p.m. the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 3043.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
