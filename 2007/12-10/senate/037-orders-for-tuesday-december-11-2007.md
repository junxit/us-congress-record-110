# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 11, 2007

**Speaking:** Salazar, Ken (S001163) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2419

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 11, 2007

  Mr. SALAZAR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m., 
Tuesday, December 11; that on Tuesday, following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in 
the day; that there then be a period of morning business for 60 
minutes, with time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees; and Senators permitted to speak therein for 
up to 10 minutes, with the first half under the control of the 
Republicans and the final half under the control of the majority; that 
at the close of morning business, the Senate then resume consideration 
of H.R. 2419.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
