# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, Lord of history, if a sparrow does not fall without 
Your knowledge, certainly the issues facing our Senate concern You. 
Your word assures us that in everything You are working for the good of 
those who love You. May that confidence guide the Members of this body 
as they seek equitable and just solutions to complicated problems. Give 
wisdom to discern, courage to believe, and determination to do Your 
will.
  Bless, O God, the faithful men and women who manage the machinery of 
the Senate, without whom this legislative body could not function. 
Thank You for their efficient and productive work. Fill this place with 
Your presence.
  We ask in the name of Him who in love gave Himself for us all. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
