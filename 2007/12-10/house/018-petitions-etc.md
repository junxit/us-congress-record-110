# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.Res. 106

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       195. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Eight Former 
     Secretaries of State, relative to a petition opposing H. Res. 
     106; to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
       196. Also, a petition of the City Council of Albany, 
     California, relative to Resolution No. 07-57 calling for the 
     impeachment of President George W. Bush and Vice President 
     Richard B. Cheney; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
