           COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 7, 2007

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Subcommittee 
on Science, Technology, and Innovation, to hold hearings to examine 
carbon sequestration technologies, 2:30 p.m., SR-253.

Appeals for Veterans Claims, 9:30 a.m., SD-562.
