# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Manzullo, Donald A. (M001138) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 3355

```
H.R. 3355

                        Offered By: Mr. Manzullo

       Amendment No. 1: Page 15, line 2, strike ``and''.
```
