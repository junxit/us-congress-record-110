# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period of morning business for 60 minutes, 
with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
time equally divided and controlled between the leaders or their 
designees, with Republicans controlling the first half and the majority 
controlling the final half.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Georgia.

                          ____________________
```
