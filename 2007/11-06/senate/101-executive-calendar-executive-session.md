# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider Calendar No. 371, the 
nomination of Patrick Francis Kennedy, to be Under Secretary of State; 
that the nomination be confirmed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon 
the table, the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action, and the Senate then return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nomination considered and confirmed is as follows:


                          Department of State

       Patrick Francis Kennedy, of Illinois, a Career Member of 
     the Senior Foreign Service, Class of Career Minister, to be 
     an Under Secretary of State (Management).

                          ____________________
```
