**Measures:** H.R. 513, H.R. 744, H.R. 3222

                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                     HON. STEPHANIE HERSETH SANDLIN

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 6, 2007

  Ms. HERSETH SANDLIN. Madam Speaker, due to airline delays, on 
November 5, 2007, I missed the vote on rollcall No. 1034 (the Motion to 
Close Portions of the Conference--H.R. 3222); rollcall No. 1035 (H.R.

military personnel); and rollcall No. 1036 (H.R. 744, recognizing the

voting, I would have voted ``yea'' on all three.
