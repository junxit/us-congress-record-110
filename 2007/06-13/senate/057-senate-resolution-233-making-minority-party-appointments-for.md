# SENATE RESOLUTION 233--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS FOR THE SELECT COMMITTEE ON ETHICS FOR THE 110TH CONGRESS

**Measures:** S.Res. 233

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

   SENATE RESOLUTION 233--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS FOR THE 
           SELECT COMMITTEE ON ETHICS FOR THE 110TH CONGRESS

  Mr. McCONNELL submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 233

       Resolved, That the following be the minority membership on 
     the Select Committee on Ethics for the remainder of the 110th 
     Congress, or until their successors are appointed; Mr. 
     Cornyn, Mr. Roberts, and Mr. Isakson.
```
