# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 14, 2007

**Speaking:** Menendez, Robert (M000639) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 6

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 14, 2007

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 
a.m., Thursday, June 14; that on Thursday, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved 
for their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume 
consideration of H.R. 6, the comprehensive energy legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
