# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Berkley, Shelley (B001231) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2641

```
H.R. 2641

                        Offered By: Ms. Berkley

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of the bill, before the short

     website.
```
