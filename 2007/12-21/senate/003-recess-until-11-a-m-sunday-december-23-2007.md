# RECESS UNTIL 11 A.M., SUNDAY, DECEMBER 23, 2007

```
            RECESS UNTIL 11 A.M., SUNDAY, DECEMBER 23, 2007

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Sunday, December 23, 2007.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:31 a.m. and 10 seconds, recessed until 
Sunday, December 23, 2007, at 11 a.m.
```
