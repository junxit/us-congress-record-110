# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 1011, H.R. 3867

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

       The amendment to be offered by Representative Goodlatte or 
     a designee to H.R. 1011--Virginia Ridge and Valley Act of 
     2007, does not contain any congressional earmarks, limited 
     tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 
     9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of rule XXI.
       The amendment to be offered by Representative Velazquez or 
     a designee to H.R. 3867, the Small Business Contracting 
     Program Improvements Act, does not contain any congressional 
     earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as 
     defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of rule XXI.

                          ____________________
```
