# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 23, 2007

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 23, 2007

  Mr. NELSON of Florida. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 
2 p.m., Monday, April 23; that on Monday, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved 
for their use later in the day; that there then be a period of morning 
business until 2:45 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein, 
with the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees, with the majority controlling the first half and the 
second half controlled by the Republicans; that at 2:45 p.m., the 
Senate resume consideration of S. 761, the America COMPETES Act.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
