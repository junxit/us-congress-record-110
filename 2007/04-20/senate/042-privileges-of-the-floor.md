# PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM], Alexander, Lamar (A000360) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 761

```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Dr. Melanie 
Roberts, who is a fellow in my office; Mr. Kevin Eckerle, a fellow in 
the Commerce Committee; Dr. Steve Leherman, a fellow in Senator Pryor's 
office; and Mr. Craig Robinson, a fellow in Senator Lieberman's office, 
all be granted the privilege of the floor during the pendency of S. 761 
and any votes that occur on this bill.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Jack 
Wells, a fellow on my staff, be granted floor privileges for the 
duration of the debate on S. 761, the America COMPETES Act.

                          ____________________
```
