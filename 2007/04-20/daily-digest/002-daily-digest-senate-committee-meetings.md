# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
APPROPRIATIONS: NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human 
Services, Education, and Related Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine proposed budget estimates for fiscal year 2008 for the National 
Institutes of Health, focusing on the burden of chronic diseases, after 
receiving testimony from Richard J. Hodes, Director, National Institute 
on Aging, Stephen I. Katz, Director, National Institute of Arthritis 
and Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases, Elizabeth G. Nabel, Director, 
National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, and Griffin P. Rodgers, 
Director, National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney 
Diseases, all of the National Institutes of Health, Department of 
Health and Human Services.
```
