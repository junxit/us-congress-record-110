# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until 
10:20 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes and 
the time equally divided between the two leaders or their designees.
  The Senator from Maine is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
