# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 16, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 16, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 12 noon 
tomorrow, February 16; that on Friday, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and that the time until 12:30 p.m. be equally 
divided and controlled by the two leaders; that beginning at 12:30 
p.m., there be a period of morning business, with Senators permitted to 
speak therein, with 30-minute blocks of time in an alternating fashion, 
with the majority controlling the first 30 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
