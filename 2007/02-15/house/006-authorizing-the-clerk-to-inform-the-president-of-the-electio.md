# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO INFORM THE PRESIDENT OF THE ELECTION OF THE CLERK

```
 AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO INFORM THE PRESIDENT OF THE ELECTION OF THE 
                                 CLERK

  Mr. HOYER. Madam Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 
166) and ask for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows

                              H. Res. 166

       Resolved, That the Clerk be instructed to inform the 
     President of the United States that the House of 
     Representatives has elected Lorraine C. Miller, a citizen of 
     the State of Texas, Clerk of the House of Representatives of 
     the One Hundred Tenth Congress.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
