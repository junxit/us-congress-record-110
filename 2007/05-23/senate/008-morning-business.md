# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 
60 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each, the time to be equally divided, with the first half of 
the time under the control of the majority and second half of the time 
under the control of the Republicans.
  The Senator from Rhode Island is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
