# LINO PEREZ, JR. POST OFFICE

```
                      LINO PEREZ, JR. POST OFFICE

Postal Service located at 500 West Eisenhower Street in Rio Grande 
City, Texas, as the ``Lino Perez, Jr. Post Office,'' was ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
