# ATANACIO HARO-MARIN POST OFFICE

```
                    ATANACIO HARO-MARIN POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 625) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 4230 Maine Avenue in Baldwin Park, 
California, as the ``Atanacio Haro-Marin Post Office,'' was ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
