# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that we have a 
period of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Iowa is recognized.
  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to speak longer 
than 10 minutes. I don't intend to speak for more than 25 minutes and 
maybe not that long. I would at least like to have the freedom of going 
beyond 10 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
