# MESSAGES FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 863, S. 1327

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 863. An act to amend title 18, United States Code, with 
     respect to fraud in connection with major disaster or 
     emergency funds.
       S. 1327. An act to create and extend certain temporary 
     district court judgeships.

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 100-696, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Republican Leader, announces the appointment of 
the Senator from Tennessee (Mr. Alexander) as a member of the United 
States Capitol Preservation Commission, vice the Senator from Colorado 
(Mr. Allard).

                          ____________________
```
