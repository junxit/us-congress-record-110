# TRIBUTE TO ELIZABETH SIMONE

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO ELIZABETH SIMONE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 5, 2007

  Mr. GRAVES. Madam Speaker, I proudly ask you to join me in 
recognizing Elizabeth Simone of Gladstone, Missouri. Elizabeth will be 
celebrating her 78th birthday on November 22nd and it is my privilege 
to offer her my warmest regards.
  Elizabeth raised three children and has eight grandchildren, 
including my legislative attorney Dominic Lackey. Elizabeth is a 
talented pianist and now spends her days retired after serving as an 
accomplished secretary for the International Association of Machinists 
and Aerospace Workers.
  Madam Speaker, it is an honor to represent Elizabeth in the United 
States Congress, and I wish her all the best on this birthday and many 
more in the future.

                          ____________________
```
