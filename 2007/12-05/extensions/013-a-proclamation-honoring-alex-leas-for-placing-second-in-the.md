# A PROCLAMATION HONORING ALEX LEAS FOR PLACING SECOND IN THE MANCHESTER UMBRO INTERNATIONAL CUP

**Speaking:** Space, Zachary T. (S001173) [D-OH]

```
A PROCLAMATION HONORING ALEX LEAS FOR PLACING SECOND IN THE MANCHESTER 
                        UMBRO INTERNATIONAL CUP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 5, 2007

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Alex Leas competed in England's largest youth soccer 
tournament; and
  Whereas, Alex Leas showed hard work and dedication to the sport of 
soccer; and
  Whereas, Alex Leas has broadened her abilities and skills in the 
sport of soccer; and
  Whereas, Alex Leas was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Alex Leas always displayed sportsmanship on and off of the 
field; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with her friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Alex Leas on placing 
second in the Manchester Umbro International Cup. We recognize the 
tremendous hard work and sportsmanship she has demonstrated.

                          ____________________
```
