# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 
minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein for 10 minutes each 
and with the time equally divided between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half and the 
majority controlling the second half.
```
