# Text of Senate Amendment 3145

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
  SA 3145. Mr. KYL submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 3222, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2008, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of title VIII, add the following:
       Sec. 8107.  Of the amount appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by title III under the heading ``Procurement, 
     Defense-Wide'', up to $7,000,000 may be available for DISA 
     Information Systems Security for the Insider Threat program.
                                 ______
```
