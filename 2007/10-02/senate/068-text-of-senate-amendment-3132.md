# Text of Senate Amendment 3132

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

```
  SA 3132. Mr. LEVIN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 3222, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2008, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of title VIII, add the following:
       Sec. 8107.  The amount appropriated by title IV under the 
     heading ``Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation, Army'' 
     is hereby increased by $30,215,000, of which--
       (1) up to $6,000,000 may be for Advanced Automotive 
     Technology (PE #0602601A); and
       (2) up to $20,215,000 may be for Combat Vehicle and 
     Automotive Advanced Technology (PE #0603005A), of which--
       (A) up to $14,215,000 may be for the Future Combat Systems; 
     and
       (B) up to $10,000,000 may be the Fuel Efficiency ground 
     vehicle Demonstrator (FED).
                                 ______
```
