     Americans and their immense contributions to the Nation; 
     considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. BIDEN (for himself, Mr. Leahy, Ms. Cantwell, 
             Mrs. Murray, Mr. Nelson of Nebraska, Ms. Mikulski, 
             Mr. Durbin, Mr. Sanders, Mr. Casey, Mr. Lautenberg,

     States by democratically-elected officials of Taiwan; to the 
     Committee on Foreign Relations.
