# Text of Senate Amendment 3143

**Speaking:** Biden, Joseph R., Jr. (B000444) [D-DE]

```
  SA 3143. Mr. BIDEN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 3222, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2008, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of title VIII, add the following:
       Sec. 8107. (a) Additional Amount for RDTE, Defense-Wide, 
     for MARK V Replacement Research.--The amount appropriated by 
     title IV under the heading ``Research, Development, Test, and 
     Evaluation, Defense-Wide'' is hereby increased by up to 
     $8,000,000, with the amount of the increase to be available 
     for Program Element 1160402BB for MARK V replacement research 
     for the pursuit by the Special Operations Command of 
     manufacturing research needed to develop all-composite hulls 
     for ships larger than 100 feet.
       (b) Offset.--The amount appropriated by title III under the 
     heading ``Other Procurement, Army'' is hereby decreased by 
     $8,000,000.
                                 ______
```
