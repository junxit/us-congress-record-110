# Text of Senate Amendment 3144

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
  SA 3144. Mr. KYL (for himself, Mr. Sessions, and Mr. Thune) submitted 
an amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 3222, 
making appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 2008, and for other purposes; which was ordered to 
lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of title VIII, add the following:
       Sec. 8107.  Of the amounts appropriated or other otherwise 
     made available by title IV under the heading ``Research, 
     Development, Test, and Evaluation, Defense-Wide'', up to 
     $10,000,000 may be available for Program Element 0603895C for 
     the Space Test Bed.
                                 ______
```
