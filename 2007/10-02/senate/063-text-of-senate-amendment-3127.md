# Text of Senate Amendment 3127

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
  SA 3127. Mr. BROWN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 3222, making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2008, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of title VIII, add following:
       Sec. 8107.  Of the amount appropriated by title IV under 
     the heading ``Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation, 
     Army'', up to $1,000,000 may be available for Army Missile 
     Defense Systems Integration (PE #0603308A) for the High 
     Altitude Airship Program.
                                 ______
```
