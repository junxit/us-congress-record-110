# Text of Senate Amendment 3138

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
  SA 3138. Mr. BROWN (for Mr. Durbin) proposed an amendment to the 
resolution S. Res. 319, expressing the sense of the Senate regarding 
the United States Transportation Command on its 20th anniversary; as 
follows:

       In the eighth clause of the preamble, strike 
     ``4,000,000,000,000 gallons'' and insert ``4,000,000,000 
     gallons.''
```
