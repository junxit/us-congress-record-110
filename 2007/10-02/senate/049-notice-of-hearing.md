# NOTICE OF HEARING

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      COMMITTEE ON INDIAN AFFAIRS

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet on Thursday, October 4, 2007, at 
9:30 a.m. in room 628 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building to conduct 
an oversight hearing on backlogs at the Department of the Interior: 
Land into Trust Applications; Environmental Impact Statements; Probate; 
and Appraisals and Lease Approvals.
  Those wishing additional information may contact the Indian Affairs 
Committee at 224-2251.
```
