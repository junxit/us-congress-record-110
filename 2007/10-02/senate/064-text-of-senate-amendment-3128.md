# Text of Senate Amendment 3128

**Speaking:** Kohl, Herb (K000305) [D-WI]

```
  SA 3128. Mr. KOHL (for himself and Mr. Kennedy) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 3222, making 
appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2008, and for other purposes; as follows:

       At end of title VIII, add the following:
       Sec. 8107.  Of the amount appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by title IV under the heading ``Research, 
     Development, Test, and Evaluation, Navy'' and available for 
     the Permanent Magnet Motor, up to $2,000,000 may be used for 
     the DDG-51 Class Modernization-Hybrid Propulsion Permanent 
     Magnet Drive System.
```
