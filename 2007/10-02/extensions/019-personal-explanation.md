                        HON. TIMOTHY V. JOHNSON

``aye.''

passing H.R. 2276, the Corporal Christopher E. Esckelson Post Office 
Building Designation, I would have voted ``aye.''
  Had I been present for rollcall No. 926 on suspending the rules and 
passing H.R. 3325, the Corporal Stephen R. Bixler Post Office 
Designation, I would have voted ``aye.''
