# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3246, REGIONAL ECONOMIC AND INFRASTRUCTURE DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 2007

**Measures:** H.Res. 704, H.R. 3246

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3246, REGIONAL 
          ECONOMIC AND INFRASTRUCTURE DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 2007

  Mr. WELCH of Vermont, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 110-361) on the resolution (H. Res. 704) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3246) to amend title 40, 
United States Code, to provide a comprehensive regional approach to 
economic and infrastructure development in the most severely 
economically distressed regions in the Nation, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
