# ROBERT E. COYLE UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

**Measures:** S. 801

```
                ROBERT E. COYLE UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

  The bill (S. 801), to designate a United States courthouse located in 
Fresco, California, as the ``Robert E. Coyle United States 
Courthouse'', was order to be engrossed for a third reading, read the 
third time, and passed; as follows:

                                 S. 801

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. DESIGNATION.

       The United States courthouse bordered by O Street, P 
     Street, Tulare Street, and Capitol Street in Fresno, 
     California, shall be known and designated as the ``Robert E. 
     Coyle United States Courthouse''.

     SEC. 2. REFERENCES.

       Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, 
     or other record of the

[[Page S4317]]

     United States to the United States courthouse referred to in 
     section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the ``Robert 
     E. Coyle United States Courthouse''.

                          ____________________
```
