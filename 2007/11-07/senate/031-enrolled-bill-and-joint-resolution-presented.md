# ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 7, S. 2206

```
              ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, November 7, 2007, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bill and joint resolution:

       S. 2206. An act to provide technical corrections to Public 
     Law 109-116 (2 U.S.C. 213la note) to extend the time period 
     for the Joint Committee on the Library to enter into an 
     agreement to obtain a statue of Rosa Parks, and for other 
     purposes.
       S.J. Res. 7. Joint resolution providing for the 
     reappointment of Roger W. Sant as a citizen regent of the 
     Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.
```
