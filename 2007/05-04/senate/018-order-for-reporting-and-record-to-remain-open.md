# ORDER FOR REPORTING AND RECORD TO REMAIN OPEN

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
             ORDER FOR REPORTING AND RECORD TO REMAIN OPEN

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Friday, May 
4, 2007, notwithstanding a recess or adjournment of the Senate, Senate 
committees may report Legislative and Executive Calendar business and 
that the Record remain open for the submission of statements and the 
introduction of legislation until 1 p.m.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
