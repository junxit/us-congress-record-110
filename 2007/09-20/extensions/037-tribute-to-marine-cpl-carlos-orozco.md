# TRIBUTE TO MARINE CPL CARLOS OROZCO

```
                  TRIBUTE TO MARINE CPL CARLOS OROZCO

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ZOE LOFGREN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 20, 2007

  Ms. ZOE LOFGREN of California. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor 
Marine Cpl. Carlos Gil Orozco, who died on September 10th while out on 
patrol in AI-Anbar province.
  In 1993, Carlos and his family immigrated to San Joe, California from 
Colombia, in search of a better life. Carlos graduated from Willow Glen 
High School, and later decided to join the Marines with the support of 
his family. Carlos was described by friends as a good American, who 
will be sorely missed. More than 300 mourners gathered in downtown San 
Jose this past Monday to attend his memorial service.
  On behalf of this Congress and California's 16th Congressional 
District, I offer Carlos's

[[Page 25137]]

family our deepest condolences as they cope with this painful loss. We 
are so thankful for his dedicated service to our country. Our thoughts 
and prayers are with Carlos's family and friends.

                          ____________________
```
