# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 21, 2007

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 21, 2007
 
   Mr. KERRY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
 Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:15 
 a.m., Friday, September 21; that on Friday, following the prayer and 
 pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
 hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders reserved for their 
 use later in the day; that the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 
 1585, the Department of Defense authorization, as provided for under a 
 previous order.
   The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
 
                           ____________________
```
