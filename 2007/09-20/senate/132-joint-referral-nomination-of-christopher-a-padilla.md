# JOINT REFERRAL--NOMINATION OF CHRISTOPHER A. PADILLA

**Speaking:** Kerry, John F. (K000148) [D-MA]

```
          JOINT REFERRAL--NOMINATION OF CHRISTOPHER A. PADILLA

  Mr. KERRY. Mr. President, on behalf of Senator Reid, as in executive 
session, I ask unanimous consent that PN 861, the nomination of 
Christopher A. Padilla to be Under Secretary of Commerce for 
International Trade, be jointly referred to the Finance Committee and 
the Banking Committee.
```
