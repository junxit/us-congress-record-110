# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, this morning, following any time Senator 
McConnell and I may use, the Senate will conduct a period of morning 
business until 10:30. We need to start at that time. There is so much 
left on the Defense authorization bill. The time in morning business is 
equally divided and controlled between the two sides; the majority will 
control the first portion. Following that, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the Department of Defense authorization bill with 
debate continuing on the Cornyn amendment.
```
