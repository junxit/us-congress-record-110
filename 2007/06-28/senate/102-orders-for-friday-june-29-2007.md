# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 29, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 29, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:45 a.m. 
Friday, June 29. On Friday, following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; that there then be a period for morning business with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
