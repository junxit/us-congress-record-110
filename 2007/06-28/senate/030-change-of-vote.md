# CHANGE OF VOTE

**Speaking:** Lugar, Richard G. (L000504) [R-IN], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                             CHANGE OF VOTE

  Mr. LUGAR. Mr. President, on rollcall vote No. 231, I voted ``nay.'' 
It was my intention to vote ``aye.''
  I ask unanimous consent that I be recorded as an ``aye.'' This would 
not affect the outcome of the vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

[[Page S8675]]

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
