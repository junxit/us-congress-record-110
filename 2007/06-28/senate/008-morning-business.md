# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent we go to a period of 
morning business with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each, and Senator Robert C. Byrd be recognized to speak for double what 
everyone else is allowed to speak, 20 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The President pro tempore is recognized for 20 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The President pro tempore is recognized for 20 
minutes.
```
