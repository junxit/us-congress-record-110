# APPOINTMENT OF HON. C.A. "DUTCH" RUPPERSBERGER AND HON. ELIJAH E.

```
  APPOINTMENT OF HON. C.A. ``DUTCH'' RUPPERSBERGER AND HON. ELIJAH E. 
CUMMINGS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT 
                   RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JULY 10, 2007

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                    June 28, 2007.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable C.A. Dutch Ruppersberger and 
     the Honorable Elijah E. Cummings to act as Speaker pro 
     tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions through 
     July 10, 2007.
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
