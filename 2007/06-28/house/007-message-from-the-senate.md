# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 1612

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Secretary be directed to request the House of Representatives 
to return to the Senate the bill (S. 1612) entitled ``An Act to amend 
the penalty provisions in the International Emergency Economic Powers 
Act, and for other purposes.'', and that upon the compliance of the 
request, the Secretary of the Senate be authorized to make corrections 
in the engrossment of the aforesaid bill.

                          ____________________
```
