# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, JULY 2, 2007

**Speaking:** Pallone, Frank (P000034) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 179

```
            CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, JULY 2, 2007

  Mr. PALLONE. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns today on a motion offered pursuant to this order, it 
adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Monday, July 2, 2007, unless it sooner has 
received a message from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in 
House Concurrent Resolution 179, in which case the House shall stand 
adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New Jersey?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
