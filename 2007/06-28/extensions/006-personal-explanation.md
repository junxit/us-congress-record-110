# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. CAROLYN B. MALONEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 28, 2007

  Mrs. MALONEY of New York. Madam Speaker, on June 25, 2007, I missed 
rollcall votes numbered 549, a Resolution expressing the sense of the 
House of Representatives that a ``Welcome Home Vietnam Veterans Day'' 
should be established and 550, a Resolution to designate the Department 
of Veterans Affairs Medical Center in Asheville, North Carolina, as the 
``Charles George Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center.''
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall votes 
numbered 549 and 550.
```
