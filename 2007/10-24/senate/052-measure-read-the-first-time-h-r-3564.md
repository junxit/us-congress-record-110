# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 3564

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 3564

  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 3564 has been 
received from the House and is at the desk. I ask for its first 
reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title for 
the first time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3564) to amend title 5, United States Code, to 
     authorize appropriations for the Administrative Conference of 
     the United States through fiscal year 2011, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. LAUTENBERG. I ask for its second reading and object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read a
```
