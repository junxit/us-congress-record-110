# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we are going to immediately return to 
executive session to continue the consideration of Judge Southwick to 
be nominated to 1 of our circuit courts. The debate time until 11 
o'clock is equally divided and controlled. The 20 minutes prior to the 
11 a.m. vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination will be 
for the 2 leaders who will be recognized to speak, with the majority 
leader controlling the final 10 minutes. That order is already in 
effect. The consent agreement says if cloture is invoked the Senate 
would go to confirmation following that cloture vote. Following 
disposition of the nomination, there will be 20 minutes of debate, 
equally divided, prior to the vote on the motion to invoke cloture.
```
