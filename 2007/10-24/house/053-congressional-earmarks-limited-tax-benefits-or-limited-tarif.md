# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                     Offered by Mr. John D. Dingell

     Committee on Energy and Commerce, H.R. 3963, the Children's

     or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), 
     or 9(f) of rule XXI.

     limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), or 
     9(f) of rule XXI.

     Health Insurance Program Reauthorization Act of 2007, does 
     not contain any congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, 
     or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), 
     or 9(f) of rule XXI.

               Offered by Mr. George Miller of California
```
