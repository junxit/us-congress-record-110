# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 327, H.R. 1284, H.R. 3233

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Ms. Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly 
enrolled bills of the House of the followings titles, which were 
thereupon signed by the Speaker:

       H.R. 327. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to develop and 
     implement a comprehensive program designed to reduce the 
     incidence of suicide among veterans.
       H.R. 1284. An act to increase, effective as of December 1, 
     2007, the rates of compensation for veterans with service-
     connected disabilities and the rates of dependency and 
     indemnity compensation for the survivors of certain disabled 
     veterans.
       H.R. 3233. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at Highway 49 South in Piney 
     Woods, Mississippi, as the ``Laurence C. and Grace M. Jones 
     Post Office Building.''

                          ____________________
```
