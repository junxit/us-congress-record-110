# COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIRMAN OF COMMITTEE ON WAYS AND MEANS

```
       COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIRMAN OF COMMITTEE ON WAYS AND MEANS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Charles B. Rangel, Chairman, Committee 
on Ways and Means:

                                      Committee on Ways and Means,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, January 17, 2007.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker, I am forwarding to you the Committee's 
     recommendations for certain positions for the 110th Congress.
       First, pursuant to Section 8002 of the Internal Revenue 
     Code of 1986, the Committee designated the following Members 
     to serve on the Joint Committee on Taxation: Mr. Rangel, Mr. 
     Stark, Mr. Levin, Mr. McCrery, Mr. Herger.
       Second, pursuant to Section 161 of the Trade Act of 1974, 
     the Committee recommended the following Members to serve as 
     official advisors for international conference meetings and 
     negotiating sessions on trade agreements: Mr. Rangel, Mr. 
     Levin, Mr. Tanner, Mr. McCrery, Mr. Herger.
       Third, pursuant to House Rule X, Clause 5 (2)(A)(i), the 
     Committee designated the following Members to serve on the 
     Committee on the Budget: Mr. Becerra, Mr. Doggett, Mr. 
     Blumenauer, Mr. Tiberi, Mr. Porter.
           Best regards,
                                                Charles B. Rangel,
     Chairman.

                          ____________________
```
