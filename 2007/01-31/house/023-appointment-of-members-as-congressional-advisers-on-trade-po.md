# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS AS CONGRESSIONAL ADVISERS ON TRADE POLICY AND NEGOTIATIONS

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS AS CONGRESSIONAL ADVISERS ON TRADE POLICY AND 
                              NEGOTIATIONS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 161(a) of the Trade Act 
of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2211), and the order of the House of January 4, 
2007, the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the following 
Members of the House as congressional advisers on trade policy and 
negotiations:
  Mr. Rangel, New York
  Mr. Levin, Michigan
  Mr. Tanner, Tennessee
  Mr. McCrery, Louisiana
  Mr. Herger, California
```
