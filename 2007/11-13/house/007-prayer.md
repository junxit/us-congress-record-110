# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  God of power and mercy, blessed to be citizens of these United States 
of America and called by Your people to serve as their honored 
representatives in the 110th Congress, we pray that by Your mighty arm 
You protect this Nation, keep us from all harm and deliver us from all 
evil. Give us freedom of spirit, that we may live, work, and worship as 
the free children of God. May we enjoy good health of mind and body and 
so address the responsibilities set upon us, so our service may give 
You greater glory now and forever. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
