# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 4156

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                      Offered by Mr. David R. Obey

       H.R 4156, making emergency supplemental appropriations for 
     the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending 
     September 30, 2008, and for other purposes, does not contain 
     any congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of 
     rule XXI.

                   Offered by Mr. John M. Spratt, Jr.

       The emergency designations H.R. 4156, the Orderly and 
     Responsible Iraq Redeployment Appropriations Act, 2008, that 
     warranted a referral to the Committee on the Budget do not 
     contain congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or 
     limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), or 
     9(f) of Rule XXI.

                          ____________________
```
