# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business until the hour of 12:30 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each and 
the time equally divided between the leaders or their designees, with 
the Republicans in control of the first half of the time and the 
majority in control of the second half.
  The Senator from Texas.
```
