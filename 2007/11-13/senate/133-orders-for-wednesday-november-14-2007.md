# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2419

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Wednesday, November 14; that on Wednesday, following the 
prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day; that there then be 
a period of morning business for 60 minutes, with Senators permitted to 
speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally divided 
and controlled, with the majority controlling the first half, and the 
Republicans controlling the final half; that at the close of morning 
business, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 2419, the farm bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
