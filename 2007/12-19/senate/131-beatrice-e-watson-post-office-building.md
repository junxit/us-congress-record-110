# BEATRICE E. WATSON POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3569

```
                BEATRICE E. WATSON POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3569) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 16731 Santa Ana Avenue in Fontana, 
California, as the ``Beatrice E. Watson Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
