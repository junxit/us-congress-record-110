# ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

```
             ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, December 19, 
2007, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled bills and joint resolution:

       S. 597. An act to amend title 39, United States Code, to 
     extend the authority of the United States Postal Service to 
     issue a semipostal to raise funds for breast cancer research.
       S. 2174. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 175 South Monroe Street in 
     Tiffin, Ohio, as the ``Paul E. Gillmor Post Office 
     Building''.
       S. 2484. An act to rename the National Institute of Child 
     Health and Human Development as the Eunice Kennedy Shriver 
     National Institute of Child Health and Human Development.
       S.J. Res. 13. Joint resolution granting the consent of 
     Congress to the International Emergency Management Assistance 
     Memorandum of Understanding.

                          ____________________
```
