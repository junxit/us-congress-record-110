# MARINE CORPS CORPORAL STEVEN P. GILL POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
       MARINE CORPS CORPORAL STEVEN P. GILL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3974) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 797 Sam Bass Road in Round Rock, Texas, as 
the ``Marine Corps Corporal Steven P. Gill Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
