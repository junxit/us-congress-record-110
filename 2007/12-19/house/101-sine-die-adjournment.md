# SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Altmire, Jason (A000362) [D-PA]

**Measures:** S. 61

```
                          SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. ALTMIRE. Madam Speaker, pursuant to Senate Concurrent Resolution 
61, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. In accordance with Senate Concurrent 
Resolution 61, 110th Congress, the Chair declares the House first 
session of the 110th Congress adjourned sine die.
  Thereupon (at 7 o'clock and 36 minutes p.m.), pursuant to Senate 
Concurrent Resolution 61, the House adjourned.

                          ____________________
```
