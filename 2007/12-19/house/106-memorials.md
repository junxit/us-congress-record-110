# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Res. 176, H.Res. 207

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       223. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Michigan, relative to House 
     Resolution No. 176 memorializing the Congress of the United 
     States to Repeal Title II of the REAL ID Act of 2005 and to 
     support a return to a negotiated rulemaking process with the 
     states; jointly to the Committees on the Judiciary and 
     Oversight and Government Reform.
       224. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to House Resolution No. 207 
     memorializing the Congress of the United States to enact 
     federal legislation designed to prevent elder abuse; jointly 
     to the Committees on Ways and Means, the Judiciary, Energy 
     and Commerce, and Education and Labor.
```
