# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1312

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1312

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1312

  Mr. REID. I understand that S. 1312 is at the desk and due for a 
second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the bill by 
title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1312) to amend the National Labor Relations Act 
     to ensure the right of employees to a secret-ballot election 
     conducted by the National Labor Relations Board.

  Mr. REID. I object to any further proceedings with respect to the 
bill.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard. The bill will 
be placed on the calendar.
```
