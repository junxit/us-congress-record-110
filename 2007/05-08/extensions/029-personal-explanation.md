                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TIM MURPHY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 8, 2007

  Mr. TIM MURPHY of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, on rollcall No. 247, 
to designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located 
at 320 South Lecanto Highway in Lecanto, Florida as the ``Sergeant 
Dennis O. Flannigan Lecanto Post Office Building,'' I was attending a 
memorial service for the students and faculty of the 18th Congressional 
District of Pennsylvania affected by the Virginia Tech shootings.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea.''
