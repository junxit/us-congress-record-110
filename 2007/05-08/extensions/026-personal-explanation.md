# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TIM MURPHY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 8, 2007

  Mr. TIM MURPHY of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, on rollcall No. 246, 
on H.R. 1434, to designate the facility of the United States Postal 
Service located at 896 Pittsburgh Street in Springdale, Pennsylvania, 
as the ``Rachel Carson Post Office Building,'' I was attending a 
memorial service for the students and parents of the 18th Congressional 
District of Pennsylvania affected by the Virginia Tech shootings. I am 
also a cosponsor of H.R. 1434.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea.''

                          ____________________
```
