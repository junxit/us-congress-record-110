Budget Resolution: Senate began consideration of S. Con. Res. 21, 
setting forth the congressional budget for the United States Government 
for fiscal year 2008 and including the appropriate budgetary levels for 
fiscal years 2007 and 2009 through 2012, taking action on the following 
amendments proposed thereto:
  Pages S3308-40
Pending:
  Sessions Amendment No. 466, to exclude the extension of tax relief 
provided in 2001 and 2003 from points of order provided in the 
resolution and other budget points of order.
Pages S3332-36
  Cornyn Amendment No. 477, to provide for a budget point of order 
against legislation that increases income taxes on taxpayers, including 
hard-working middle-income families, entrepreneurs, and college 
students.
Pages S3336-40
  A unanimous-consent-time agreement was reached providing that Senate 
continue consideration of the resolution at 9:15 a.m., on Wednesday, 
March 21, 2007, and that there be 42 hours remaining for debate equally 
divided; that on Wednesday, March 21, 2007, the first amendment be 
offered by a Republican Senator and that the next amendment be one 
offered by the Majority Leader, or his designee; provided further, that 
no roll call votes occur prior to 5 p.m., Wednesday, March 21, 2007, 
and that the first vote in the sequence be the amendment offered by the 
Majority Leader, or his designee.
Page S3452

  United States-China Economic Security Review Commission: The Chair, 
on behalf of the President pro tempore, pursuant to Public Law 106-398, 
as amended by Public Law 108-7, in accordance with the qualifications 
specified under section 1238(b)(3)(E) of Public Law 106-398, and upon 
the recommendation of the Republican Leader, in consultation with the 
Chairmen of the Senate Committee on Armed Services and the Senate 
Committee on Finance, appointed the following individual to the United 
States-China Economic Security Review Commission: Mark Esper of 
Virginia, for a term expiring December 31, 2008.

the NATO Parliamentary Assembly during the spring session, to be held 
in Madeira, Portugal, May 2007: Senator Cardin.
