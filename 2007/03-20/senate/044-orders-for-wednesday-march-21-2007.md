# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 21, 2007

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 21, 2007

  Ms. STABENOW. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it

[[Page 6957]]

stand adjourned until 9:15 a.m., Wednesday, March 21; that on 
Wednesday, following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, and the time 
for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day; that 
the Senate then resume consideration of S. Con. Res. 21, as provided 
for under a previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
