# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 30 
minutes, under the control of the Republican leader or his designee, 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The Senator from Tennessee is recognized.
  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, will the Chair let me know when 6 
minutes has expired?
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Chair will so inform the 
Senator.
```
