# ORDER FOR FIRST READING OF FISA LEGISLATION

```
              ORDER FOR FIRST READING OF FISA LEGISLATION

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
majority leader or his designee introduces FISA legislation on August 
2, they be considered as having received their first reading on the 
legislative day of August 1, 2007.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
