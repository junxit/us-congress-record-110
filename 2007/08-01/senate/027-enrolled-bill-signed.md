# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  At 2:36 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by one of its clerks, announced that the Speaker has signed the 
following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 1. An act to provide for the implementation of the 
     recommendations of the National Commission on Terrorist 
     Attacks Upon the United States.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Byrd).

                          ____________________
```
