# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3159, ENSURING MILITARY READINESS THROUGH STABILITY AND PREDICTABILITY DEPLOYMENT POLICY ACT OF 2007

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3159, ENSURING 
  MILITARY READINESS THROUGH STABILITY AND PREDICTABILITY DEPLOYMENT 
                           POLICY ACT OF 2007

  Ms. SLAUGHTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 110-292) on the resolution (H. Res. 601) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3159) to mandate minimum periods of 
rest and recuperation for units and members of the regular and reserve 
components of the Armed Forces between deployments for Operation Iraqi 
Freedom or Operation Enduring Freedom, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
