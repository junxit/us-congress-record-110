# Text of House Amendment 56

**Speaking:** Boozman, John (B001236) [R-AR]

**Measures:** H.R. 3161

```
H.R. 3161

                        Offered By: Mr. Boozman

       Amendment No. 56: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to implement the National Animal Identification 
     System where the participation by livestock owners in such a 
     system is mandatory.
```
