# Text of House Amendment 15

**Speaking:** Castle, Michael N. (C000243) [R-DE]

**Measures:** H.R. 3222

```
H.R. 3222

                         Offered By: Mr. Castle

       Amendment No. 15: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be obligated or expended by the Department of Defense to 
     award a contract in an amount greater than $5,000,000 to any 
     entity that does not have in place an internal ethics 
     compliance program.
```
