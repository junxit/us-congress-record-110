# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 26, 2007

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 26, 2007

  Mr. CARPER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 
a.m., Friday, October 26; that on Friday, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed to have expired, and the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day; that the Senate then resume 
consideration of Calendar No. 158, S. 294, Amtrak authorization; that 
at 10 a.m., the Senate proceed to a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; that 
during morning business, Senator Dodd be recognized for up to 20 
minutes, Senator Dorgan up to 30 minutes, and Senator Inhofe up to 2 
hours.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
