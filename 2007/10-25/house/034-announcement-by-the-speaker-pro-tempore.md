# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

24, 2007:
  H.R. 995, to amend Public Law 106-348 to extend the authorization for
