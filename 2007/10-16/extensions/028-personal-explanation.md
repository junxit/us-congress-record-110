953; ``nay'' on rollcall No. 954; ``aye'' on rollcall No. 955; ``nay'' 
on rollcall No. 956; ``aye'' on rollcall No. 957; ``aye'' on final 
passage of H.R. 2895, the National Affordable Housing Trust Fund Act, 
rollcall No. 958; ``aye'' on rollcall No. 959; and ``nay'' on final 
passage of H.R. 3056, the Tax Collection Responsibility Act, rollcall 
No. 960.
