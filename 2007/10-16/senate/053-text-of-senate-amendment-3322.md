# Text of Senate Amendment 3322

**Speaking:** Coburn, Tom (C000560) [R-OK]

```
  SA 3322. Mr. COBURN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 3043, making appropriations for the Departments of 
Labor, Health and Human Services, and Education, and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2008, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __. (a)  Notwithstanding any other provision of this 
     Act, no funds appropriated under this Act shall be made 
     available for--
       (1) the Lyndon Baines Johnson Foundation in Austin, Texas, 
     for the Presidential timeline project;
       (2) the ECHO Center in, Burlington, Vermont, for the Lake 
     Champlain Quadracentennial; or
       (3) the Virginia Aquarium and Marine Science Center in 
     Virginia Beach, Virginia, to expand outreach programs.



       (b) Amounts available as a result of the prohibition under 
     subsection (a) shall be transferred to the Secretary of 
     Education to be used to increase funding for special 
     education programs authorized by the Individuals with 
     Disabilities Education Act.
                                 ______
```
