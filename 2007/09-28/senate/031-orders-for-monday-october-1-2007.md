# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 1, 2007

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 1, 2007

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 2 p.m., 
Monday, October 1; that on Monday, following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in 
the day, and there then be a period of morning business until 3 p.m., 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, and 
the time equally divided and controlled between the 2 sides; that at 3 
p.m., the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 1585.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
