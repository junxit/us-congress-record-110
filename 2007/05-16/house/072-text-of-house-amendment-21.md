# Text of House Amendment 21

**Speaking:** Hinojosa, Ruben (H000636) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 1427

```
H.R. 1427

                        Offered By: Mr. Hinojosa

       Amendment No. 21: Page 140, line 3, before the semicolon 
     insert the following: ``; except that the Director may, at 
     the request of a State, waive the requirements of this 
     subparagraph with respect to a geographic area or areas 
     within the State if (i) the travel time or distance involved 
     in providing counseling with respect to such area or areas, 
     as otherwise required under this subparagraph, on an in-
     person basis is excessive or the cost of such travel is 
     prohibitive, and (ii) the State provides alternative forms of 
     counseling for such area or areas, which may include 
     interactive telephone counseling, on-line counseling, 
     interactive video counseling, and interactive home study 
     counseling''.
```
