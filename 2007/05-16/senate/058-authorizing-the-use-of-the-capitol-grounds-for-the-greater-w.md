# AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE CAPITOL GROUNDS FOR THE GREATER WASHINGTON SOAP BOX DERBY

**Speaking:** Cantwell, Maria (C000127) [D-WA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 79

```
 AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE CAPITOL GROUNDS FOR THE GREATER WASHINGTON 
                             SOAP BOX DERBY

  Ms. CANTWELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 79, just 
received from the House and is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 79) authorizing the 
     use of the Capitol grounds for the Greater Washington Soap 
     Box Derby.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Ms. CANTWELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
concurrent resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table, without intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 79) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
