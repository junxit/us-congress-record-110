# NOTICE OF HEARING

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                 COMMITTEE ON RULES AND ADMINISTRATION

  Mrs. FEINSTEIN. Mr. President, I wish to announce that the Committee 
on Rules and Administration will meet on Wednesday, September 26, 2007, 
at 10 a.m., to conduct an executive business meeting to consider on the 
Nomination of Robert C. Tapella of Virginia, to be Public Printer, 
Government Printing Office; and the nominations of Steven T. Walther of 
Nevada, David M. Mason of Virginia, Robert D. Lenhard of Maryland, and 
Hans von Spakovsky of Georgia to be members of the Federal Election 
Commission.
  For further information regarding this hearing, please contact Howard 
Gantman at the Rules and Administration Committee.
```
