# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the previous 
order governing the consideration of the immigration legislation be 
delayed until 2:15 p.m. today and the time between 2:15 p.m. and 3:30

proponents, with the votes occurring beginning at 3:30 p.m., with all 
other provisions of the previous order remaining in effect.

ordered.
```
