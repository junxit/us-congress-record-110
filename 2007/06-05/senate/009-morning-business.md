# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to a period of morning business until 12:30 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak therein, after Senator Enzi completes his 
remarks immediately following mine, for up to 15 minutes each--Senator 
Enzi can speak for whatever time he feels appropriate--that at 12:30 
p.m., the Senate stand in recess until 2:15 p.m.; that upon 
reconvening, the Senate resume consideration of S. 1348, the 
immigration legislation.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
