# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
5, 2007 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

  Henry Bonilla, of Texas, to be Permanent Representative of the United 
States of America to the Organization of American States, with the rank 
of Ambassador, vice John F. Maisto, resigned, which was sent to the 
Senate on March 15, 2007.
```
