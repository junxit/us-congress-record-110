# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** S.Res. 220

```
                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, if there is no further business today, I 
now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand adjourned under the 
provisions of S. Res. 220, as a mark of further respect to the memory 
of our late colleague, Senator Craig Thomas.

Wednesday, June 6, 2007, at 9:30 a.m.
```
