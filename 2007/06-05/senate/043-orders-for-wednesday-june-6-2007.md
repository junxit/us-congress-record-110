# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 6, 2007

```
                   ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 6, 2007

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 
a.m., Wednesday, June 6; that on Wednesday, following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders reserved for 
their use later in the day; that the Senate then resume consideration 
of S. 1348, as provided for under the previous order; further, that the 
mandatory quorum required under rule XXII be waived with respect to the 
cloture motion filed this evening.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
