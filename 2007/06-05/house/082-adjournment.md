# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Burgess, Michael C. (B001248) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 454

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. BURGESS. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to House Resolution 454, the House 
stands adjourned until 10 a.m. today, as a further mark of respect to 
the memory of the late Honorable Craig Thomas.
  Thereupon (at midnight), pursuant to House Resolution 454, the House 
adjourned as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late 
Honorable Craig Thomas until today, Wednesday, June 6, 2007, at 10 a.m.
```
