# COMMUNICATION FROM CONSTITUENT SERVICES REPRESENTATIVE OF HON. MICHAEL R. PENCE, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
COMMUNICATION FROM CONSTITUENT SERVICES REPRESENTATIVE OF HON. MICHAEL 
                      R. PENCE, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from John Shettle, Constituent Services Representative of 
the Honorable Michael R. Pence, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                     Washington, DC, May 21, 2007.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I have received a subpoena for 
     testimony issued by the Superior Court of Madison County, 
     Indiana.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     inconsistent with the precedents and privileges of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                     John Shettle,
     Constituent Services Representative.

                          ____________________
```
