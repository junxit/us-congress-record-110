# COMPREHENSIVE IMMIGRATION REFORM ACT OF 2007--MOTION TO PROCEED

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1348

```
    COMPREHENSIVE IMMIGRATION REFORM ACT OF 2007--MOTION TO PROCEED


                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. REID. I move to proceed to calendar No. 144, S. 1348 and send a 
cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close debate on the motion to 
     proceed to Calendar No. 144, S. 1348, Comprehensive 
     Immigration Reform.
         Barbara Boxer, Harry Reid, Patrick Leahy, Carl Levin, 
           Jack Reed, Dick Durbin, Daniel K. Inouye, B.A. 
           Mikulski, Robert Menendez, Amy Klobuchar, Daniel K. 
           Akaka, Maria Cantwell, Jeff Bingaman, Ken Salazar, 
           Dianne Feinstein, Christopher Dodd, Edward Kennedy.

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the vote on 
the cloture motion occur on Wednesday, May 16, at a time determined by 
the majority leader, following consultation with the Republican leader, 
and that the mandatory quorum required under rule XXII be waived, and I 
now withdraw the motion to proceed.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
