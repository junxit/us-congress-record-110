# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period for the transaction of morning business until 3 p.m., 
with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each and the time 
equally divided between the two leaders or their designees.
  The Senator from Iowa is recognized.
```
