Prosecutors and Defenders Incentive Act, ``nay''; rollcall vote No. 
348, final passage on H.R. 1700--COPS Improvement Act of 2007, ``aye''; 
rollcall vote No. 349, final passage on H.R. 1773--Safe American Roads 
Act, ``aye''.
