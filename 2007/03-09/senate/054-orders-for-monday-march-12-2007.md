# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 12, 2007

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI]

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 12, 2007

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 2:30 
p.m. Monday, March 12; that on Monday following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed to have expired, and the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day; that there then be a period of 
morning business with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
