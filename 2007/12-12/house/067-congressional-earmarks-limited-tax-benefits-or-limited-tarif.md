# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.Res. 12, H.J.Res. 69

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                          Offered by Mr. Obey

  H.J. Res. 69, making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal 
year, 2008, and for other purposes, does not contain any congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined 
in clause 9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of Rule XXI.

                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       202. The SPEAKER presented a petition of House of 
     Representatives of the Republic of the Philippines, relative 
     to House Resolution No. 12 expressing indignation and 
     condemning the American tv series ``Desperate Housewives'' 
     and demanding an apology from the producer; which was 
     referred to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
```
