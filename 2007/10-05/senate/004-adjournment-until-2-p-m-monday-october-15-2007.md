# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 2 P.M., MONDAY, OCTOBER 15, 2007

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 49

```
           ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 2 P.M., MONDAY, OCTOBER 15, 2007

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, pursuant 
to the provisions of S. Con. Res. 49, the Senate stands adjourned until 
Monday, October 15, 2007, at 2 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:30 and 43 seconds a.m., adjourned until 
Monday, October 15, 2007, at 2 p.m.
```
