# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be now a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 
minutes, with the first half of the time under the control of the 
majority and the second half of the time under the control of the 
Republicans.
  The Senator from Illinois is recognized.
```
