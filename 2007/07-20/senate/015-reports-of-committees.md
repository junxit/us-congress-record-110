# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

```
                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Mr. LEAHY, from the Committee on the Judiciary, without 
     amendment and with a preamble:
       S. Res. 236. A resolution supporting the goals and ideals 
     of the National Anthem Project, which has worked to restore 
     America's voice by re-teaching Americans to sing the national 
     anthem.
       S. Res. 248. A resolution honoring the life and 
     achievements of Dame Lois Browne Evans, Bermuda's first 
     female barrister and Attorney General, and the first female 
     Opposition Leader in the British Commonwealth.
       S. Res. 261. A resolution expressing appreciation for the 
     profound public service and educational contributions of 
     Donald Jeffry Herbert, fondly known as ``Mr. Wizard''.
```
