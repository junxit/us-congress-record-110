# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 23, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1642

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 23, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m., Monday, 
July 23; that on Monday, following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in the 
day; that the Senate then proceed to the consideration of S. 1642, with 
the other provisions of the previous order remaining in effect.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
