# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL THURSDAY, JANUARY 3, 2008

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 61

```
              ADJOURNMENT UNTIL THURSDAY, JANUARY 3, 2008

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the provisions of S. Con. 
Res. 61, as amended, the Senate stands adjourned sine die until 
Thursday, January 3, 2008.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10:00:40 a.m., adjourned until Thursday, 
January 3, 2008, at 12 noon.
```
