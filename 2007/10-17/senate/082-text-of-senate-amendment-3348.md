# Text of Senate Amendment 3348

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
  SA 3348. Mr. BROWN (for himself and Mr. Voinovich) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 3043, making 
appropriations for the Departments of Labor, Health and Human Services, 
and Education, and related agencies for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2008, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in title III, insert the 
     following:
       Sec. __.  Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, 
     $2,000,000 shall be available for the Underground Railroad 
     Educational and Cultural Program. Amounts appropriated under 
     title III for administrative expenses shall be reduced on a 
     pro rata basis by $2,000,000.
                                 ______
```
