# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 
minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the time equally divided and controlled between the 2 
leaders or their designees, with the majority controlling the first 
half and the Republicans controlling the final half.
  The Senator from Florida is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
