# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       179. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the City 
     Commission of Belle Glade, Florida, relative to Resolution 
     No. 2613 requesting the Congress of the United States 
     appropriate funds necessary to bring the Herbert Hoover Dike, 
     and surrounding Lake Okeechobee, into compliance with current 
     levee protection safety standards; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
```
