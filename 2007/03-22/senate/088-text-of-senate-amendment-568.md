# Text of Senate Amendment 568

**Speaking:** DeMint, Jim (D000595) [R-SC]

```
  SA 568. Mr. DeMINT submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the concurrent resolution S. Con. Res. 21, setting forth the 
congressional budget for the United States Government for fiscal year 
2008, and including the appropriate budgetary levels for fiscal years 
2007 and 2009 through 2012; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       On page 34, line 9, before the period at the end, insert 
     the following: ``, except that the authority to designate 
     shall not apply to funding for peanut storage costs on a 
     supplemental appropriations bill pursuant to subsection 
     (f)(1) that is designated to supplement funding for ongoing 
     combat operations''.
                                 ______
```
