# Text of Senate Amendment 593

**Speaking:** Burr, Richard (B001135) [R-NC]

```
  SA 593. Mr. BURR submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the concurrent resolution S. Con. Res. 21, setting forth 
congressional budget for the United States Government for fiscal year 
2008 and including the appropriate budgetary levels for fiscal years 
2007 and 2009 through 2012; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       On page 53, line 9, insert ``, except that, in order to 
     promote competition and lower drug prices under part D of 
     title XVIII of such Act, the Secretary of Health and Human 
     Services may not interfere with the negotiations between drug 
     manufacturers and pharmacies and PDP sponsors with respect to 
     drugs for the treatment of diabetes,'' after ``1395w-
     111(i)(1))''.
```
