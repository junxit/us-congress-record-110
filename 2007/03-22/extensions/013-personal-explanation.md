  Ms. EDDIE BERNICE JOHNSON of Texas. Madam Speaker, I was not able to 
attend a number of votes that took place March 21, 2007 on the House

Recovery Act of 2007; ``Yea'' on rollcall 173, On Motion to Suspend the 
Rules and Pass, Hawaiian Homeownership Opportunity Act; ``Yea'' on 
rollcall 174, On Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass, as Amended, 
Joshua Omvig Veterans Suicide Prevention Act; ``Yea'' on rollcall 175, 
On Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass, as Amended, Dr. James Allen 
Veteran Vision Equity Act; ``Yea'' on rollcall 176, On Motion to 
Suspend the Rules and Pass, Veterans' Compensation Cost-of-Living 
Adjustment Act of 2007; ``Yea'' on rollcall 177, On Motion to Suspend 
the Rules and Pass, Judicial Disclosure Responsibility Act; and ``Yea''
