```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed en bloc to consideration of the following calendar items: 
Calendar No. 28, S. Res. 47; Calendar No. 29, S. Res. 49; Calendar No.
```
