# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 13, 2007

**Speaking:** Klobuchar, Amy (K000367) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 6

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 13, 2007

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 9:30 
a.m., Wednesday, June 13; that on Wednesday, following the prayer and 
the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders reserved for their 
use later in the day, and there be a period of morning business for 60 
minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the time equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees, with the first half under the control of 
the majority and the second half under the control of the Republicans; 
that 20 minutes of the majority time be under the control of Senator 
Brown or his designee; that upon the conclusion of morning business, 
the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 6, as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
