# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:46 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that it has passed 
the act (S. 676) to provide that the Executive Director of the Inter-
American Development Bank or the Alternative Executive Director of the 
Inter-American Development Bank may serve on the Board of Directors of 
the Inter-American Foundation, without amendment.
  The message also announced that the House has passed the following 
bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 65. An act to provide for the recognition of the 
     Lumbee Tribe of North Carolina, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 1441. An act to strengthen controls on the export of 
     surplus parts for F-14 fighter aircraft.
       H.R. 2356. An act to amend title 4, United States Code, to 
     encourage the display of the flag of the United States on 
     Father's Day.

                          ____________________
```
