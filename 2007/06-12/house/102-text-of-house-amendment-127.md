# Text of House Amendment 127

**Speaking:** Cantor, Eric (C001046) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                         Offered By: Mr. Cantor

       Amendment No. 127: Page 73, after line 4, insert the 
     following new subsection:
       (c) For the purposes of this section, the term ``other 
     mechanism that is required By: statute'' does not include any 
     provision of this Act that specifies by name or specific 
     physical location the intended recipient of a grant or 
     contract.
```
