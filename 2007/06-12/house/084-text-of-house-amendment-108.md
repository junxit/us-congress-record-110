# Text of House Amendment 108

**Speaking:** Souder, Mark E. (S001143) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                         Offered By: Mr. Souder

       Amendment No. 108: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. 544. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to administer or advertise any policy of the 
     Department of Homeland Security to provide lawful status

[[Page H6333]]

     to aliens unlawfully present in the United States under any 
     provision of law not in effect on the date of the enactment 
     of this Act.
```
