# Text of House Amendment 113

**Speaking:** LaTourette, Steven C. (L000553) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                       Offered By: Mr. LaTourette

       Amendment No. 113: At the end of title V, add the following 
     new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to implement any plan developed under section 
     7209(b)(1) of the Intelligence Reform and Terrorism 
     Prevention Act of 2004 (Public Law 108-458; 8 U.S.C. 1185 
     note) before June 1, 2009.
```
