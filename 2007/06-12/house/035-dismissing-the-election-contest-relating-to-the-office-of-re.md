# DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE TWENTY-FOURTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF FLORIDA

```
       DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF 
REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE TWENTY-FOURTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF FLORIDA

  Mr. BRADY of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent for 
the immediate consideration of House Resolution 461 in the House.
  The Clerk read the title of the resultion.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 461

       Resolved, That the election contest relating to the office 
     of Representative from the Twenty-fourth Congressional 
     District of Florida is dismissed.

   The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
