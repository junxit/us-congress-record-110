# DISMISSING ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO OFFICE OF REPRESENTATIVE FROM 21ST CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF FLORIDA

**Speaking:** Brady, Robert A. (B001227) [D-PA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 459

```
 DISMISSING ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO OFFICE OF REPRESENTATIVE FROM 
                 21ST CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF FLORIDA

  Mr. BRADY of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent for 
the immediate consideration in the House of the resolution (H. Res. 
459) dismissing the election contest relating to the office of 
Representative from the Twenty-first Congressional District of Florida.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 459

       Resolved, That the election contest relating to the office 
     of Representative from the Twenty-first Congressional 
     District of Florida is dismissed.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
