# Text of House Amendment 120

**Speaking:** McHenry, Patrick T. (M001156) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                        Offered By: Mr. McHenry

       Amendment No. 120:   At the end of the bill (before the 
     short title), insert the following:
       Sec. 544. Of the funds appropriated in this Act under the 
     heading ``Federal Emergency Management Agency--state and 
     local programs'' for law enforcement terrorism prevention 
     grants pursuant to section 1014 of the USA PATRIOT ACT (42 
     U.S.C. 3714), not more than $30,000,000 shall be available 
     for Operation Stonegarden.
```
