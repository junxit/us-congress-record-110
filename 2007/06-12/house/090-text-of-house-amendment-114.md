# Text of House Amendment 114

**Speaking:** Kuhl, John R. "Randy", Jr. (K000364) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                    Offered By: Mr. Kuhl of New York

       Amendment No. 114: Page 8, line 5, insert before the period 
     the following: ``: Provided further, That the Comptroller 
     General of the United States and the Secretary of Homeland 
     Security conduct a study that examines the potentially 
     adverse economic impact of the requirement for land and sea 
     travelers of the Western Hemisphere Travel Implementation Act 
     (WHTI) upon businesses in neighboring regions.''.
```
