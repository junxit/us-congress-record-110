# Text of House Amendment 106

**Speaking:** Souder, Mark E. (S001143) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                         Offered By: Mr. Souder

       Amendment No. 106: Page 16, line 20, strike ``Provided, 
     That no aircraft'' and insert ``Provided further, That no 
     aircraft''.

     Enforcement and other Federal, State, and local agencies and 
     the number of such requests that are denied:''.
```
