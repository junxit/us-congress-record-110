# Text of House Amendment 115

**Speaking:** Kuhl, John R. "Randy", Jr. (K000364) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                    Offered By: Mr. Kuhl of New York

       Amendment No. 115: Page 8, line 5, insert before the period 
     the following: ``: Provided further, That the Comptroller 
     General of the United States and the Secretary of Homeland 
     Security conduct a study that examines security at the 
     Northern Border, evaluates the ability of United States 
     Customs and Border Protection to identify and stop all 
     potential threats from crossing the Northern Border, lists 
     all breaches of security and the reason for such breaches 
     since 2005, and contains recommendations to concerning how 
     and what must be done to improve United States Customs and 
     Border Protection and security at the Northern border.''.
```
