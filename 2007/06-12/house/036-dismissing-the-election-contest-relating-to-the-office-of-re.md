# DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE FOURTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF LOUISIANA

```
       DISMISSING THE ELECTION CONTEST RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF 
   REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE FOURTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF LOUISIANA

  Mr. BRADY of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent for 
the immediate consideration of House Resolution 462 in the House.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                               H. Res. 462

       Resolved, That the election contest relating to the office 
     of Representative from the Fourth Congressional District of 
     Louisiana is dismissed.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
