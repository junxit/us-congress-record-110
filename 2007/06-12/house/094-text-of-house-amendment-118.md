# Text of House Amendment 118

**Speaking:** King, Peter T. (K000210) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                    Offered By: Mr. King of New York

       Amendment No. 118: At the end of Title IV insert the 
     following new section:
       Sec. __. The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by reducing the amount made available for ``Federal 
     Emergency Management Agency--Emergency Management Performance 
     Grants'' by $35,000,000, by reducing the amount made 
     available for ``Coast Guard--Research, Development, Test, and 
     Evaluation'' by $5,000,000, and by increasing the amount made 
     available for ``Domestic Nuclear Detection Office--Systems 
     Acquisition'' by $40,000,000 to carry out the mission and 
     responsibilities of the Domestic Nuclear Detection Office 
     authorized in paragraphs (1), (2), (3), and (4)(A) of the 
     second section 1802(a) of the Homeland Security Act of 2002 
     (6 U.S.C. 592(a)).
```
