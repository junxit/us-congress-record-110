# VETERAN'S DAY POEM

**Speaking:** Lofgren, Zoe (L000397) [D-CA]

```
                           VETERAN'S DAY POEM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ZOE LOFGREN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 12, 2007

  Ms. ZOE LOFGREN of California. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor 
our country's veterans with a poem by my constituent, Jack Richards.

             Veteran's Day for Sgt. Joshua P. Morgan, USMC

     I think of your valor
     In being willing to take a stand for liberty
     I am speechless
     In light of your bravery
     I know your type
     Is not charmed, by show-offs, in the ranks
     Nonetheless
     Please, lower your guard for a moment
     And hear a grateful nation's thanks
     It's not the battles you fight, it's signing the line, saying 
           you will.

                          ____________________
```
