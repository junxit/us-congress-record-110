# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Tancredo, Thomas G. (T000458) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                        Offered By: Mr. Tancredo

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. 544. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used in contravention of section 642(a) of the Illegal 
     Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996 
     (8 U.S.C. 1373(a)).
```
