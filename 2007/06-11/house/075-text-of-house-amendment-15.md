# Text of House Amendment 15

**Speaking:** Deal, Nathan (D000168) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                    Offered By: Mr. Deal of Georgia

       Amendment No. 15: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following new section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to destroy or put to pasture any horse or mule 
     belonging to the Unites States that has become unfit for 
     service until the individual trainer or handler of the horse 
     or mule is given the option for the transfer or conveyance of 
     the horse or mule to the trainer's possession.
```
