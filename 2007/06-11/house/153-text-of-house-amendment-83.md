# Text of House Amendment 83

**Speaking:** Hensarling, Jeb (H001036) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                       Offered By: Mr. Hensarling

       Amendment No. 83: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for the alteration or removal of the Burlington 
     Northern Railroad Bridge in Burlington, Iowa.
```
