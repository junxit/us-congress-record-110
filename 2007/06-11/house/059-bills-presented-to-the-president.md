# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Lorraine C. Miller, Clerk of the House reports that on June 6, 2007, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills.

       H.R. 1675. To suspend the requirements of the Department of 
     Housing and Urban Development regarding electronic filing of 
     previous participation certificates and regarding filing of 
     such certificates with respect to certain low-income housing 
     investors.
       H.R. 1676. To reauthorize the program of the Secretary of 
     Housing and Urban Development for loan guarantees for Indian 
     housing.
```
