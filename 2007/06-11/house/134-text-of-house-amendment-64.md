# Text of House Amendment 64

**Speaking:** Hensarling, Jeb (H001036) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                       Offered By: Mr. Hensarling

       Amendment No. 64: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act in 
     title III under the heading ``Federal Emergency Management 
     Agency--State and local programs'' for grants for terrorism 
     prevention activities may be used to support dance classes.
```
