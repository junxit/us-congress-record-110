# Text of House Amendment 92

**Speaking:** Hensarling, Jeb (H001036) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                       Offered By: Mr. Hensarling

       Amendment No. 92: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to send or otherwise pay for the attendance of more 
     than 50 Federal employees at any single conference occurring 
     outside the United States.
```
