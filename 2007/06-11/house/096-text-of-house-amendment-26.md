# Text of House Amendment 26

**Speaking:** Issa, Darrell (I000056) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
H.R. 2638

                          Offered By: Mr. Issa

       Amendment No. 26: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. 544. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to enter into a contract with an entity that employs 
     individuals unless the entity agrees to elect to participate 
     in the basic pilot program described in section 403(a) of the 
     Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act 
     of 1996 (8 U.S.C. 1324a note).
```
