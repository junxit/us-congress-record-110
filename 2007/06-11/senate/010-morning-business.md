# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period for the transaction of morning business until 3:30 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with 
the time equally divided and controlled between the 2 leaders or their 
designees.
  The Senator from North Dakota.

                          ____________________
```
