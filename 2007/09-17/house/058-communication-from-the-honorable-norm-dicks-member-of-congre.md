# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE NORM DICKS, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
    COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE NORM DICKS, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Norm Dicks, Member of Congress:

                                Congress of the United States,

     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the

[[Page H10417]]

     Rules of the House of Representatives, that I have been 
     served with a subpoena, issued in the U.S. District Court for 
     the Southern District of California, for testimony in a 
     criminal case.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     inconsistent with the precedents and privileges of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                       Norm Dicks,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```
