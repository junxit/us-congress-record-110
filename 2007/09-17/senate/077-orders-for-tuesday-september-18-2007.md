# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 18, 2007

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 1124

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 18, 2007

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until tomorrow morning 
at 10 a.m., Tuesday, September 18; that on Tuesday, following the 
prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and there then be a period of 
morning business for 60 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the Republicans controlling the 
first 30 minutes and the majority controlling the final 30 minutes; 
that following morning business, the Senate proceed to H.R. 1124, as 
provided for under a previous order; that on Tuesday, following 
disposition of H.R. 1124, the Senate stand in recess until 2:15 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
