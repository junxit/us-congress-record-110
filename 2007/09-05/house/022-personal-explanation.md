# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** LaHood, Ray (L000552) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 811

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. LaHOOD. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to insert a 
personal explanation at this point with respect to H.R. 811. Although I 
am listed as a cosponsor of the bill, on further reflection, I do not 
support the bill and intend to vote against the bill. Were the bill not 
already on the Union Calendar, I would delete my name from the list of 
cosponsors.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Ms. Solis). Without objection, the 
gentleman's statement will appear in the Record.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
