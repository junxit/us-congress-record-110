# MEASURES DISCHARGED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 17

```
                          MEASURES DISCHARGED

  The following measure was discharged from the Committee on Foreign 
Relations, and referred as indicated:

       S.J. Res. 17. A joint resolution directing the United 
     States to initiate international discussions and take 
     necessary steps with other Nations to negotiate an agreement 
     for managing migratory and transboundary fish stocks in the 
     Arctic Ocean; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
```
