# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will now be in a period for the transaction of morning business 
until approximately 11:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up 
to 10 minutes each, and the time equally divided and controlled between 
the two leaders or their designees, with the Republicans controlling 
the first half of the time and the majority controlling the second half 
of the time, with the Senator from North Dakota, Mr. Dorgan, 
controlling up to 20 minutes of the majority time.
  The Senator from Georgia.

                          ____________________
```
