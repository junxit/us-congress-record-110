# SENATOR TIM JOHNSON

**Speaking:** Isakson, Johnny (I000055) [R-GA]

```
                          SENATOR TIM JOHNSON

  Mr. ISAKSON. Mr. President, I appreciate this opportunity. Before I 
make my speech, I join the two leaders in expressing my joy at the 
return of Senator Johnson. He has suffered a tragic event, but his 
grit, his determination, and I think the prayers of the entire Senate 
have paid off. I am delighted he is returning to this body, with us, at 
2:15 p.m. today.
```
