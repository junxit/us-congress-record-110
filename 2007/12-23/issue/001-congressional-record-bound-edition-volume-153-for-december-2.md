# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 153 for December 23, 2007

```
[[Page 36501]]


                    SENATE--Sunday, December 23, 2007

            (Legislative day of Wednesday, December 19, 2007)
  The Senate met at 11 and 50 seconds a.m., on the expiration of the 
recess, and was called to order by the Honorable Jim Webb, a Senator 
from the State of Virginia.

                          ____________________



          RECESS UNTIL 9:30 A.M., WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 26, 2007

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Wednesday, December 26, 2007.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 58 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Wednesday, December 26, 2007, at 9:30 a.m.
```
