# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 60 
minutes, equally divided, with Senators permitted to speak therein for 
up to 10 minutes each, with the first half of the time under the 
control of the majority and the second half of the time under the 
control of the Republicans.
  The Senator from Maryland is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
