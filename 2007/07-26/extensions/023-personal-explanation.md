# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. KEITH ELLISON

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 26, 2007

  Mr. ELLISON. Madam Speaker, on July 24, 2007, I inadvertently voted 
``aye'' on rollcall No. 692, an amendment to the Transportation and 
Housing and Urban Development Appropriations Bill, H.R. 3074. I 
intended to vote ``no'' on the amendment, which would impose an 
unacceptable cut to Amtrak. I am pleased that my colleagues did not 
support this amendment, and I congratulate the gentleman

[[Page 21074]]

from Massachusetts, Transportation and Housing and Urban Development 
Chairman John Olver who provided such important support for 
transportation and Amtrak.
```
