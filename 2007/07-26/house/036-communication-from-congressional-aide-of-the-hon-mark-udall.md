# COMMUNICATION FROM CONGRESSIONAL AIDE OF THE HON. MARK UDALL, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
COMMUNICATION FROM CONGRESSIONAL AIDE OF THE HON. MARK UDALL, MEMBER OF 
                                CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from John Bristol, Congressional Aide, Office of the 
Honorable Mark Udall, Member of Congress:


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, July 12, 2007.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, that I have been served with a subpoena, 
     issued by the Westminster, Colorado Municipal Court, for 
     testimony in a criminal case.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the precedents and privileges of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                     John Bristol,
     Congressional Aide.

                          ____________________
```
