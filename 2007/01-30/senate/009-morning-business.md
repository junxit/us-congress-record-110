# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 
60 minutes, with each Senator permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the first half of the time under the control of the majority 
and the second half of the time under the control of the minority.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
