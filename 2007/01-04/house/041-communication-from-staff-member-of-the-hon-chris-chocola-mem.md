# COMMUNICATION FROM STAFF MEMBER OF THE HON. CHRIS CHOCOLA, MEMBER OF CONGRESS, AFTER SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

```
 COMMUNICATION FROM STAFF MEMBER OF THE HON. CHRIS CHOCOLA, MEMBER OF 
                  CONGRESS, AFTER SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

                                                December 20, 2006.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to formally notify you, pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the House of Representatives, that I have 
     been served with a grand jury subpoena for testimony issued 
     by the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of 
     California.
       After consulting with the Office of General Counsel, I have 
     determined that compliance with the subpoena is consistent 
     with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
     Rebecca Kuhn.

                          ____________________
```
