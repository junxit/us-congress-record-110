# NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE U.S. SENATE

```
 NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT 
                     PRO TEMPORE OF THE U.S. SENATE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I send a resolution to the desk.
  The VICE PRESIDENT. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 5) notifying the House of 
     Representatives of the election of a President pro tempore.

  The VICE PRESIDENT. Without objection, the resolution is considered 
and agreed to.
  The resolution (S. Res. 5) was agreed to, as follows:

                               S. Res. 5

       Resolved, That the House of Representatives be notified of 
     the election of the Honorable Robert C. Byrd as President of 
     the Senate pro tempore.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I move to reconsider the vote.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I move to lay that motion on the table.
  The motion to lay on the table was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
