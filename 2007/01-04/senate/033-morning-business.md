# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the next 2 hours of morning 
business be controlled as follows: the first 60 minutes under the 
control of the majority, the second 60 minutes under the control of the 
minority, with Senators therein limited to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The assistant majority leader.

                          ____________________
```
