# SENATE RESOLUTION 13--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A SERGEANT AT ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 13--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE 
      ELECTION OF A SERGEANT AT ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

  Mr. REID (for himself and Mr. McConnell) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:
```
