# SENATE RESOLUTION 16--TO MAKE EFFECTIVE APPOINTMENT OF THE SENATE LEGAL COUNSEL

**Measures:** S.Res. 16

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 16--TO MAKE EFFECTIVE APPOINTMENT OF THE SENATE LEGAL 
                                COUNSEL

  Mr. REID (for himself and Mr. McConnell) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 16

       Resolved, That the appointment of Morgan J. Frankel to be 
     Senate Legal Counsel made by the President pro tempore this 
     day is effective as of January 3, 2007, and the term of 
     service of the appointee shall expire at the end of the One 
     Hundred Eleventh Congress.

                          ____________________
```
