# SENATE RESOLUTION 5--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.Res. 5

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 5--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE 
                  ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  Mr. REID (for himself and Mr. McConnell) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 5

       Resolved, That the House of Representatives be notified of 
     the election of the Honorable Robert C. Byrd as President of 
     the Senate pro tempore.

```
