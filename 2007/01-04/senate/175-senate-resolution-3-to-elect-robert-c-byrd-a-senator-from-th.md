# SENATE RESOLUTION 3--TO ELECT ROBERT C. BYRD, A SENATOR FROM THE STATE OF WEST VIRGINIA, TO BE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES

**Measures:** S.Res. 3

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 3--TO ELECT ROBERT C. BYRD, A SENATOR FROM THE STATE 
  OF WEST VIRGINIA, TO BE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE OF THE 
                             UNITED STATES

  Mr. REID (for himself and Mr. McConnell) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 3

       Resolved, That Robert C. Byrd, a Senator from the State of 
     West Virginia, be, and he is hereby, elected President of the 
     Senate pro tempore.
```
