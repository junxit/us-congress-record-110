# SENATE RESOLUTION 9--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 9--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
              THE ELECTION OF THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

  Mr. REID (for himself and Mr. McConnell) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 9

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of the Honorable Nancy Erickson as 
     Secretary of the Senate.
```
