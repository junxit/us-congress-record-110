# NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

```
  NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF THE 
                        SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I send a resolution to the desk.
  The VICE PRESIDENT. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 9) notifying the President of the 
     United States of the election of the Secretary of the Senate.

  The VICE PRESIDENT. Without objection, the resolution is considered 
and agreed to.
  The resolution (S. Res. 9) was agreed to, as follows:

                               S. Res. 9

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of the Honorable Nancy Erickson as 
     Secretary of the Senate.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I move to reconsider the vote.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I move to lay that motion on the table.
  The motion to lay on the table was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
