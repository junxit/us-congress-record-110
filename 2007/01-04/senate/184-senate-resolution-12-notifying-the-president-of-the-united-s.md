# SENATE RESOLUTION 12--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF A SERGEANT AT ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 12

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 12--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
    THE ELECTION OF A SERGEANT AT ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

  Mr. REID (for himself and Mr. McConnell) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 12

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of the Honorable Terrance W. Gainer 
     as Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.
```
