# PERMISSION TO OFFER AMENDMENT NO. 10 AT ANY TIME DURING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3915

**Speaking:** Frank, Barney (F000339) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 825, H.R. 3915

```
    PERMISSION TO OFFER AMENDMENT NO. 10 AT ANY TIME DURING FURTHER 
                       CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3915

  Mr. FRANK of Massachusetts. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that during further consideration of H.R. 3915 in the Committee of the 
Whole, pursuant to House Resolution 825, amendment No. 10 be permitted 
to be offered at any time.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Ms. McCollum of Minnesota). Is there 
objection to the request of the gentleman from Massachusetts?
  There was no objection.
```
