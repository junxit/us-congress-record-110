# PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3915

```
PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING CONSIDERATION OF 
                               H.R. 3915

  Mr. FRANK of Massachusetts. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that, during consideration of H.R. 3915 pursuant to House Resolution 
825, the Chair may reduce to 2 minutes the minimum time for electronic 
voting under clause 6 of rule XVIII and clauses 8 and 9 of rule XX.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Massachusetts?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
