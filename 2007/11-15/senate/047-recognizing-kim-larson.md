# RECOGNIZING KIM LARSON

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         RECOGNIZING KIM LARSON

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I wish to recognize Kim Larson 
for receiving the Supporter of the Year Award from the South Dakota 
Habitat for Humanity. This is a prestigious award that reflects her 
hard work and dedication to eliminating poverty around the world. It is 
also a reflection of the valuable role she has played in giving back to 
her local community.
  Kim Larson with Oahe Habitat for Humanity is Executive Assistant for 
the CEO of BankWest in Pierre where she is instrumental in providing 
servicing on loans and facilitating the documentation for the partner 
families. Always available, Kim is able to keep Oahe Habitat 
representatives and the partner families informed. Kim has proven to be 
an integral part of the Oahe affiliate of Habitat for Humanity.
  It gives me great pleasure to recognize Kim Larson and to 
congratulate her on receiving this well-earned award and wish her 
continued success in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
