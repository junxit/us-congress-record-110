# A PROCLAMATION HONORING HILLARY BROWN FOR PLACING SECOND IN THE MANCHESTER UMBRO INTERNATIONAL CUP

```
    A PROCLAMATION HONORING HILLARY BROWN FOR PLACING SECOND IN THE 
                   MANCHESTER UMBRO INTERNATIONAL CUP

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ZACHARY T. SPACE

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 15, 2007

  Mr. SPACE. Madam Speaker:
  Whereas, Hillary Brown competed in England's largest youth soccer 
tournament; and
  Whereas, Hillary Brown showed hard work and dedication to the sport 
of soccer; and
  Whereas, Hillary Brown has broadened her abilities and skills in the 
sport of soccer; and
  Whereas, Hillary Brown was a supportive team player; and
  Whereas, Hillary Brown always displayed sportsmanship on and off of 
the field; now, therefore, be it
  Resolved, that along with her friends, family, and the residents of 
the 18th Congressional District, I congratulate Hillary Brown on 
placing second in the Manchester Umbro International Cup. We recognize 
the tremendous hard work and sportsmanship she has demonstrated.

                          ____________________
```
