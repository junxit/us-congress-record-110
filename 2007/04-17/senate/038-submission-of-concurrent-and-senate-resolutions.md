            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were

           By Mr. AKAKA (for himself, Mr. Voinovich, Mr.

     Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
           By Mr. ENZI (for himself and Mr. Thomas):

     Women's National Invitation Tournament; considered and agreed 
     to.

             Mr. Kerry, Mr. Obama, and Mr. Cardin):
       S. Res. 152. A resolution honoring the lifetime 
     achievements of Jackie Robinson; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. REID:
       S. Res. 153. A resolution making temporary appointments to 
     the Select Committee on Ethics; considered and agreed to.
