# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 
60 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each, with the first 30 minutes controlled by the Republican 
leader or his designee and the last 30 minutes controlled by the 
majority leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Utah is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
