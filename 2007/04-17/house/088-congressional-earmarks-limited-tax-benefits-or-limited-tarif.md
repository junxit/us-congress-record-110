# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 363, H.R. 1361

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                         Offered By: Mr. Chabot

       The amendments to be offered by Representative Chabot or a 
     designee to H.R. 1361, the RECOVER Act, do not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of 
     rule XXI.

                     Offered By: Mr. Hall of Texas

       The amendment to be offered by Representative Hall of Texas 
     or a designee to H.R. 363, the Sowing the Seeds through 
     Science and Engineering Research Act does not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9(d), 9(e), or 9(f) of 
     rule XXI.

                          ____________________
```
