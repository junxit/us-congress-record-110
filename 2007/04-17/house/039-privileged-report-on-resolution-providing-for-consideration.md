**Measures:** H.Res. 301, H.R. 1257

```
  PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 
          1257, SHAREHOLDER VOTE ON EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION ACT
```
