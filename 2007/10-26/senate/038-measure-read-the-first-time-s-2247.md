# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2247

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2247

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2247

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk due for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2247) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to make permanent the depreciation classification of motor 
     sports entertainment complexes.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask for a second reading, and in order to 
place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
