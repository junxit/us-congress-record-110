# Text of Senate Amendment 3471

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
  SA 3471. Mr. REID (for Mr. Coleman) proposed an amendment to the 
resolution S. Res. 346, expressing heartfelt sympathy for the victims 
of the devastating thunderstorms that caused severe flooding during 
August 2007 in the States of Illinois, Iowa, Minnesota, Ohio, Oklahoma, 
and Wisconsin, and for other purposes; as follows:

       Amend the title to read as follows: ``Expressing heartfelt 
     sympathy for the victims of the devastating thunderstorms 
     that caused severe flooding during August 2007 in the States 
     of Illinois, Iowa, Minnesota, Ohio, Oklahoma, and Wisconsin, 
     and for other purposes.''
```
