# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Salazar, Ken (S001163) [D-CO]

```
[[Page S629]]

                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SALAZAR. Mr. President, the Senate has voted on two amendments 
relating to earmarks that were approved unanimously by a vote of 98 to 
0. Plus, the Senate voted to invoke cloture on the Reid amendment 
regarding travel and corporate jets. I understand there are several 
second-degree amendments that were filed, and we hope to address any 
germane second-degree amendments prior to disposing of the travel 
amendment. Once we dispose of the travel amendment, then we will have a 
cloture vote on the substitute amendment.
```
