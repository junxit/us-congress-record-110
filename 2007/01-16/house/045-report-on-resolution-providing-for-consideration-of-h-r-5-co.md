# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5, COLLEGE STUDENT RELIEF ACT OF 2007

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5, COLLEGE 
                       STUDENT RELIEF ACT OF 2007

Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 110-1) on
```
