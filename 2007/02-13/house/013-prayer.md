# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God, giver of all good gifts, endow this Nation with moral 
integrity. Help us to grow in virtue and in vision, that America may 
truly be a leader in the community of nations. Use us to create Your 
kingdom here on earth, a kingdom of goodness and truth, a kingdom of 
peace and justice, a sign of Your presence dwelling here on earth, 
active in Your people, and a great blessing for the rest of the world.
  We ask this, trusting in Your Holy Name, both now and forever. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
