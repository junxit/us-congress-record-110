# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

```
                       ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

  Mr. CARDIN. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask unanimous consent the Senate stand 
adjourned in accordance with the provisions of S. Res. 79, as a further 
mark of respect to the memory of the late Honorable Charles W. Norwood, 
Jr., a Representative from the State of Georgia.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 5:09 p.m., adjourned until 
Wednesday, February 14, 2007, at 12 noon.
```
