# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 14, 2007

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 14, 2007

  Mr. CARDIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 12 noon 
Wednesday, February 14; that on Wednesday, following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day; that there then be a period of morning 
business for 60 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein for 
up to 10 minutes each, with each side controlling 30 minutes; that at 
the close of morning business, the Senate resume consideration of H.J. 
Res. 20, the continuing funding resolution; that all time during the 
adjournment and morning business count postcloture.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
