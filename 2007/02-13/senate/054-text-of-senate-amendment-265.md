# Text of Senate Amendment 265

**Speaking:** Collins, Susan M. (C001035) [R-ME]

```
  SA 265. Ms. COLLINS submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 259 submitted by Mr. Warner (for himself, Mr. Levin, Ms. 
Collins, Mr. Nelson of Nebraska, Mr. Hagel, Ms. Snowe, Mr. Smith, Mr. 
Biden, and Mr. Salazar) and intended to be proposed to the joint 
resolution H.J. Res. 20, making further continuing appropriations for 
the fiscal year 2007, and for other purposes; which was ordered to line 
on the table; as follows:

       On page 7, between lines 11 and 12, insert the following:
       (23) Congress and the American people will continue to 
     support and protect the members of the United States Armed 
     Forces who are serving or who have served bravely and 
     honorably in Iraq.
```
