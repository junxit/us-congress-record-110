# RECOGNIZING LOWRY, SOUTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                    RECOGNIZING LOWRY, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Lowry, SD. The 
town of Lowry will celebrate the 100th anniversary of its founding this 
year.
  Since its beginning in 1907, Lowry has been a strong reflection of 
South Dakota's values and traditions. As they celebrate this milestone 
anniversary, I am confident that Lowry will continue to thrive and 
succeed for the next 100 years.
  I would like to offer my congratulations to the citizens of Lowry on 
their anniversary and wish them continued prosperity in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
