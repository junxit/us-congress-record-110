# Text of Senate Amendment 1908

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

```
  SA 1908. Mr. CORNYN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 1639, to provide for comprehensive immigration 
reform and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       On page 635, strike line 12 and all that follows through 
     page 636, line 14, and insert the following:
       ``(4) Grounds of inadmissibility.--
       ``(A) In general.--In the determination of an alien's 
     eligibility for a Z-A visa or a Z-A dependent visa, the 
     grounds of inadmissibility under section 601(d)(2) of the 
     Secure Borders, Economic Opportunity and Immigration Reform 
     Act of 2007 shall apply.
       ``(B) Construction.--Nothing in this paragraph may be 
     construed to affect the authority of the Secretary to waive 
     provisions of section 212(a).
                                 ______
```
