# Text of Senate Amendment 1925

**Speaking:** Voinovich, George V. (V000126) [R-OH]

```
  SA 1925. Mr. VOINOVICH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill S. 1639, to provide for comprehensive immigration 
reform and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; 
as follows:

       On page 2, lines 8 and 9, strike ``based on analysis by and 
     in consultation with the Comptroller General'' and insert the 
     following: ``based on analysis by the Comptroller General, 
     and in consultation with the Comptroller General, the 
     Committee on the Judiciary and the Committee on Homeland 
     Security and Governmental Affairs of the Senate, and the 
     Committee on the Judiciary and the Committee on Homeland 
     Security of the House of Representatives''.
                                 ______
```
