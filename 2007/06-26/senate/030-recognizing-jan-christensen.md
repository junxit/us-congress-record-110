# RECOGNIZING JAN CHRISTENSEN

```
                      RECOGNIZING JAN CHRISTENSEN

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Jan Christensen, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she 
has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Jan is a graduate of Mitchell High School in Mitchell, SD. Currently 
she is attending the University of South Dakota, where she is majoring 
in political science. She is a hard worker who has been dedicated to 
getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jan for 
all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
