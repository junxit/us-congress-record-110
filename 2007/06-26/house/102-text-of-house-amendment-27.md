# Text of House Amendment 27

**Speaking:** Flake, Jeff (F000444) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                         Offered By: Mr. Flake

       Amendment No. 27: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                                TITLE IX

                     ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. None of the funds made available in this Act to 
     the Small Business Administration may be used for SEKTDA [SE 
     KY Tourism Development Association] for economic and small 
     business development.
```
