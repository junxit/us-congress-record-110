# Text of House Amendment 29

**Speaking:** Flake, Jeff (F000444) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                         Offered By: Mr. Flake

       Amendment No. 29: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                                TITLE IX

                     ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. None of the funds made available in this Act to 
     the Small Business Administration may be used for the Boston 
     Chinatown Neighborhood Center Workforce Development 
     Initiative.
```
