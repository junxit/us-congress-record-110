# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 25, S. 1612

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed a bill and a concurrent resolution 
of the following titles in which the concurrence of the House is 
requested:

       S. 1612. An act to amend the penalty provisions in the 
     International Emergency Economic Powers Act, and for other 
     purposes.
       S. Con. Res. 25. Concurrent resolution condemning the 
     recent violent actions of the Government of Zimbabwe against 
     peaceful opposition party activists and members of civil 
     society.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Committee will resume its sitting.

                          ____________________
```
