# Text of House Amendment 33

**Speaking:** Goode, Virgil H., Jr. (G000280) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                         Offered By: Mr. Goode

       Amendment No. 33: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                TITLE IX--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. None of the Federal funds made available in title 
     IV or VIII may be used to implement or enforce the Health 
     Care Benefits Expansion Act of 1992 (D.C. Law 9-114; D.C. 
     Official Code, section 32-701 et seq.).
```
