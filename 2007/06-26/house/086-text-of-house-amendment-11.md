# Text of House Amendment 11

**Speaking:** Hulshof, Kenny C. (H000948) [R-MO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                  Offered By: Mr. Hulshof of Missouri

       Amendment No. 11: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                                TITLE IX

                     ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by reducing the amount made available under 
     ``Election Reform Programs'' for election assistance grants 
     and by increasing the amount made available for ``Federal 
     Drug Control Programs, High Intensity Drug Trafficking Areas 
     Programs'' by $8,000,000.
```
