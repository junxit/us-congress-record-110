# Text of House Amendment 23

**Speaking:** Flake, Jeff (F000444) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2829

```
H.R. 2829

                         Offered By: Mr. Flake

       Amendment No. 23: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                                TITLE IX

                     ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. None of the funds made available in this Act to 
     the Small Business Administration may be used for the 
     Youngstown Warren Regional Chamber, Salute to Success, 
     Business Entrepreneurship Incubator.
```
